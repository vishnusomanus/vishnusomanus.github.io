The tour

Five tools that fly in formation

Each one does a single job and hands off cleanly to the next. Everything below is a working piece of the beta, so go ahead and press things.

Loft Editor

A page, a cursor and your words. Type a slash when you need a photo, a button or a product. The spacing and the dark-mode fixes are our problem.

  • Saved sections for your header, sign-off and sponsor slot
  • Plain-text version beside the HTML, edited by hand
  • Paste from anywhere, including a 40-row table, and it holds up
  • The subject-line check runs as you type
