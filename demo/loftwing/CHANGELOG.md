## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-07

### Changed

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added

- Nine HTML pages for Loftwing, a pre-launch email marketing company in Spokane, Washington, with dark glass panels over a dusk sky and original homing pigeon artwork.
- Waitlist flow with validation, a queue position, a referral link, tiers at 3, 10 and 25 invites, duplicate address handling and a spot saved in the browser.
- Home page with a live launch countdown, a campaign editor mockup and a working subject-line tester.
- Five-tab product tour with a preview toggle, automation viewer, segment builder, warm-up planner and analytics chart.
- Founding-member pricing calculator with list size bands, sends per month, monthly or annual billing and a comparison table.
- Migration estimator with a step checklist, hands-on time and a warm-up plan.
- Roadmap with one saved vote per item, sorting and status filters, a filterable build journal, a team page and a contact form with a reference number.
- Launch, cohort and founding dates that roll forward from today, with a `KEEP_DEMO_CURRENT` switch and a `?now=` preview parameter, and full documentation.
