Spokane, Washington

We started with a pigeon club's mailing list

Tova Halvor grew up cleaning her grandfather's racing loft on the South Hill. Later, she ran email for small publishers on tools that called her readers leads. In October 2025, she and Imre Delacroix started building what they wished existed.

The first campaign went out that December to 212 members of a pigeon club. It's now 412 publications and shops, and everything they've told us is on the roadmap or in the journal.
