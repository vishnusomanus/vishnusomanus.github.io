      Waitlist questions

      Anything else? Ask us directly. A person in Spokane writes back.

      When do I actually get in?+
      Your place in line decides your cohort. The first 12,000 places join Cohort 5 on December 15, places up to 17,000 join Cohort 6 on January 12, places up to 18,000 join Cohort 7 on February 9, and everyone else joins on public launch day, March 9.
      Does joining commit me to paying?+
      No. Joining the list costs nothing, and you can leave it at any time. If you decide to subscribe, your founding price is waiting for you.
      How long does the founding price last?+
      For as long as you stay subscribed. Founding pricing closes on Monday, March 8, 2027, the day before public launch.
      What counts as an accepted invite?+
      Someone who joins from your link, or whose address you match to your invite, and who is new to the list. Addresses already on the list don't count, and neither does your own.
      I joined twice with the same address. Do I have two spots?+
      No. One address holds one spot. If you join again, we'll bring up the spot you already have, invites and all.
