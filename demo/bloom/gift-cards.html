Gift card questions

How do I redeem a gift card?

Give the code when you book online or by phone, or show the email at your appointment. You can use it over several visits.

Can I buy a printed card instead?

Yes. Stop by the salon and we'll wrap one in our letterpress envelope while you wait.

Do gift cards expire?

No. Bloom gift cards have no expiration date and no fees, so you can use yours whenever you're ready.
