Pricing by level

Why prices vary for hair & color

Our stylists progress through three levels as their experience grows. Hair and color prices above are for a Stylist; Seniors and Directors charge a little more for their time. See who's who.

Good to know

Patch tests

New color clients need a quick skin test 48 hours before. Stop by any time we're open; it takes five minutes.

Changes & cancellations

Move or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before. Later changes may incur a 50% fee.

Running late?

Call us and we'll do our best. After 15 minutes we may need to shorten or rebook your treatment.
