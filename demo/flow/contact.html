When we’re open

The doors open fifteen minutes before each class and close half an hour after the last one. The phone is answered 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

See the schedule for today’s classes.

By train

The nearest trains stop at San Jose Diridon station. From there the Highway 17 Express bus runs over the hill to downtown Santa Cruz.

By bus

Santa Cruz METRO buses from downtown stop in Seabright, a two-minute walk away. Check METRO for current routes.

Parking

Two short-term spaces outside for pregnancy and restorative classes. Otherwise use the paid parking lot at the harbor.

By bike

Racks in the courtyard for six bikes. Bring a lock; the gate is open during classes.
