    The small print, kept short

    Can I share my class pass?

    Yes, with one other person at the same address. Just book their mat under your name and tell the desk who’s coming.

    What happens if I miss a class?

    Cancel at least two hours before and the class goes back on your pass or plan. Later than that, or a no-show, and it counts as used. We’re human about illness and emergencies.

    Can I pause my membership?

    You can freeze it for up to two months a year, in whole months, for vacations, injury or a new baby. Let us know before the month starts.

    Do workshops count toward passes?

    Workshops and retreats are booked separately. Members get 10% or 15% off, depending on their plan.
