Teachers

Small studio, familiar faces

Four teachers who know the regulars by name, remember your knee and put the kettle on after evening classes.

Founder · Hatha & yin

Morwenna Tregear

“I wanted a studio that felt like a deep breath out.”

Morwenna grew up in Aptos and found yoga in a drafty church hall in Watsonville twenty years ago. She opened Salt & Sage in 2019 in a converted sail loft she’d walked past for years.

Her classes are thoughtful and unhurried, with clear explanations and a soft spot for anyone who thinks they’re “not flexible enough for yoga”.

Teaches
Hatha, Yin
Training
500-hour teacher training; yin yoga training with further study in anatomy for movement teachers
Off the mat
Ocean swimming at Twin Lakes all year round, badly tuned ukulele
See Morwenna’s classes

Vinyasa & strong flow

Jago Maddern

“Work hard, then lie down. That’s the deal.”

Jago came to yoga from surfing and football, looking for something to keep his back happy. He teaches the early-morning and after-work flows that get the loft studio warm.

Expect good music at a low volume, precise cues and a properly long rest at the end. He’s patient with wrists, shoulders and anyone learning to balance on their hands.

Teaches
Vinyasa flow
Training
300-hour vinyasa training; arm balance and inversion teacher course
Off the mat
Cold-water surfing at Pleasure Point, wood-fired pizza
See Jago’s classes

Restorative & pregnancy yoga

Anouk Delahaye

“Rest is a skill. We practice it here on purpose.”

Anouk moved to Santa Cruz from Brittany for a year and stayed for ten. She teaches restorative, yin and pregnancy yoga in the candlelit Garden room.

Her pregnancy classes have become a small community of their own. She keeps a big box of bolsters, a bigger teapot and a list of local midwife-led groups to share.

Teaches
Restorative, Pregnancy yoga, Yin
Training
200-hour hatha training; specialist pregnancy yoga and restorative yoga trainings
Off the mat
Pottery, sourdough, long walks on West Cliff Drive
See Anouk’s classes

Slow flow & beginners

Kerensa Kernick

“Nobody arrives knowing how. That’s why we have a beginners’ course.”

Kerensa was an elementary school teacher before she trained in yoga, and it shows: she’s great at explaining things simply and making a room of strangers feel at ease.

She runs the rolling Beginners’ foundations course and the slow flow classes, including the 45-minute lunchtime sessions that suit people working in town.

Teaches
Slow flow, Beginners’ foundations
Training
200-hour hatha and vinyasa training; chair yoga and accessible yoga training
Off the mat
Community garden plot in Live Oak, crosswords, Castroville artichokes (fried and grilled)
See Kerensa’s classes
