A FEW PARTICULARS

What counts,
and what doesn’t.

What is a task?

A completed action in a matching flow is one task. The trigger and condition check are not tasks. A skipped action does not count.

Why are AI steps separate?

AI actions use both a task and an AI credit. Keeping the allowances separate makes the extra processing easier to plan.

What changes with annual billing?

The plan fee is reduced by 20% and paid for twelve months. Task overage, AI credits and extra editor prices remain the same.

Can we start with one process?

Please do. A small, well-understood process is the best way to check access, responsibilities and what the handoff should look like.
