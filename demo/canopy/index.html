Tent · RV · Cabins · 12 miles north of Marquette

Pitch your tent where the pines meet Lake Superior.

Tamarack Pines is a family-run campground in forty acres of birch and red pine on the big lake: walk-in tent sites on the bluff, RV sites with full hookups, five cabins, half a mile of agate beach and trails from your picnic table.

Meet the cabins

The cabins

Bring a sleeping bag. We’ll bring the roof.

Arvid built the first cabin in 1994 from tamaracks he cleared for the road. There are five now, each with its own porch, picnic table and fire ring, and a short walk to the bathhouse. Trillium Cabin has its own accessible bathroom.

Out the back gate

Five trails, one lake and a lot of agates

Every trail starts in the campground, blazed in its own color. Walk the bog boardwalk before breakfast, climb Juneberry Ridge for the view to Granite Island, or paddle the shoreline when the lake lies down.

At the fire ring

Bog walks, star parties and stories by the fire

Josie, our naturalist, and Teemu, who runs the kayaks, lead something most days of the season. Programs are free for campers unless there’s a boat involved.

Camp store

Firewood and pasties, waiting when you pull in

Pre-order from the camp store and we’ll have it bagged with your name on it at check-in: seasoned firewood, s’mores kits, ice, and pasties baked in Marquette that morning. You pay when you pick up.
