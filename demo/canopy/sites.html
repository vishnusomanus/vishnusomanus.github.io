    The cabins

    Five cabins, each built for a different kind of trip

    Every cabin has beds with mattresses (bring sleeping bags or sheets and pillows), electric heat, lights and outlets, a mini fridge, a porch with a picnic table and a fire ring. Water and showers are at the bathhouse, except in Trillium Cabin.

    C1 · Lake view

    Agate Cabin

    from$95a night

    The closest cabin to the water: one room with a queen bed and a twin bunk, a deck facing north over the lake and two chairs we have to chase people out of at sunset.

    • Sleeps 4
    • Lake view deck
    • No pets
    • 90 yards to the bathhouse

    Chickadee Cabin

    from$89a night

    Painted barn red, with a screened porch for blackfly season and a fenced yard, so the dog can come along. Two dogs at most; there’s a towel hook by the door for after the beach.

    • Sleeps 4
    • Screened porch

    • Fenced yard

    C3 · Two floors

    Osprey Cabin

    from$125a night

    Our biggest cabin stands on cedar posts with an outside stair. Downstairs has a queen and a bunk; the loft has two twins and a window level with the pine tops, looking out to the lake.

    • Sleeps 6
    • Sleeping loft

    • Steep stairs: not for toddlers

    Balsam Cabin

    from$105a night

    Around camp

    What every camper gets

    • Hot showers

      Two heated bathhouses, cleaned twice a day. Showers are free and the hot water doesn’t run out.

    • Laundry

      Two washers and two dryers on Birch Loop, open 7 am to 10 pm. Quarters at the store.

    • Dump station

      By the office, with a rinse hose. Free for campers, $10 for passers-through.

    • Wi-Fi at the office

      On the porch and in the store. Out at the sites, enjoy the quiet: cell coverage is patchy.
