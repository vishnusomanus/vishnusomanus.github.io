      What you need

      • An iPad and stylus

        Any iPad from the last few years works. A cheap stylus is fine to start.

      • A drawing app

        I teach in Procreate and show the Fresco way too. Any layers app will do.

      • Twenty minutes

      Questions people ask

      I’ve never drawn before. Is that OK?

      More than OK. Most of my students start from zero. We build every drawing from simple shapes, so you never need to “just see it”.

      How long do I have access?

      Forever, including every update I make to the course. Watch on your iPad, phone or laptop.

      When does the early-bird price end?

      At the date and time shown on the price card, in Eastern time. After that the course goes back to its usual price automatically.

      What if I don’t enjoy it?

      Email me within 14 days and I’ll refund you in full. No forms and no hard feelings.

      Can I buy it as a gift?

      Yes. Drop me a line and I’ll send you a gift card to print or email.
