about me

Hello! I’m Marnie, and I draw every day

I spent eight years as a graphic designer in New York City, drawing only in meeting notes. In 2017 I moved home to Asbury Park, bought an iPad “for work” and started drawing my breakfast every morning.

People asked how. So I showed them, first in a few short videos, then in the Sunday Sketch, then in a real course. Today I teach full time from a tiny studio above a pizza place, with a view of the ocean if I lean.

I believe anyone can learn to draw. It isn’t a gift you’re born with; it’s a handful of skills you can practice, one wobbly line at a time.

How we got here

  1. 2017

    Back to Asbury Park, a secondhand iPad and one breakfast drawing a day.

  2. 2020

    The first Sunday Sketch goes out to 212 people, mostly my mom’s friends.

  3. 2021

    Draw Anything on iPad launches. The first student draws a very good dog.

  4. 2023

    Live Saturday workshops start, with a coffee pot on in every kitchen.

  5. 2025

    Thirty thousand students and a studio above the pizza place.

  6. 2026

    Seven courses, 41,000 students and a new animation course.

Things I believe about learning to draw

Shapes before details

Every drawing is a few circles, boxes and blobs wearing a costume. Get those right and the rest follows.

Little and often

Ten minutes a day beats a whole Sunday once a month. That’s why my lessons are short.

Wobbly is charming

Nobody wants a drawing that looks like a photo. Your wobble is your style, so keep it.

What’s in my bag

The kit I use for every course and workshop. You don’t need any of it to start, but people always ask.

  • 02A stylus with a soft rubber grip, because hands get tired
  • 03Procreate for nearly everything, Fresco for watercolor days
  • 04A paper sketchbook and a black fineliner for the beach
  • 05A very large mug that appears in roughly half my drawings
  • 06Headphones and a podcast about something else entirely
