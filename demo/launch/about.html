About us

We’re building the calm way to ship software

Launch is an independent, profitable company of 48 people across 14 countries. We make tools for teams who care about the craft of shipping.

Our story

Before Launch, Maya and Daniel ran product and engineering at a fast-growing startup. Every Friday they spent hours stitching together roadmaps, tickets and release notes from five different tools.

They built a small internal tool to connect it all. Within a year, friends at other companies were asking to use it. In 2019 they made it official.

Today Launch helps more than 4,000 teams plan and ship, and we still use it to build Launch every day.

Our values

What we believe

Respect attention

Every notification has to earn its place. Calm software makes better products.

Speed is a feature

If it takes more than 100 ms, it is a bug. We sweat performance so you do not wait.

Default to open

Public roadmap, public changelog, open salaries. We build the transparency we sell.

Small teams, big ownership

Teams of four own a problem end to end, from first sketch to release note.
