Our sourcing

Good coffee starts with a fair price.

First Crack began in 2017 with a 10-pound roaster in a Hampden garage. Today we roast a ton a week in an old trolley barn, and we still buy most of our coffee from the same fourteen producers.

How we buy

  1. 1

    Same farms, year after year

    Most of our coffee comes from producers we have bought from for three harvests or more. Long relationships let farmers plan, invest and experiment, and they let us commit to volumes before the harvest.

  2. 2

    Paid for quality, not the market

    We agree a price per pound on cup quality, never on the commodity market. When the market fell in 2023, our prices didn’t.

  3. 3

    Every price, published

    The table below shows what we paid for each coffee we sell this season, free on board at the export port. Ask us anything about it.
