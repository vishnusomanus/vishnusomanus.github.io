Questions we hear a lot

When will my coffee arrive?

Orders placed by Sunday night are roasted on Monday and shipped on Tuesday. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days after that; express arrives the next business day after shipping.

How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $45 and $6 below that. Express is $12. Subscriptions always ship free, and you can pick up at the roastery for nothing.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh for longer, so grind just before brewing if you can. If you don’t have a grinder, choose your brewer at checkout and we’ll grind it the morning it ships.

How long does a bag stay fresh?

Light roasts taste best from about a week to six weeks after the roast date printed on the bag; darker roasts peak a little sooner. Keep the bag sealed, away from light, and out of the fridge.

Can I change or pause my subscription?

Any time, up to the Friday before a roast. We email you two days ahead of every delivery with a link to skip, swap the coffee or change the schedule.

Are your bags recyclable?

Yes. Our bags are a single plastic (LDPE 4) with a recyclable valve, so they can go in store drop-off bins. Shipping boxes are recycled card.
