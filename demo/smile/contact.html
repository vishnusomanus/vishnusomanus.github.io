Getting here

  • By car

    Free parking behind the building, entered from N 13th St. Two accessible spaces by the door.

  • By bus

    Route 9 stops at 13th & Juniper, a one-minute walk away. Bike racks out front.

  • Wheelchair access

    Street-level entrance, wide doors, an accessible restroom and a treatment room with extra space.

  • Communication

    ASL appointments with Lena, free interpreters for any language and a quiet room if the waiting area feels busy.
