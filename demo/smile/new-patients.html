New patients

Nice to meet you. Here’s what happens next.

Your first visit takes about 75 minutes. No lectures, no surprises: just a real conversation, a gentle exam and a clear plan.

Book your first visit Fill out the patient form

Your first visit, step by step

  1. Step 1 · 5 min

    Before you come

    Book online or by phone, then fill out the new patient form below. Send your insurance details and we’ll check your benefits.

  2. Step 2 · 10 min

    Arrive and settle in

    Come 10 minutes early. We’ll give you a quick tour, offer the comfort menu and never keep you waiting in the chair.

  3. Step 3 · 10 min

    A real conversation

    Before anyone looks in your mouth, your dentist asks what’s bothering you, what you’d like to change and how you feel about dental visits.

  4. Step 4 · 45 min

    Exam, x-rays and a gentle cleaning

    A full exam of your teeth, gums and bite, digital x-rays and a cleaning with your hygienist. We tell you what we’re doing as we go.

  5. Step 5 · 10 min

    Your plan, in writing

    We show you what we found on screen, explain your options and give you a written plan with costs. No pressure to decide that day.

What to bring

  • A photo ID and your insurance card
  • A list of any medications you take
  • Recent x-rays, if your last dentist can send them
  • Your questions, big or small

For nervous patients

Scared of the dentist? You’re not the only one.

Lots of our patients felt the same before their first visit. These are the things that help most. Tell us when you book and we’ll plan around you.

Raise your hand, and we stop.

Every time. No questions asked.

Before your visit

  • Tell us how you feelMention it when you book or in the patient form. It changes how we plan your visit.
  • Pick a calm timeEarly mornings are quietest, and you won’t spend the day worrying.
  • Bring someoneA friend or partner is welcome to sit in with you.
  • Start with a helloYou can book a free 15-minute “meet the team” visit with no treatment at all.

In the chair

  • A stop signalRaise your hand at any time and we pause right away.
  • Tell-show-doWe explain each step and show you the instruments before we use them.
  • Numbing, done gentlyNumbing gel first, then a slow, steady injection, with time for it to work.
  • Laughing gasNitrous oxide can help you feel relaxed. Ask your dentist whether it’s suitable for you.

The comfort menu

Free for every patient. Just ask.

  • Noise-canceling headphones
  • Weighted blanket
  • Sunglasses for the bright light
  • Neck pillow and lip balm
  • Breaks whenever you need
  • Your own playlist or show

Afterward

  • A plan you can take homeEverything we discussed, in writing, with costs. Take your time to decide.
  • A check-in callAfter any treatment, we call the next day to see how you’re doing.
  • Small stepsWe can split treatment into shorter visits so each one feels manageable.
