Treatments

Gentle care for every smile in the family.

What each treatment involves, how long it takes and what it costs, explained in plain English. Not sure what you need? Start with a checkup and we’ll talk it through.

All treatments

  • General

    Checkups & cleanings

    The foundation of everything else: a thorough cleaning, a full exam and a talk about anything you’ve noticed.

    What to expect

    1. 1Your hygienist removes plaque and tartar, then polishes.
    2. 2Your dentist checks teeth, gums, bite and the soft tissues of your mouth.
    3. 3X-rays when they’re due, usually once a year, and a plan if anything needs attention.

    60 min

    From $165

    Included in Grin Club

    Book a checkup: Checkups & cleanings

  • General

    White fillings

    Tooth-colored fillings that repair a cavity or a small chip and blend in with your natural teeth.

    What to expect

    1. 1Numbing gel first, then a local anesthetic so you stay comfortable.
    2. 2Decay is removed and the filling is placed in thin layers.
    3. 3We shape and polish it and check your bite before you go.

    30–60 min

    From $180

    Per tooth

    Book an exam: White fillings

  • General

    Crowns

    A custom cap that protects a tooth weakened by a large filling, a crack or root canal treatment.

    What to expect

    1. 1We prepare the tooth and take a quick 3D scan, no goopy impressions.
    2. 2You leave with a comfortable temporary crown.
    3. 3About two weeks later we fit and fine-tune the porcelain crown.

    2 visits

    From $1,195

    Per tooth

    Book an exam: Crowns

  • General

    Root canal treatment

    Treats an infected or inflamed nerve inside a tooth so you can keep the tooth. Most people say it feels like a long filling.

    What to expect

    1. 1We numb the area thoroughly and place a small protective sheet.
    2. 2The inside of the root is cleaned, shaped and sealed.
    3. 3Most back teeth then need a crown to protect them.

    60–90 min

    From $950

    Front tooth; molars from $1,250

    Book an exam: Root canal treatment

    Gum care (deep cleaning)

    For gums that bleed or have pulled back: a deeper cleaning below the gum line, with numbing if you’d like it.

    What to expect

    1. 1We measure your gums to see where they need help.
    2. 2Each area is cleaned below the gum line, usually over two visits.
    3. 3A follow-up in about six weeks to check how they’re healing.

    2 visits

    From $240

    Per quarter of the mouth

    Book an exam: Gum care (deep cleaning)

Questions

Things people often ask us

Does it hurt?

Most treatment doesn’t. We use numbing gel before every injection and give the anesthetic time to work before we start. If you feel anything, raise your hand and we stop.

Nervous? Tell us when you book and we’ll plan extra time, breaks and comfort options.

How often should I have a checkup?

Most adults and children do well with a checkup and cleaning every six months. Some people need them more often, for example with gum problems. Your dentist will recommend what works for you.

Are your prices fixed?

The prices on this site are starting prices for straightforward cases. You’ll always get a written estimate before any treatment, and nothing is added without asking you first.

Do you take my insurance?

We’re in network with many PPO plans and will file claims for you. Send us your plan details before your visit and we’ll check your benefits so you know what to expect. Insurance and payment.

Is whitening safe for my teeth?

Professional whitening is a common, well-established treatment when it’s supervised by a dentist. Some people notice temporary sensitivity. We’ll check your teeth first, because whitening doesn’t change the color of fillings or crowns.

Am I too old for clear aligners?

There’s no upper age limit. What matters is healthy teeth and gums. At a free consult we’ll scan your teeth and tell you honestly whether aligners are a good fit.

When should my child first see a dentist?

Around their first tooth or first birthday, whichever comes first. Early visits are short and playful and help children feel at home. The first visit for children under 3 is free.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out adult tooth, or bleeding that won’t stop all need prompt care. Our symptom guide can help you decide what to do next.
