How it works

From your first lesson to real conversations

  1. 1

    Find your level

    Take our ten-question placement test, or book a free ten-minute chat with a teacher.

  2. 2

    Join a small class

    Groups of ten or fewer, in our San Antonio school or live online, at times that fit around work.

  3. 3

    Speak from week one

    Every class is at least half conversation, with feedback on the mistakes that matter.

Two ways to learn

Around a table, or around the world

In person in San Antonio

Five light-filled classrooms on Villita Lane, two minutes from the River Walk and ten from the Amtrak station.

  • Free coffee and tea before every class
  • A library of novels, films and comics to borrow
  • Monthly conversation evenings, free for students
In-person courses

Live online

The same teachers and small groups, live on video. Not a recording, and never more than ten faces on screen.

  • Breakout rooms for pair work
  • Class notes and recordings of new words after each lesson
  • Early-morning and lunchtime groups
