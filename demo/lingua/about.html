About Lingua

A school built around conversation

We opened in 2009 with two teachers, one classroom and a simple idea: adults learn languages best by talking to each other, with a teacher who cares whether they come back next week.

Our story

From one classroom to five, and online

Camille Roussel and Lucía Ferrer met teaching at a language school in Houston, where classes had twenty students and nobody got to speak. When they moved to San Antonio, they rented one room above a bike shop on Villita Lane and capped every class at ten.

Fifteen years later the bike shop has gone, and we have the whole building: five classrooms, a library and a kitchen that is always out of cookies. Since 2020 we also teach live online, with the same small groups and the same teachers.

How we teach

Four things you'll notice in every class

Half the time, you talk

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Ask us.

What if I'm in the wrong level?

Tell your teacher. In the first two weeks of term you can move to another level or course for free, as long as it has a seat open.

What happens if I miss a class?

Your teacher shares notes and homework after every lesson. In-person students can also join the online class at the same level once a term.

Do I need to buy a book?

Our courses use one course book per level ($28). It's optional, but most students find it useful for homework.

Can I get a certificate?

Yes. Everyone who attends at least eight classes gets a certificate with their level. We can also prepare you for DELE, DELF, CILS and Goethe exams.

Is the school accessible?

Yes. There's step-free access from Villita Lane, an elevator to every floor and an accessible restroom. Classrooms have hearing loops.
