Levels

Which level am I?

We use the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR), the same levels as most exams and schools in Europe.

A1Beginner
You're starting from scratch or remember only a few words.
A2Elementary
You can handle simple, familiar situations and want more confidence.
B1Intermediate
You can get by on a trip and want to say more of what you really think.
B2Upper intermediate
You speak with some ease and want to sound natural on any topic.
C1Advanced
You're fluent and want nuance, idiom and confidence in fast conversation.

Still not sure? Take the free placement test.
