Spanish for Beginners

Your first ten weeks of Spanish: real conversations from the very first evening.

When
Mon & Wed, 6:30–8:00 PM
Starts
January 11, 2027
Length
10 weeks, 30 hours
Where
In person · Room 2
Class size
Up to 10 students
Teacher
Lucía Ferrer

A friendly evening class for complete beginners. You'll learn to introduce yourself, order, ask for directions and talk about your week, with grammar introduced as you need it and plenty of speaking in pairs.

By the end of the course you'll be able to

  • Introduce yourself and others
  • Order in a café or restaurant
  • Ask for directions and understand the answer
  • Talk about your day and your plans

What we'll cover

  1. 1Weeks 1–2First conversationsGreetings, introducing yourself, numbers and spelling your name.
  2. 2Weeks 3–4Everyday lifeDays, times and routines; talking about home, family and work.
  3. 3Weeks 5–6Out and aboutOrdering food and drink, shopping and asking about prices.
  4. 4Weeks 7–8Getting aroundDirections, transport and places in town.
  5. 5Weeks 9–10Plans and memoriesMaking plans and saying what you did last weekend.

Lucía Ferrer

Head of Spanish · from Valencia · 14 years teaching

Lucía taught in Valencia and Houston before helping to found Lingua. She believes in talking from the first minute, and in learning the grammar once you need it.

“Mistakes are the sound of someone learning.”
