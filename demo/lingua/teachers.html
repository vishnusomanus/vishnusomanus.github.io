Our teachers

Eight teachers, many accents, one way of teaching

Qualified, experienced and native speakers of the languages they teach. Between them they've taught for more than ninety years, in San Antonio and in their home cities.

Showing all 8 teachers

  • Spanish

Lucía Ferrer

Head of Spanish · from Valencia

Lucía taught in Valencia and Houston before helping to found Lingua. She believes in talking from the first minute, and in learning the grammar once you need it.

“Mistakes are the sound of someone learning.”

Teaching this term: Spanish for Beginners, Intermediate Spanish

  • Spanish
  • Portuguese

Tomás Ibarra

Spanish teacher · from Bilbao

Tomás grew up between Bilbao and São Paulo. He teaches our online Spanish groups and loves a good debate about soccer, food or film.

“Every class should have at least one good laugh.”

Teaching this term: Spanish Elementary

  • Portuguese
  • Spanish

Beatriz Lima

Portuguese and Spanish teacher · from Porto

Beatriz runs the Saturday conversation club and our Portuguese course. Before teaching she was a radio presenter in Porto.

“Listen first. The words will follow.”

Teaching this term: Spanish Conversation Club, Portuguese Elementary

  • French

Camille Roussel

Academic director, French · from Lyon

Camille co-founded Lingua in 2009. She trains our teachers, writes our level descriptors and still teaches the advanced French class every Thursday.

“A language is a way of seeing, not a list of rules.”

Teaching this term: Lunchtime French, Advanced French: Culture and Debate

  • French

Julien Mbaye

French teacher · from Dakar and Marseille

Julien teaches beginners and brings French from Dakar, Montréal and Marseille into class through music, podcasts and news.

“French has many accents. You'll hear all of them.”

Teaching this term: French for Beginners

  • Italian

Giulia Conti

Head of Italian · from Bologna

Giulia is a translator as well as a teacher. Her Saturday group ends each term with a long lunch, ordered entirely in Italian.

“Gesture counts. So does pronunciation.”

Teaching this term: Italian for Beginners, Italian Intermediate

  • German

Anke Weber

German teacher · from Hamburg

Anke designed our two-week German intensive. She also teaches early-morning online classes for people who learn best before work.

“Small steps, every day, add up fast.”

Teaching this term: German Intensive Beginners, Upper-intermediate German

  • Japanese

Haruka Sato

Japanese teacher · from Sendai

Haruka teaches speaking and the two kana scripts side by side, with plenty of practice from manga, menus and train schedules.

“Read a little every day. Kana become friends.”

Teaching this term: Japanese for Beginners
