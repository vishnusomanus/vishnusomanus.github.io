About / Since 1987

Built to last

Three generations of builders, twelve architects and one rule: the people who draw the building sit next to the people who build it.

01 / Our story

From a framing crew to a design-build firm.

Walt Corbel started with a pickup, two carpenters and a reputation for framing that came out square. Contractors kept calling him back to fix the gaps between what architects drew and what got built.

So when his son Daniel took over the commercial side, and architect Amara Okafor joined in 2009, they closed the gap for good: one firm, one team, one contract. Corbel Studio now designs most of what we build, and every drawing is priced by the estimators sitting across the room.

In 2017 we became fully employee-owned. The 140 people who work here own the company, and you can tell on site.

  1. 1987

    Walt Corbel starts a three-person framing crew in a rented garage on Mitchell Street.

  2. 1996

    First commercial contract: the Bay View Credit Union branch, still open today.

  3. 2009

    Amara Okafor founds Corbel Studio, bringing architecture in-house.

  4. 2017

    Madison studio opens. Corbel becomes 100% employee-owned.

  5. 2025

    Malt House Lofts wins the Wisconsin Preservation Award.

02 / What we stand for

Four rules we don’t bend.

  • 01

    Draw it, price it, build it

    Every drawing is costed by the people who will build it, the day it is made.

  • 02

    Open books

    Clients see the same cost ledger we do. Savings are shared 50/50.

  • 03

    Everyone home safe

    Any worker can stop any task. No schedule is worth an injury.

  • 04

    Buildings that last

    We design for a hundred years and detail for the next owner too.
