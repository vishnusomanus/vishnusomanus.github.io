06 / Ways to work with us

Three contract models.

  • Recommended

    Design-build

    One contract for design and construction. Budget fixed at the end of concept design as a guaranteed maximum price.

    • Single point of responsibility
    • Fastest route to site
    • Savings shared 50/50

  • Your architect

    Construction manager

    We join your architect’s team early, price every design stage and build with an open-book fee.

    • Keep the architect you chose
    • Cost checks at each stage
    • Open-book fee of 4–6%

  • Bid projects

    General contracting

    You bring complete drawings, we tender them to our trade partners and give you a lump-sum price.

    • Fixed lump sum
    • For finished designs
    • Public-sector ready
