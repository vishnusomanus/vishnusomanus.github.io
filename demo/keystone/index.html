03 / How we work

From first sketch to handover.

Because design and construction sit in the same office, the steps overlap. Permits start before design is finished, and the site team prices every drawing as it is made.

  2. 01 Weeks 0–12

    Design

    Site study, concept options and a design you can walk through in 3D. Your fixed budget is set here, not after.

    • Measured survey
    • Concept & 3D model
    • Budget at ±8%
  3. 02 Weeks 8–20

    Permits

    Construction drawings, zoning and building permits, and a guaranteed maximum price you sign once.

    • Permit drawings
    • Zoning approvals
    • GMP contract
  4. 03 Months 5–20

    Build

    One site lead, weekly photo reports and an open-book cost ledger you can check any day.

    • Weekly reports
    • Open-book costs
    • Daily safety briefs
  5. 04 Final 4 weeks

    Handover

    Snag list, manuals and training. We come back at month 11 for a full warranty walk.

    • Snag-free handover
    • O&M manuals
    • 2-year warranty
