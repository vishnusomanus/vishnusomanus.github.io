What happens next?

We start with your research process, then agree on the right seats and allowance. A named specialist helps your team set up a repeatable review routine.

Can we change the plan later?

Yes. We review changes with you before a new billing period. There is no automatic jump to another plan when a forecast changes.

Do you hold or trade assets?

No. Hashfen is a research product. It does not take custody of assets, execute trades or provide investment advice.

Can we begin with our own assumptions?

Yes. Start with a profile, adjust the planner inputs and save the scenario with your research note.
