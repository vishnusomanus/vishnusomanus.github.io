Everything we cook touches the flames.

There is no gas line in our kitchen. Bread, fish, vegetables and the occasional whole lamb all cook over oak and olive wood, in an oven we built by hand a decade ago.

The menu follows the market and changes most weeks. What stays the same is the warmth: of the fire, of the room, and of a team that has mostly been here since the beginning.
