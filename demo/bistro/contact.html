Questions

Frequently asked

Can you cater for allergies?

Yes. Tell us when you book and again when you arrive. Our menu marks vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and the kitchen can adapt most plates. We cook over one shared fire, so we can't guarantee the absence of allergens.

Do you take walk-ins?

Always. We keep the counter and a few tables free every evening. Fridays and Saturdays get busy after 7pm, so come early or join the list at the bar.

Are children and dogs welcome?

Children are very welcome, and we have high chairs and a small kids' menu. Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the terrace.

Is the restaurant accessible?

The dining room, terrace and toilets are step-free. The Cellar Room is down a flight of stairs; please let us know if you need help planning an event.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount, at the restaurant or by phone. They're valid for two years and can be used for food, wine and events.
