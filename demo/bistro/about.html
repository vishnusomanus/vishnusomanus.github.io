Our story

A neighborhood kitchen, built around a fire.

Olive & Ember started with a secondhand oven, twenty-two seats and a stubborn idea: cook everything over wood, buy from people we know, and make everyone feel like a regular.

How we got here

Ten years of cooking over flames

  1. 2014

    A secondhand oven

    Inês and Tomás open a 22-seat room on Harbor Street with an oven rescued from a closing bakery.

  2. 2016

    Fire only

    We remove the last gas burner. Every dish now cooks over oak and olive wood from Hollow Oak Wood Co.

  3. 2018

    The long table

    A 23-foot table, made from reclaimed boatyard timber, becomes the heart of the dining room.

  4. 2021

    The Cellar Room

    We open the old bakery cellar next door for private dinners and our monthly winemaker suppers.

  5. 2024

    Restaurant of the Year

    Named Restaurant of the Year at the Coastal Food Awards. We celebrate with a very long lunch.

The people

Meet the team

Twenty-three people cook, pour, wash and welcome at Olive & Ember. Here are four of them.

Inês Duarte

Head chef & co-owner

Trained in Lisbon and San Sebastián, then came home to build the oven herself.

Tomás Reyes

Co-owner & front of house

Runs the room, the music and most of the jokes. Knows every regular by name.

Amara Cole

Sommelier

Visits every winemaker on our list and pours with stories to match.

Theo Marchetti

Pastry chef

Bakes the bread at dawn and the olive oil cake by lunchtime.
