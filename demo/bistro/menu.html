Good to know

Allergies & dietary needs

VVegetarian
Vegetarian
VGVegan
Vegan (also vegetarian)
GFGluten-free
Gluten-free

Our kitchen handles nuts, sesame, gluten, dairy, fish and shellfish, and everything shares one fire, so we can't guarantee any dish is completely free of allergens. Please tell your server about allergies before you order and we'll guide you through the menu.

A full allergen matrix is available on request. Prices don't include sales tax. Gratuity is up to you and is shared with the whole team.
