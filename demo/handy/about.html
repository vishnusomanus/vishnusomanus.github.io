About Brightline

A local team that picks up the phone

We’re 38 licensed technicians, office staff and project managers who live in the neighborhoods we work in. Family-owned since 2011.

Our story

Dana Ruiz spent ten years watching homeowners get vague quotes, surprise invoices and no-shows. In 2011 she started Brightline with a simple promise: show up on time, price the job before starting and fix it right the first time.

  1. 2011

    Dana Ruiz starts Brightline with one van and a pager.

  2. 2015

    Electrical crew joins; we move into the Larimer Street workshop.

  3. 2019

    Heating & cooling team added; 24/7 emergency line launches.

  4. 2024

    Remodel studio opens; 10,000th job completed.

  5. 2026

    38 technicians, 21 vans and a 4.9-star rating.

What we stand for

  • Tell the truth

    If a repair isn't worth it, we say so, even when replacing would earn us more.

  • Leave it cleaner

  • Own the outcome

    Our two-year guarantee means the job isn't done until it's done right.
