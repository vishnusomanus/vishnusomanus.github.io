Before you book

No. Online estimates are instant and free, and in-home quotes for remodels are free too. Repair visits include a $79 diagnostic fee that is waived if you go ahead with the work.

Are your prices really upfront?

Yes. Your technician gives you a written flat-rate price before any work starts. If we discover something unexpected, we stop, explain it and ask before doing anything extra.

Do you work weekends and holidays?

Our emergency line is open 24/7, 365 days a year. Standard appointments run Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

What does the two-year guarantee cover?

Our workmanship on every job. If something we fixed fails because of how we did it, we come back and make it right at no charge.
