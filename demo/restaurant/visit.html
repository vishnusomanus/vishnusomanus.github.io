The room before service.

Twenty-eight seats, set while the hills are still bright. There is no music. Coats go on hooks by the door. The kitchen is visible from the last four seats, which we give to people who ask for them.

Largo da Estação 2, Pinhão. The station is a three-minute walk. There is no car park on the square; leave the car by the river.
