A church for everyone in Lawrence, Kansas

There’s a seat saved for you.

Riverbend is a non-denominational church on the bend of the Kansas River. Whether you’ve been to church all your life or never at all, come as you are. The coffee is good, the kids’ rooms are safe and the questions are welcome.

Everything about your first visit
  1. 1

    Tell us you’re coming

    Pick a Sunday and we’ll save you seats, get the kids’ check-in ready and have someone meet you at the welcome tent.

    Plan your visit
  2. 2

    Come as you are

    Seventy-five minutes of songs, a short prayer, a practical message and good coffee. Nobody will ask you to stand up or say anything.

  3. 3

    Find your people

    Church gets smaller when you join a group. Thirteen meet around Lawrence and online, on every night of the week.
