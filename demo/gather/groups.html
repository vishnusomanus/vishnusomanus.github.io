    Ministries

    Where Riverbend shows up during the week

    Riverbend Kids

    Birth to 5th grade · Sundays

    Bible stories told well, songs with actions, crafts and a lot of snacks, at both Sunday gatherings. Every room has two background-checked adults and a secure check-in.

    • • Nursery and preschool rooms
    • • Elementary large and small groups
    • • Kids’ craft mornings
    • • Family movie nights

    Led by June Whitaker, Kids Director

    Riverbend Youth

    Grades 6–12 · Wednesdays 6:30 pm

    Dinner, a ridiculous game, worship and small groups split by grade. Plus a summer camp, a winter retreat and a serve week in Kansas City.

    • • Wednesday Youth Night in the Loft
    • • Grade-based small groups
    • • Summer camp at Lake Perry
    • • Parent nights each semester

    Led by Marcus Bell, Youth Director

    Care & Riverbend Pantry

    Every day, and Saturdays 9–12

    A meal train after a new baby, surgery or loss; hospital visits; grief and recovery groups; and a client-choice food pantry open to anyone in Douglas County.

    • • Meal train and visits
    • • Grief and recovery groups
    • • Counseling referrals and help with costs
    • • Riverbend Pantry, no questions asked

    Led by Hannah Brooks and Ruth Aldana

    Music & Arts

    Rehearsals Thursdays 7 pm

    The band and choir lead Sundays, and the open Thursday rehearsal is for anyone who wants to sing or play, whatever their level. Artists help shape our seasons and spaces.

    • • Band and community choir
    • • Sound, lights and livestream teams
    • • Visual artists’ collective
    • • Songwriting workshops

    Led by Theo Nakamura, Worship & Arts
