About us

Twelve friends, a borrowed coffee urn and a bend in the river.

Riverbend is an independent, non-denominational church in Lawrence, Kansas. We’re about 650 people on a Sunday: students and retirees, lifelong churchgoers and people who are still not sure what they believe.

Our story

We didn’t set out to start a big church. We set out to be the kind of people who would notice our neighbors, and the rest followed, slowly.

Come and see

  1. 2011

    A living room in Old West Lawrence

    Dana Okafor and eleven friends start meeting on Sunday nights to read Luke and eat dinner. Within a year they need folding chairs.

  2. 2013

    A bakery on Massachusetts Street

    A closed bakery lends us its back room on Sundays. The building smells of cinnamon for two years and nobody complains.

  3. 2016

    An old roller rink by the river

    We buy the Riverside Avenue rink and renovate it with a lot of volunteer weekends. The skate-hire counter is now the kids’ check-in desk.

  4. 2020

    The Riverbend Pantry opens

    What started as a box of groceries on the porch becomes a client-choice pantry serving about 180 households a week.

  5. Today

    Two gatherings, thirteen groups, one long table

    We’re renovating the River Room so the whole neighborhood can use it, and we still put the coffee on at 8:30.

What we believe

Historic Christian faith, held with open hands

We stand with the church across the centuries in the Apostles’ and Nicene Creeds. These are the convictions that shape everything we do.

  • God is love

    One God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, who made the world good and has never stopped caring for it.

  • Jesus changes everything

    Jesus lived, died and rose again. Through him anyone can be forgiven, made new and welcomed home.

  • The Bible is trustworthy

    Scripture is God’s word to us. We read it together, carefully and in context, and let it read us.

  • Grace comes first

    Nobody earns their place here. We follow Jesus because we’re loved, not so that we will be.

  • Church is a family

    We practice baptism and communion, share our lives in small groups and carry each other’s burdens.

  • Love your city

    Faith that stays inside the building isn’t finished. We serve Lawrence alongside anyone doing good.

How we’re led

Riverbend is led by a board of seven elders elected by members, who oversee the pastors, the budget and the care of the church. Our finances are reviewed by an independent accountant every year, and our safeguarding policy is reviewed with the Douglas County child advocacy center. Ask for either; we’re glad to share them.
