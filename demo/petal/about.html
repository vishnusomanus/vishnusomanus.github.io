About us

A small flower shop downtown on Mulberry Street.

Bettany Corbett opened Hollyhock in 2014 in a former shoe repair shop with a leaky skylight and a bucket of sweet peas. The skylight is fixed; the sweet peas still come from the same grower near Fort Valley.

We buy from Georgia and Carolina growers when the season allows and through the Miami flower importers when it doesn’t, and we never wrap anything in plastic.

Good to know

What if no one is home?+

We’ll leave the flowers somewhere safe and shady, or with a neighbor, and text you a photo. Add a note at checkout if there’s a good spot.

Can I choose a delivery time?+

We deliver between 10am and 6pm and can’t promise an exact time, but we text the recipient when we’re on our way. For funerals and weddings we agree a time with you.

Will the bouquet look like the picture?+

It will keep the colors, size and feel, but the exact flowers change with the season and the market. That’s half the fun.

Something wasn’t right. What now?+

Send us a photo within two days of delivery and we’ll replace the bouquet or refund you. No forms, no fuss.

Can you deliver for Valentine’s Day and Mother’s Day?+

Valentine’s Day is February 14 and Mother’s Day is the second Sunday in May. They’re our busiest days, so book as soon as the delivery calendar opens, four weeks ahead.
