Shipping and returns

Shipping

Orders placed by Sunday night leave Hudson on Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday. Shipping is $8, and free when the order is $80 or more. We ship within the United States. Glass and clay are packed in a double box.

Returns

You have 30 days. Write to hello@lumengoods.com with your order number and we will send a label. Candles that have been burned cannot come back. Everything else can, if it is unused and in the box it arrived in.
