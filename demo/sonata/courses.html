Before your first note

Do I need to read music?

No. Reading grows alongside listening and playing. Beginner courses start from the beginning, including how to hold and care for an instrument.

Can I borrow an instrument?

Pianos, keyboards and drum sets are here for lessons. We have a small pool of child-sized guitars and violins to try in the room. For home practice, ask about local rental options before buying.

What if a group is the wrong fit?

Tell your teacher after the first lesson. We can suggest another group or individual teaching and carry the unused lesson credit over.
