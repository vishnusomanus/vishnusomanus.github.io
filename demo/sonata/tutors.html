The right person
makes a difference.

Prefer a very clear plan? Want to learn by ear? Need a little longer to process a new idea? Tell us. We will talk through the options and introduce you to a teacher before you commit to a term.

All teachers follow our child-safety code, complete background checks for their work with children, and take part in peer observations each term. Parents and guardians are welcome at a child’s first lesson.
