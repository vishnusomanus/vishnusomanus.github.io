A little help finding the door.

Step-free arrival

Use the courtyard entrance from North Walnut Street. The gate and teaching-room doors have a clear width of 36 inches. Two teaching rooms and the accessible restroom are on the ground floor. Tell us if stairs are a barrier so we book the right room.

Bus, bike and parking

Bloomington Transit’s Downtown Transit Center is around ten minutes on foot. There are bike racks inside the courtyard. Use public parking at the downtown garages; the courtyard is for pedestrian access and pre-arranged drop-off.

Before you visit

Can I wait during my child’s lesson?

Yes. Our courtyard waiting room has seating, water, books and a small table. Parents are welcome in a child’s first lesson. For safety, children under 12 must be handed directly to a teacher and picked up from the waiting room.

Is there somewhere quiet to wait?

The smaller ground-floor waiting area can be reserved for a quiet arrival. Please tell us before your first visit. We can also arrange a familiarization visit outside the busiest after-school hours.

What happens on federal holidays?

The school is closed on federal holidays. Term confirmations list the affected dates and any replacement lessons. We do not schedule regular federal-holiday sessions.
