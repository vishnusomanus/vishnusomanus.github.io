# Changelog

All notable changes to Sonata are recorded here.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.4] - 2026-11-07

### Changed

- American wording throughout, including US terms for checkboxes, business days, schedules and units.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Bloomington, Indiana, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.
- Times show on a 12-hour clock in Eastern time and dates use the US format. The school now closes on US federal holidays, worked out by rule, and each term's mid-term break week starts on Presidents' Day, Memorial Day or Columbus Day.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed

- The logo link's accessible name now includes its visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).
- The course page's Save course button's accessible name now includes its visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Ten responsive pages for an independent music school, with original art for every course, page illustrations and tutor portraits.
- Course search and filters, tutor filter, weekly timetable showing the current teaching week, term-price calculator and guided enrollment inquiry with a confirmation and reference number.
- Term, half-term and bank-holiday dates worked out for any year, with the date new starters join.
- Saved shortlist, weekly practice planner and accessible contact form.
- Complete customization, deployment and integration documentation.
