Finding your time

Do lessons run during school breaks?

Our regular groups follow a ten-week teaching term. Your confirmation includes every lesson date and the break week. Individual sessions during breaks are arranged directly with your teacher.

Can siblings come at the same time?

We try to match parallel lessons where the schedule allows. Tell us both instruments and ages in your inquiry. The waiting room has books, coloring materials and a small table.

What if I miss a lesson?

Please let us know as soon as possible. With 48 hours’ notice, individual lessons can be rearranged once per term subject to your teacher’s availability. Group lessons cannot be refunded individually, but we share the practice notes.
