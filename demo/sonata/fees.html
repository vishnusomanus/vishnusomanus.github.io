Money questions

Are there joining fees?

No joining or administration fee. Printed beginner books usually cost $10–$20 and are only purchased after your teacher recommends one. No book is required for an introduction.

Can I pay in installments?

For a ten-week term we can split payment into two equal amounts, due before lessons one and six. Ask when we confirm your spot. There is no additional installment charge.

How do scholarships work?

We offer six group spots at 40% of the regular fee each term, prioritizing households who could not otherwise attend. Ask us privately for a short conversation; no financial documents are uploaded through this website.

What is your cancellation policy?

Before a term starts, cancellations at least seven days ahead receive a refund less any lessons already taken. Once a group term begins, we can carry remaining credit to a suitable course by agreement. If we cancel a lesson, we offer a replacement or refund.
