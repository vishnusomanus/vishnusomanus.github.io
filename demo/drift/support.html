Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. The free plan works without an account. You only need one if you want to back up your diary or share a Family plan.

Which phones does Nightjar work on?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later, and Android phones with Android 10 or later. There’s no tablet layout yet, but the app runs on iPad and Android tablets.

Does Nightjar work offline?

Yes. Free sounds are built in, and Premium sounds can be downloaded. The alarm, routines and diary never need a connection.

Sleep sounds

How do I mix sounds?

On Premium, start one sound, then tap “Add to mix” on up to two more. Each has its own volume slider. Save the mix to find it on the Tonight screen.

Can sounds play all night?

Yes. Set the sleep timer to “All night”. Otherwise the sound fades out slowly when the timer ends.

Why does a sound stop when my screen locks?

Some Android battery savers close apps in the background. In your phone’s battery settings, set Nightjar to “Unrestricted”.

Smart alarm

How does the smart alarm decide when to wake me?

In your wake window, Nightjar uses the microphone to listen for movement such as turning over. When it notices you stirring, it starts a gentle sound. If it hears nothing, it rings at your set time. Audio is analyzed on the phone and never recorded or uploaded.

Will it go off if my phone is on silent?

Yes. Alarms play even in silent mode and Do Not Disturb, as long as the volume isn’t at zero.

Where should I put my phone?

On the nightstand, plugged in, within about three feet of you. Under the pillow muffles the microphone.

Premium and billing

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone go to Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android open Google Play, Payments and subscriptions.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their policies. If something went wrong, contact us too and we’ll help where we can.

How do I add people to a Family plan?

Open Settings, then Family, and send an invite link. Each person gets their own sounds, alarm and diary.

Privacy and health

Is Nightjar a medical device?

No. Nightjar is a wellness app to help you relax and build routines. It doesn’t diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. If you have persistent sleep problems, speak to a doctor.

Do you record audio at night?

No. The smart alarm listens for movement in your wake window only, analyzes it on your phone and keeps nothing.

How do I delete my data?

Your diary lives on your phone, so deleting the app removes it. If you made an account, go to Settings, Account, Delete account, and we erase your backup within 30 days.

About Nightjar

Nightjar is a sleep and wind-down app for iPhone and Android, made by Nightjar Labs in Sacramento, California. It combines sleep sounds, a guided evening routine, a smart alarm that wakes people in a lighter moment of their wake window and a simple sleep diary. Nightjar is free to start, with Premium and Family plans. It is a wellness app, not a medical device.

Privacy

Privacy in plain English.

  • On your phone first

    Your diary, routines and alarm settings are stored on your phone. Backup is optional.

  • No recordings

    The smart alarm analyzes sound for movement in real time and keeps nothing.

  • No ads, no data sales

    We make money from subscriptions, not from your data.

  • Anonymous app analytics

    We count things like crashes and which sounds are popular, without linking them to you. You can turn this off in Settings.
