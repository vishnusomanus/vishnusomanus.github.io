Smart alarm

An alarm that waits for a lighter moment.

Set the latest time you want to be up and a wake window of 10, 20 or 30 minutes. With your phone on the nightstand, Nightjar listens for movement and starts a gentle sound when you seem to be stirring. If it doesn’t notice anything, it rings at your set time.

  • Never later than the time you choose
  • A slow sunrise glow on screen before the sound starts
  • Microphone audio is analyzed on the phone and never recorded or uploaded

Sleep diary

Ten seconds each morning.

Tap how you feel, add a note about yesterday and carry on with your day. Over a few weeks, simple patterns appear: maybe you sleep better after an evening walk, or worse after a late coffee.

  • Notes and moods, not scores
  • Export a PDF to share with a health professional
  • Stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up
