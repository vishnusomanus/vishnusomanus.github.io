Try to fall asleep at one of these times

To wake up at 7:00 AM, aim to be in bed by one of these times. We’ve allowed about 15 minutes to fall asleep.

    A general guide, not a rule. Sleep cycles average about 90 minutes but vary from person to person and night to night. Most adults need around 7 to 9 hours.

    How it works

    Counting in cycles, not hours.

    Through the night you move through cycles of lighter and deeper sleep. Waking at the end of a cycle, when sleep is lighter, tends to feel less groggy.

    The calculator adds or subtracts whole 90-minute cycles from your time, plus the minutes you usually take to fall asleep. It can’t know how your own night will go: that’s what the smart alarm in the app is for.

    Good to know

    More on routines and mornings in our sleep guides.

    Is 90 minutes right for everyone?

    No. It’s a commonly used average. Real cycles can be shorter or longer, and they change through the night, so the suggested times are a starting point you can adjust.

    Which suggestion should I pick?

    The highlighted options give 7.5 or 9 hours of sleep, which suits most adults. The shorter options are there for the nights when that isn’t possible.

    Why does it ask how long I take to fall asleep?

    Your first cycle starts when you fall asleep, not when you get into bed, so the calculator adds that time on.

    I still wake up tired. What should I do?

    Feeling tired often has many causes. If you regularly wake unrefreshed, snore loudly or struggle to sleep for weeks at a time, speak to a doctor. Nightjar isn’t a medical device and can’t diagnose sleep problems.
