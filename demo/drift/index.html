TonightSoundsAlarmDiary

A sound for every kind of quiet.

Sixty-four sounds recorded and tuned for sleep, from rain on canvas to a Sierra creek. Mix up to three, set a sleep timer and let them fade out on their own.

  • Gentle fade-out when the timer ends
  • Works offline, so you can keep your phone in airplane mode
  • Mix rain, noise and nature at their own volumes
Browse the sound library

Mornings that start softly.

Choose a latest wake time and a window of up to 30 minutes. Nightjar listens for movement and rings when you seem to be stirring, with a slow sunrise glow on screen.

  • Never rings later than the time you set
  • Dawn chorus, bells or a soft piano to wake to
  • Works with your phone on the nightstand
How the smart alarm works

The hour before bed, made easier.

Build a short routine of steps you actually enjoy: dim the lights, some breathing, a few pages of a book. Nightjar nudges you when it’s time to start and keeps you company through each step.

  • Guided breathing with a slow, glowing pacer
  • Gentle reminders, never a guilt trip
  • Ready-made routines for busy evenings
Build your own routine

Spot the habits that help.

Each morning, tap how you feel and add a note or two: a late coffee, an evening walk. After a few weeks you’ll see which evenings tend to lead to better mornings.

  • Takes ten seconds each morning
  • Your diary stays on your phone
  • Export it if you want to share it with your doctor

Gentle by design

No streaks to break. No ads at bedtime.

Sleep isn’t a contest. Nightjar keeps things calm, private and honest about what an app can and can’t do.

  • Private by default

    Your diary and alarm data stay on your phone. No account needed for the free plan.

  • Dark all the way down

    Every screen is dim and warm, with a red-tint night mode for 3am glances.

  • Works offline

    Download sounds once and use them with Wi-Fi and data switched off.

  • Honest about health

    Nightjar is a wellness app, not a medical device. Ongoing sleep trouble deserves a chat with a doctor.
