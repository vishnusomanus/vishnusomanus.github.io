Donor FAQ

Questions about giving

Can't find your answer? Email supporter care at give@clearwatertrust.example.

Is my donation tax-deductible?+

Yes. Clearwater Trust is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts are tax-deductible in the US to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive a receipt by email.

Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?+

Any time, with one email or from the link in your receipt. No questions asked.

How do you choose which projects to fund?+

Communities apply through our local partners. We fund projects with community ownership, a repair plan and fair water fees.

Do you accept gifts of stock or from donor-advised funds?+

We do. Contact our supporter care team and we'll send the details.
