About us

We believe water is a right, and that lasting change is led locally.

Clearwater Trust funds and supports community-led water, sanitation and hygiene projects in nine countries. We've stayed small, local and accountable on purpose.

Our mission

To bring safe, lasting water to rural communities by backing the people who live there.

Our vision

A world where no child walks hours for water, and every tap keeps running.

Our story

Seventeen years, one village at a time

What started with one broken pump has grown into a network of partners, mechanics and committees, but the approach hasn't changed.

  1. 2009

    A borehole and a promise

    Founded in Minneapolis after co-founder Grace Wanjiru's home village in Kenya asked for help fixing a broken well.

  2. 2012

    Community committees

    We make village water committees a condition of every project. Breakdowns fall by two thirds.

  3. 2015

    First schools in Nepal

    After the earthquake, we help 40 schools rebuild with rainwater harvesting built in.

  4. 2019

    Sensors on every pump

    Remote sensors flag breakdowns in hours. Repairs now average 3 days.

  5. 2023

    One million people

    Our partners reach the one-millionth person with safe, lasting water.

  6. 2026

    Mechanics cooperatives

    312 trained mechanics now run their own repair businesses across East Africa.
