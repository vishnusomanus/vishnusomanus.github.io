StrengthR., 44 · 1:1 with Ffion · 2× a week · 16 weeks

From nervous in the weight room to a 245 lb deadlift

Starting point
Had never lifted a barbell and found gyms intimidating. Wanted to feel strong and stop putting his back out moving furniture.
What we did
Hinge patterns with a kettlebell first, then the trap bar. Small, steady jumps in weight, logged every session.
Where they are now
Deadlifting 245 lb for a single with good form, and training on his own on Fridays.
“I’d never touched a barbell. Now Tuesday mornings are the best part of my week.”

StrengthJ., 29 · Small-group strength · 2× a week · 12 weeks

Zero to twelve full push-ups

Starting point
Couldn’t do a full push-up and wanted upper-body strength for climbing with friends.
What we did
Incline push-ups on a lowering bar height, rows to balance it out and a short home routine.
Where they are now
Twelve full push-ups in one set, and her first unassisted chin-up.
“The group made me show up even on the days I didn’t feel like it.”

SportM., 36 · Sport block with Yusuf · 2× a week · 12 weeks

Five minutes off a 5K time, without more running

Starting point
Stuck at around 31 minutes for a 5K for two years, with achy knees whenever mileage went up.
What we did
Two strength sessions a week, plyometrics and a simple speed session. Weekly mileage stayed the same.
Where they are now
A 26:28 5K at week 12 and a calf-strength routine he still does twice a week.
“The strength work did what extra miles never did. And my knees feel great.”

Fat lossS., 52 · Small group + nutrition add-on · 6 months

Doubled her daily steps, one small habit at a time

Starting point
Busy job, low energy by mid-afternoon and a history of diets that didn’t last.
What we did
Two group sessions a week and one nutrition habit every two weeks: protein at breakfast, a lunchtime walk, a regular bedtime.
Where they are now
Averaging over 9,000 steps a day, sleeping better and lifting more than she thought she could.
“No diet, no drama. Just habits I could actually keep.”

MobilityD., 58 · Movement & mobility with Yusuf · weekly · 12 weeks

Touching his toes again after twenty years at a desk

Starting point
Stiff hips and hamstrings, and a back that felt tight after long days at the desk.
What we did
A movement screen, a ten-minute morning routine and strength work through his new range.
Where they are now
Reaching an inch past his toes, gardening for whole afternoons and walking 6 miles on weekends.
“Yusuf’s mobility work changed how my whole day feels.”

PostpartumL., 34 · Return to training with Ffion · 18 weeks

Strong enough for the toddler years

Starting point
Started 14 weeks after her baby was born, once her doctor had said she could exercise. Wanted to feel like herself again.
What we did
Breathing and core foundations, then squats, carries and rows for everyday lifting, alongside her pelvic floor physical therapist’s advice.
Where they are now
Goblet squatting 35 lb and carrying a car seat up two flights of stairs with ease.
“I never felt rushed back into anything. Calm, kind and truly expert.”
