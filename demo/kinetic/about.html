About Foothill

Two coaches. One small studio.

Foothill started with a simple idea: everyone deserves coaching that fits their body, their week and their life. No mirrors full of selfies, no crowded floor, no shouting. Just good training.

Est. 2019

Our story

Built for people
who hate gyms

Ffion opened Foothill in a converted carriage works off West Colorado Avenue in 2019. Most of her first clients had one thing in common: they’d tried big gyms and felt lost, rushed or judged.

So the studio stays small on purpose. There are never more than four people in a group, every session is booked, and there’s always a coach on the floor. It’s quiet before 7 AM, buzzing after 6 PM and stroller-friendly all day.

Today we coach around 120 people a week, in the studio and online: nurses and soldiers on rotating shifts, parents fitting training around daycare drop-offs, climbers, runners and retirees learning to deadlift.

The coaches

Meet Ffion

Founder & head coach

Ffion Beckett

Ffion grew up in Wales playing field hockey, moved to Colorado Springs for college and never left. She spent eight years coaching in big commercial gyms before opening Foothill in 2019, so she could give every client the time she’d always wanted to.

She specializes in strength for beginners, sustainable fat loss and returning to training after pregnancy. Expect clear explanations, a lot of encouragement and absolutely no shouting.

Our approach

Four things we
won’t compromise on

01

Strength is for everyone

Whatever your age, size or history, getting stronger makes daily life easier. We start where you are and build from there.

02

Progress you can measure

We track lifts, times, habits and how you feel, so you can see change happening, even on weeks when it doesn’t feel like it.

03

Life comes first

Shift work, a new baby, finals week: plans change. Your program flexes with you instead of making you feel guilty.

04

Honest about our limits

We’re coaches, not doctors or physical therapists. If something needs a professional, we’ll say so and work alongside them.
