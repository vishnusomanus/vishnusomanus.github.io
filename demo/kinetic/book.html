Before you train: seven quick questions

A simple pre-exercise check we ask everyone. Answering yes doesn’t mean you can’t train with us; it means we’ll talk first. Your answers are only used to keep you safe.

  1. 1.Has a doctor ever told you that you have a heart condition, or that you should only do exercise a doctor recommends?

  2. 2.Do you get pain or discomfort in your chest when you’re active, or at rest?

  3. 3.Do you lose your balance because of dizziness, or have you fainted in the past 12 months?

  4. 4.Are you taking prescribed medication for blood pressure, a heart condition or another long-term condition?

  5. 5.Do you have a bone, joint or muscle problem that could be made worse by exercise?

  6. 6.Are you pregnant, or have you had a baby in the past 12 months?

  7. 7.Do you know of any other reason you shouldn’t do physical activity?
