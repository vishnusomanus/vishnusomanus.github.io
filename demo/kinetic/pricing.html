FAQ

Questions,
answered

Anything else? Send us a message or call (719) 555-0146.

Is there a joining fee or contract?

No joining fee. Memberships run month to month with 30 days’ notice to cancel, and packs have no commitment beyond using them within 12 weeks.

What happens if I need to cancel a session?

Cancel or move a session up to 24 hours before at no cost. Inside 24 hours the session is charged, but we’ll always try to move you to another time that week first.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes: up to four weeks a year for vacations, illness or busy spells. Just message your coach before your next billing date.

Can I share a pack with a partner?

Packs are personal, but partner training is designed for two: one 60-minute session for $124 in total, or ask about a shared partner membership.

I’m pregnant or have a health condition. Can I still train?

Often, yes, with the right go-ahead. Talk to your doctor or midwife before starting a new program, then book a free consult so we can plan around their advice. We’re not medical professionals and won’t give medical advice.

How do I pay?

Card or Apple Pay at the studio for single sessions and packs, and monthly autopay for memberships. Online coaching is billed monthly by card.

Do you offer student or military discounts?

Yes: 10% off memberships for students, healthcare workers, first responders, active-duty military and veterans with a valid ID.

The small print

  • Prices don’t include any sales tax that applies.
  • Sessions moved or canceled with less than 24 hours’ notice are charged.
  • Packs are valid for 12 weeks from your first session and can’t be refunded once started.
  • Memberships renew monthly; give 30 days’ notice to cancel.
  • Kickstart is for new clients, one per person, used within three weeks.
  • Everyone completes a short health questionnaire before training.
