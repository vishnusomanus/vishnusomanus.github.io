  • Leaving a job

    Severance agreement review

    Your employer wants you to sign an agreement ending your employment, or settling a dispute, in return for a payment. Once you sign, you’ve usually given up the right to sue, so it’s worth having an attorney read it first.

    What’s included

    • Every clause explained on a call, in plain English
    • Check of the payment, notice pay, vacation pay and tax treatment
    • Changes to the reference, announcement and confidentiality wording where needed
    • A final read of the revised agreement before you sign
    Typical timeline
    2–5 business days
    Fee
    $750 Fixed. Some employers will contribute.
