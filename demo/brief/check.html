      Deadline calculator

      How long do I have to make a claim?

      In Ohio, most federal discrimination and retaliation claims start with an EEOC charge filed within 300 days. You normally have to file that charge before you can sue, and other claims have their own limits.

      • Fired on March 15? The usual last day is January 9.
      • Ohio Civil Rights Commission charges have two years.
      • Waiting for an internal appeal doesn’t usually stop the clock.
