A key ceremony, start to finish

Slow on purpose. Done once, properly.

A key ceremony is the afternoon your vault is born. We bring the checklists and the patience; you bring sealed devices bought new from the maker. Nothing secret ever leaves the room.

Plan your signers

  1. Step 1 · Before

    A map, not a shopping list

    We agree who signs, where each key will live and what happens if someone can’t be reached.

  2. Step 2 · The table

    Open, check, generate

    Seals checked, devices verified, phrases generated and written by you. We watch the process, never the words.

  3. Step 3 · Rehearsal

    Sign, restore, sign again

    A small test deposit, a signature with every pair of keys and a full restore from backup before real funds arrive.

  4. Step 4 · After

    Written down, checked yearly

    You leave with a vault map and a letter of instruction draft. We check in each quarter with a drill reminder.
