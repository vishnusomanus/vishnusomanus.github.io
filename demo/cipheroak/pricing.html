    Questions about fees

    Why don’t you charge a percentage?

    Because knowing what you hold makes you a target, including to us. A fixed fee means we never need to ask.

    Which devices should I buy?

    We will send a short, maker-neutral list before your session, and we always suggest at least two different makers in a vault. We take no commissions.

    Is the care plan required?

    No. It is a yearly review and quarterly drill reminders. Many clients keep it for the first two years, then book reviews when life changes.

    Can you help with taxes or what to invest in?

    No. We work on security only. For tax and investment questions, speak to a licensed professional; we are glad to work alongside them.
