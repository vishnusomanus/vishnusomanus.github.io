## [1.0.4] - 2026-11-07

### Changed

- American wording throughout, including US terms for checkboxes, business days, schedules and units.

### Changed

- The sample copy uses American spelling, as the template is set in the US.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-04

### Fixed

- A change you make is kept for the visit when the browser can't save it, for example when its storage is full, instead of going back to the old value.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added

- Nine HTML pages for Cipheroak, a self-custody security studio in Santa Fe, New Mexico.
- Ten-question vault-readiness assessment with readiness levels, section scores and prioritized recommendations.
- Multisig signer planner with a key ring diagram and loss, theft, site and absent-signer checks.
- Fourteen illustrated security guides with filters, search, shareable links and a saved reading list.
- Quote builder with devices, signers, recovery plans, ceremony travel and a care plan.
- Field notes and advisories that roll forward in whole weeks, with type filters and severity badges.
- Review-call booking in Mountain Time with US holidays, taken slots, local-time display and a recovery-phrase guard.
- Studio hours with live status and coming closures, SVG artwork throughout and full documentation.
