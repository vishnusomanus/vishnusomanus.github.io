      Five bad days

        How to read the numbers

        Keys − threshold

        How many you can lose

        With three keys and a threshold of two, one key can be lost, broken or forgotten and the vault still opens. With two of two, none can.

        Threshold − 1

        How many a thief can take

        A thief needs as many keys as you do. With a threshold of three, two stolen keys are still useless, as long as you notice and move funds.

        Places and people

        Why location matters

        Two keys in one safe count as one place. A fire there is two losses, and a break-in is two thefts. Spread keys across buildings and people.

        Team vault engagements

        Roles before devices.

        Most team losses we see are not hacks. They are a signer who left, a policy nobody wrote down, or an urgent message that skipped a step. We start there.
