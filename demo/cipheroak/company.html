Principles

Six promises we put in every engagement letter

  1. I

    We never see your phrase

    You generate it and write it. We look away, literally, at the table.

  2. II

    We never hold a key

    Not as a backup, not as a third signer. A security company that holds keys becomes the target.

  3. III

    No commissions

    We take nothing from device makers, banks or anyone else we might recommend.

  4. IV

    No financial advice

    We won’t tell you what to buy, sell or hold. Our work ends where the security does.

  5. V

    Written down and rehearsed

    A plan nobody has practiced is a hope. Every setup ends with a drill.

  6. VI

    Plain language

    If your executor can’t follow it, it isn’t finished. We rewrite until they can.
