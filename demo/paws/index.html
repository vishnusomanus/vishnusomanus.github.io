Emergencies

Worried right now? Call, don’t book.

Online requests are for routine visits. If your pet shows any of these signs, call us or the nearest emergency vet straight away. They are examples, not a full list: if you’re unsure, call.

  • Struggling to breathe or blue gums
  • Collapse, seizures or can’t stand
  • May have eaten something toxic
  • Swollen, hard belly or retching
  • Heavy bleeding or a serious injury
  • Straining to pee with nothing coming
  • A rabbit that stops eating or pooping
  • Hit by a car, even if they seem fine

What to do

If we’re open, call (828) 555-0163 and we’ll tell you whether to come straight in. Outside our hours, call the nearest 24-hour emergency vet now: don’t wait for us to open.

What we do

Everyday care, done properly

Most visits are check-ups, vaccines and the occasional upset tummy. We also handle dental cleanings and routine surgery in-house, so your pet sees faces they know.

  • Wellness check-upsA nose-to-tail exam, weight check and a plan for the year ahead.
  • VaccinesCore and lifestyle vaccines on a schedule that fits your pet.
  • Dental careAwake dental checks, plus cleanings under anesthesia when needed.
  • SurgerySpays, neuters and lump removals with monitored anesthesia.
  • Rabbits & exoticsSeen by Dr. Castellanos, who has kept rabbits since she was six.
  • Senior petsTwice-yearly visits, bloodwork and comfort care for older friends.
