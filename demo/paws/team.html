Our team

Small team, big hearts, lots of dog hair

Three vets, two licensed veterinary technicians and a client care lead. You’ll see the same faces visit after visit, and so will your pet.

Technicians & client care

Licensed veterinary technicians (LVTs) are the vet nurses of the US: they handle anesthesia, dental X-rays, lab work and a lot of reassuring cuddles.

  • Quentin Galloway, LVT

    Lead veterinary technician

    Quentin monitors every anesthetic and runs our Saturday puppy socials. Ask him about nail trims: he has a trick involving peanut butter.

  • Dolores Bettencourt, LVT

    Dental & surgical technician

    Dolores takes the dental X-rays and sits with pets as they wake up. Twenty-two years in practice and still knits recovery blankets.

  • Keiko Haverford

    Client care lead

    Keiko answers the phone, sorts records transfers and knows most of our patients by the sound of their bark.

Ziggy

Head of reception (self-appointed)

Ziggy arrived as a stray kitten in 2019 and never left. He supervises the front desk from the windowsill and is not allowed in exam rooms, which he finds deeply unfair. Allergic or nervous about cats? Tell us and he’ll go to his office.

How we work

  • Unhurried visits

    Check-ups are 30 minutes, senior visits an hour. Time to examine properly and answer every question.

  • Estimates first

    You get a written estimate before tests or treatment, and we call before going over it.

  • Low-stress handling

    Treats, non-slip mats, cat-only hours and pheromone diffusers. No pinning, no rushing.
