After hours

If your pet needs urgent care while we’re closed, call the nearest 24-hour emergency vet now. Don’t wait for us to open or send a message: messages aren’t read out of hours.

  • Call ahead so the emergency team is ready for you.
  • Bring any medicines, packets or plants your pet may have eaten.
  • Ask them to send us a report; we’ll call you the next day we’re open.
