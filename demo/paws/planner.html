          The basics

          Core vaccines at a glance

          “Core” vaccines are recommended for every pet of that species. Others depend on lifestyle. In North Carolina, dogs, cats and ferrets must be vaccinated against rabies by four months of age.

          Questions

          Vaccine questions we hear a lot

          Still unsure? Call (828) 555-0163 or ask at your next visit.

          My pet missed a booster. Do we start again?

          Usually not. Most pets just need the missed dose, but some vaccines need two doses again if it’s been a long time. Bring any records and we’ll work it out.

          Are vaccine reactions common?

          Mild tiredness or a sore spot for a day is normal. Facial swelling, vomiting or trouble breathing are not: call us or the nearest emergency vet straight away.

          Can my puppy go outside before the last shots?

          Your own yard and carrying them around town is great for socializing. Skip dog parks and busy trails until a week or two after the final puppy vaccines.

          Does an indoor cat need vaccines?

          Yes. Rabies is required by law, and FVRCP viruses can come in on shoes and clothes. Indoor-only adults may not need FeLV boosters; we’ll talk it through.
