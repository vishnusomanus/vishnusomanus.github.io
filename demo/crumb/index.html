    How preordering works

    No line, no disappointment, no 6am alarm.

    1. 1

      Fill your bag by 2pm

      Choose loaves, pastries and cakes. Some breads only come out on certain days, and we’ll show you which.

    2. 2

      Choose a pickup slot

      Any half hour while we’re open, up to two weeks ahead. Pay online now or at the counter.

      Grab it off the shelf

      Your bag waits by the door with your name on it. Say hello, skip the line, add a coffee.
