    Good to know

    Cake questions

    Anything else? Email cakes@brambleandrye.example.

    Can you make it gluten-free?+

    We can bake a cake without gluten (the same buckwheat base as our brownies), but our kitchen handles wheat all day, so it isn’t suitable for anyone with celiac disease. Add a note at checkout.

    How should I store it?+

    Somewhere cool, out of the sun, and in the fridge if it has fresh fruit or mascarpone. Take it out an hour before serving so the buttercream softens.

    Can I change my order?+

    Up to 48 hours before pickup, yes: call or email and we’ll sort it. After that the layers are baked and we can only change the message.

    Do you deliver cakes?+

    Two- and three-tier cakes can be delivered and set up within 10 miles of the shop for $45. Single tiers travel happily on a car seat.
