Allergens and dietary needs

Each bake lists the main allergens it contains. We bake everything in one small kitchen with flour in the air, so we can’t promise anything is free from traces of gluten, nuts or sesame. If you have an allergy, call us at (518) 555-0129 before ordering and we’ll talk you through it.
