    FAQ

    Before you visit

    Can I order for today?+

    Online preorders are for the next day onwards (order by 2pm). For today, just stop in: the shelves are full until late morning, and we keep a few loaves back for latecomers.

    What if I’m late to pick up?+

    No problem: your bag waits until we close. If you can’t make it at all, call us and we’ll hold it for the next morning or pass it on to the Pantry.

    Do you take café bookings?+

    Only for groups of six or more on weekdays. Weekends are first come, first served.

    Do you deliver?+

    To wholesale customers and for tiered celebration cakes. Everything else is order-ahead pickup, which keeps our bakes fresh and our prices fair.
