FAQ

Good questions

The things travelers ask most before booking. Anything else, just ask.

How fit do I need to be?

Each trip has an activity level. Easy trips involve short walks and lots of time on your feet in towns. Moderate means walks of three to six hours on some days. Challenging trips, like the Inca Trail and Toubkal, need real preparation; we send a training plan when you book.

Can I travel on my own?

Yes, and about 40% of our travelers do. We pair solo travelers of the same gender in twin rooms at no extra cost, or you can choose your own room for a supplement shown on each trip page.

What happens if a trip doesn’t fill?

Trips run with four or more travelers. If a departure isn’t guaranteed eight weeks before it leaves, we offer you another date, another trip or a full refund. Departures marked “Guaranteed” will run.

How do deposits and payments work?

A deposit of $400 per person holds your place. The balance is due eight weeks before departure. Your payments are held in a trust account until you travel.

Are flights included?

No. Most travelers fly from different places, so we leave flights to you, but we suggest routes and timings and can recommend an agent. Airport transfers on the first and last days are included.

Can you arrange a private trip?

Any trip can run privately for four or more people on dates that suit you, with the same guides and stays. Tell us your group and month using the form above.
