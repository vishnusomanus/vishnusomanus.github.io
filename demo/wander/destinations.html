Mountains, medinas and the deep desert

Morocco & the Sahara

Snow on the High Atlas, the red walls of Marrakech and the dunes of Erg Chebbi, all within a few days’ drive. Spring and fall are warm by day and cool at night; summer in the desert is fierce.

  • A night in a Saharan camp
  • Cooking in a Berber home
  • The 9,000 lanes of the Fes medina
  • Summit of Toubkal, 13,671 ft

Inca trails and high valleys

Peru & the Andes

The dry season from May to September brings clear skies over the Sacred Valley and the Inca Trail. February is the wettest month, and the trail itself closes for maintenance.

  • The classic Inca Trail
  • Weaving cooperatives in Chinchero
  • Lima’s great kitchens
  • Machu Picchu at sunrise

Midnight sun and northern lights

Norway & the Arctic

Two seasons, two trips. Summer brings 24-hour daylight for walking the Lofoten ridges; the dark months from November to March are for the aurora, huskies and whales.

  • Northern lights above Tromsø
  • Ridge walks in the Lofoten
  • Orca and humpback whales
  • Red fishermen’s cabins

Islands, coves and the coastal path

The Aegean

Greek islands by wooden boat and Turkey’s Lycian Way on foot. Late spring and September have warm seas without the August crowds and heat.

  • Sailing the Cyclades
  • The white rocks of Sarakiniko
  • Walking the Lycian Way
  • Sunken city of Kekova

Pilgrim trails and night markets

Japan

Cherry blossom from late March and the red maples of November are the classic times. June is the rainy season and high summer is hot and humid, though never dull.

  • The Kumano Kodo pilgrim trail
  • Onsen ryokan with a view of Fuji
  • Kyoto before the crowds
  • Tokyo’s izakaya alleys

The great migration and spice islands

Tanzania & Zanzibar

The dry season from July to October is best for game drives, with the herds crossing the northern Serengeti. February is calving season in the south. April and May bring the long rains.

  • The Serengeti migration
  • Ngorongoro Crater at dawn
  • Stone Town’s night market
  • Dhow sails off Zanzibar
