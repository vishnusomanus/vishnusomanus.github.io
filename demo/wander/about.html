Our story

Fifteen years, the same rules

Clara Hughes and Sam Okoro met leading bus tours and agreed on one thing: the best moments never happened on the bus. Saltwind is what they built instead.

  1. 2011

    One van in Morocco

    Clara and Sam take twelve friends of friends from Flagstaff to the Sahara, with Hassan driving and cooking.

  2. 2014

    Peru and the Andes

    Rosa joins and we start running the Inca Trail with porters paid above the legal minimum.

  3. 2018

    B Corp-style rules, written down

    We publish our standards on group size, pay and carbon, and start removing carbon for every trip.

  4. 2022

    Japan, Norway and Tanzania

    Three new regions, each with a lead guide who lives there all year.

  5. Today

    38 guides, 6 regions

    Still twelve travelers or fewer, still run from a small office by Flagstaff’s rail depot.

Responsible travel

Promises we can be held to

Fine words are easy. These are commitments with numbers attached, and we report on them every year.

  • Small groups, always

    Never more than twelve travelers, so we fit in family homes and small boats and don’t overwhelm the places we visit.

  • Fair pay, all year

    Guides are employed year-round, and porters and crews are paid at least 30% above local guidelines, checked by a third party.

  • Carbon removed, not offset

    We pay for verified carbon removal for every trip, flights included, and publish the receipts.

  • Locally owned stays

    92% of our nights are spent in locally owned riads, guesthouses, ryokan and camps.

  • A fund run by guides

    $35 from every booking goes to a community fund. Our guides choose the projects: schools, water, trail repair.

  • Animals stay wild

    No elephant rides, no captive wildlife, and every safari follows strict viewing distances.
