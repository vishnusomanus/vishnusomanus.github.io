# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-06

### Changed

- Wardline's copy uses American wording throughout: 12-hour times in the evidence mockup and "departed employees" instead of "leavers".
- The "Show times in" list offers Alaska and Hawaii in place of London and Central Europe, and every subprocessor is in the United States. Visitors elsewhere still see times in their own zone.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- Ten HTML pages for Wardline, a compliance automation company in Richmond, Virginia.
- Hero control dashboard that switches between frameworks, and a six-tab platform tour with its own product mockup for each tool.
- Framework explorer with control families and cross-mapping between SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS and NIST CSF, computed from an 81-control library, plus a pairwise overlap matrix.
- Readiness assessment with a score, ranked gaps and an estimated preparation range, saved in the browser.
- Pricing calculator with three plans, add-ons, monthly or annual billing and an itemized estimate.
- Integrations directory with search, category filters, sorting and saved filters.
- Trust center with control status, subprocessors, rolling report dates and a document request flow with NDA and work-email checks.
- Customer stories with generated before-and-after charts, a three-step demo booking in the visitor's time zone, and full documentation.
