How it works

Connect, monitor, fix, audit. Then keep going.

Compliance isn't a project with a finish line. The same tests that get you ready for a first audit keep running afterward, so the second year is a normal week, not a scramble.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Link your cloud accounts, identity provider, HR system, code host and device management with read-only access. Most teams are connected in an afternoon.

  2. 02

    Monitor

    Wardline tests every mapped control each hour and records the result with a timestamp, so evidence builds up while you work.

  3. 03

    Fix

    A failing test opens a ticket for the right owner, with the setting to change and the deadline from your own policy.

  4. 04

    Audit

    Give your auditor a read-only workspace with every control, test and document in one place. They do the audit; you skip the screenshot hunt.

    Who does what

    Clear about where Wardline stops and your auditor starts.

    Wardline does

    • Tests your controls continuously and keeps dated evidence
    • Gives you policy templates to adapt, approve and track
    • Opens tickets for failing controls and follows them to closure
    • Gives your auditor organized, read-only access

    Your independent auditor does

    • Decides whether your controls meet the criteria
    • Issues the SOC 2 report (a licensed CPA firm)
    • Issues the ISO 27001 certificate (an accredited certification body)
    • Sets the audit scope, sampling and timeline with you

    Wardline does not provide legal advice or guarantee an audit outcome.
