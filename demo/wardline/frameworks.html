    Who gives you what, and for how long

    Wardline gets you ready and keeps you ready. It never issues a report or certificate, and a vendor that says it does is worth a second look.

    What each framework produces and who issues it
    Framework What you get From Renewal
    SOC 2 Type I or Type II attestation report A licensed CPA firm Usually every 12 months
    ISO 27001 Certificate An accredited certification body 3 years, surveillance audits yearly
    HIPAA Risk analysis and documented safeguards You, often with an independent assessor Review at least yearly
    GDPR Records that show compliance You; supervisory authorities enforce Ongoing
    PCI DSS Report on Compliance or Self-Assessment Questionnaire A Qualified Security Assessor, or you Every 12 months
    NIST CSF Current and target profiles You, or an outside reviewer Voluntary
