    Method

    How the score and range are worked out

    Five areas carry the score, 20 points each: cloud setup, written policies, sign-in and MFA, logging and vendor reviews. They are the areas where first audits most often find gaps.

    Each answer also adds weeks of work. The range starts from a typical preparation time for your target framework, adds those weeks (50% more at the top of the range), and scales for company size: 1.15× for 51–200 people, 1.35× for 201–1,000 and 1.6× above that.

    Gaps are ranked by points missing. High priority means 10 or more points missing in an area.
