Hi, I’m Delia.

AboutArchitect turned agent

I used to design houses. Now I help people choose them.

For five years I drew additions and kitchens for Nashville families. Again and again, clients came to me after they’d bought, asking how to fix a house that was never going to work: the wall that couldn’t come out, the basement that would always be damp.

So in 2015 I got my license to help people earlier, before they sign. I still tour every house with a tape measure and a sketchbook, and I still tell sellers which twenty-dollar fix will earn them five thousand.

I work alone, on purpose. When you call, you get me.

TimelineHow I got here

From drafting table to closing table

  1. 2009

    Five years of studio, a thesis on the Nashville shotgun house, and a lasting habit of sketching every porch I walk past.

  2. 2010

    Joined a small Nashville firm designing additions and renovations, mostly in East Nashville and Sylvan Park. Learned what clients really pay for.

  3. 2015

PrinciplesHow I work

Three promises I make every client

  • Honest pricing

    I price from the comps, not from hope. If your number is too high, I’ll show you why on paper before we list.

  • An architect’s eye

    Structure, light, layout and what it would cost to change them. You get a written walkthrough of every house we seriously consider.

  • One person, start to finish

    No assistants showing your home, no hand-offs at the negotiating table. You have my cell number from day one.
