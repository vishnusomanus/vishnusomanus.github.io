    How I sell a home

    1. 01

      Week 1

      Walkthrough and pricing

      I walk the house with you like an architect would: what to fix, what to leave, and a price built from the last 90 days of sales.

    2. 02

      Week 2

      Prepare and stage

      My touch-up crew handles paint, lighting and the little repairs. A stager rearranges what you own before we rent anything.

    3. 03

      Week 3

      Launch

      Photos, a measured floor plan and a Thursday launch, so the first weekend of showings is full.

    4. 04

      Week 4

      Negotiate and close

      I compare offers on net, not headline price, then manage inspection, appraisal and closing day.
