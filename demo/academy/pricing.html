Pricing questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your account at any time; we prorate the difference.

Do you offer a student discount?

Students and teachers get 50% off Plus with a school email address. Contact us with your school and we’ll set it up.

What happens to my certificates if I cancel?

They’re yours forever. You keep every certificate you earned, and free courses stay free.

How do team seats work?

Buy seats for your team and assign them from the admin dashboard. Reassign a seat whenever someone leaves.
