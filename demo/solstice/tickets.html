Good to know

Children

Kids under 5 are free and don’t need a ticket. Ages 5–15 need a child day ticket ($30) at the gate, or come on a Family weekend ticket.

Can’t make it?

Tickets are non-refundable, but you can transfer them to a friend for free until June 1, 2027 through your account.

Access tickets

Disabled visitors can bring a companion for free. Tell us what you need on the contact form.
