Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I bring my own food and drink?

Yes, a picnic is welcome in the arena. You can bring up to half a gallon of nonalcoholic drinks per person in plastic or cans. Glass and alcohol are not allowed in the arena; campers 21 or over can bring a little beer or wine to the campsite.

What age do children need a ticket?

Kids under 5 go free and don’t need a ticket. Children aged 5–15 need a child ticket or a Family weekend ticket and must be with an adult at all times.

Is the site accessible?

Main routes have ground matting, every stage has a raised viewing platform and there are accessible restrooms in every restroom area. Companions go free with a disabled ticket holder.

What happens if it rains?

The festival goes ahead, it’s northern Michigan in June. The Orchard and Firefly Tent are covered, and the Long Table has a big-top dining tent. Bring rain boots just in case.
