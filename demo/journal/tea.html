Craft

The pot on the work table

Nila Koroth · 6 min

The pot is not a ceremony. It is how the table becomes a place where work can be discussed without a screen in the middle of it. Someone fills it before the first conversation. If the pot is empty, the conversation has gone on too long or not started yet.

The tea is ordinary and the pot is not. It has been mended at the handle. The mend is wrapped cane, done by a person who does not work here anymore and is still spoken of as if they might walk in and fix the next thing. The cup is a different clay from the pot. Nobody remembers this as a decision.

We came to write about a workshop and stayed for the way the pot organized the hour. Visitors who wanted a tour were given a cup first. The tour was shorter afterward, which seemed to be the aim. The good work was on the bench, and it did not improve by being explained at length.

The photograph is the pot before the second pouring. After that the light had moved and the tray looked like a tray again, which is to say it looked like nothing we had come to see.
