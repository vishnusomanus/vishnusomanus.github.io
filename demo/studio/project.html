Wakeline

A calmer identity for the lake ferries

Year
2025
Discipline
Identity, Wayfinding
Sector
Public transport, Excelsior
Duration
14 months

Overview

Wakeline runs eleven passenger ferries on Lake Minnetonka. We rebuilt its identity around a single ring-and-wave mark and a schedule system that works on a phone, a pier and a paper fold-out.

  • Identity system
  • Wayfinding
  • Schedule design
  • Livery

The challenge

Wakeline had grown out of three older operators, and it showed: four logos, two typefaces on the same pier and schedules that changed format from boat to boat. Tourists missed the last ferry home; regulars ignored the signs altogether. The brief was to make one ferry company out of three, without losing the warmth people felt for the old boats.

Approach

  1. 01

    Audit every touchpoint

    We photographed all 23 piers and catalogd 412 signs, tickets and notices. Two thirds carried information that was already out of date.

  2. 02

    One mark, one grid

    The ring-and-wave mark reads at a third of an inch on a ticket and at ten feet on a bow. Everything else sits on a single eight-column grid shared by print, signs and screens.

  3. 03

    Schedules first

    We designed the departure board before the logo. Times are set large in tabular figures, with the last boat of the day marked in buoy orange.
