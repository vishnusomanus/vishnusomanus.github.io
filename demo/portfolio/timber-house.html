Private house · 2025

Timber house at dusk

A two-storey timber house photographed in one evening, from the lawn up into the rooms that face the garden.

The owners asked for the house at the hour they actually live in it: dinner on, shoes by the door, no styling beyond what was already there. We waited until the sky dropped and the interior lamps were warmer than the grass.

Most of the frames are from the garden, looking back through the glass. A few were made inside, always with the window in the picture, so the rooms stay attached to the trees.

Twelve photographs were delivered for the architect’s record and a smaller set for the family. The print edition is six images, made on fibre paper in the Oslo darkroom.
