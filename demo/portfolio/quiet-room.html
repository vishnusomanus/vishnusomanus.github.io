Still life · object book

The quiet room

A chapter of still photographs for a book about rooms that are used every day and rarely shown.

The publisher wanted objects, not a renovation story. We photographed the sofa, the clock, the chair by the window, and the cloth left on the arm, and we did not move them into a better arrangement.

The pictures are paired in the book with a short note from the person who lives there. Nothing in the frame was brought in for the camera.
