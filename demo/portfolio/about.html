The studio

A small practice on Markveien.

Atelier North is the studio of Sigrid Holm. The work is houses, portraits, and long looks at weather. Assignments come from architects, families, and the occasional magazine that still prints photographs large.

The studio has been in Grünerløkka since 2016. Film is processed down the street. Digital files are delivered as a sequenced set, not a dump of the day.

Sigrid takes a limited number of commissions each season and spends part of the winter on the coastal series, which is not for sale until the book is finished.
