Private commission · Bergen

Studio portraits

Three people, one rented room in Bergen, and a north window. No lights except the day.

The sitting was booked as a family record and became something quieter. Each person had forty minutes. We started with the chair they chose and stopped when the pose fell away.

Film for the formal frames, a digital body for the pictures made while the film was being changed. The contact sheets are part of the delivery.

Prints are 30 by 40 centimetres, signed on the verso. The family kept the full set. Two frames from the day are in the studio archive.
