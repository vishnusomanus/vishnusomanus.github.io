About us

One family. Six bays. Since 1994.

Neville Mottram opened the doors with one lift and a borrowed compressor. His daughter Bethan runs it now, the same way: fixed prices, straight answers, and the old parts in a bag if you want them.

How we work

The rules on the shop wall

  1. 01

    Price first

    Every job gets a written price before a wrench comes out. The website prices are the prices.

  2. 02

    Call, then fix

    Found something else? You get a call, a photo and a fixed price. No answer, no extra work.

  3. 03

    Show the part

    Old parts go back in your trunk in a labeled bag, unless you tell us to recycle them.

  4. 04

    Fix, don’t swap

    If a weld or a single part does the job safely, that’s what we quote, not the whole assembly.

Service history

The logbook

  1. 1994

    Neville rents one bay in Spinners Yard, a former woolen mill. One lift, one borrowed compressor, a hand-painted sign.

  2. 2003

    The inspection lane opens after a year of paperwork. Neville inspects every car himself for the first five years.

  3. 2011

    We take over Unit 4 next door: six bays, a customer lounge and the first loaner car.

  4. 2016

    Bethan becomes owner. Fixed prices go on the wall and on the website, and they stay there.

  5. 2021

    Ade and Bethan complete high-voltage training; the yard gets a charger and an insulated EV bay.
