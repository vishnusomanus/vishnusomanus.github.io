Safety inspections

How much is a safety inspection?

$45 for any car, SUV or light truck. Booked with any service it’s $35. If something fails, the reinspection is free within 10 business days.

How early can I get my inspection done?

Any time during the month it’s due, and your next one stays in that same month. Our inspection date checker works out the dates for you.

What happens if something fails?

Kieran shows you what failed and why, and we give you a fixed price for the repair. If we fix it here, the reinspection is free. You’re free to take it elsewhere, of course.

Does New Hampshire still require an inspection?

New Hampshire’s inspection rules have been changing and can differ by vehicle type, so check with the NH Division of Motor Vehicles for what applies to yours. Either way, drivers are expected to keep a vehicle in safe working condition, and we think a yearly look is worth it. This is general information, not legal advice.

Maintenance and prices

Will maintenance with you affect my manufacturer warranty?

Generally, no. You can usually have routine maintenance done at an independent shop as long as the work follows the manufacturer’s schedule and uses parts that meet its specifications. We do both, and keep your service record. Your warranty booklet has the details.

Is the price checker price what I’ll pay?

For most cars, yes. It’s a guide price that covers standard parts and labor. If your car needs something unusual (special oil, more spark plugs), we tell you the fixed price when we confirm the booking.

What’s the difference between basic, full and major services?

A basic service is an oil change and safety check for high-mileage drivers. A full service is the yearly one, adding filters and a road test. A major service adds spark plugs and brake fluid every second year.

What if you find something else wrong?

We stop and call you with a photo and a fixed price. Nothing extra is done without your OK, and you never pay for work you didn’t agree to.

Booking and loaner cars

How do I get a loaner car?

Check “Loaner car” when you book. We have two, free of charge, for drivers aged 21 or over with a valid driver’s license. The booking form shows how many are left on each day.

What time do I drop off and pick up?

Drop-offs are from 7:30am on weekdays and 8am on Saturdays. Most cars are ready the same afternoon; we text you when yours is.

Can I wait while you work?

Yes, for short jobs: an inspection on its own, a basic service or a diagnostic check. The lounge has Wi-Fi, a coffee machine and a very comfortable couch.

Are you open on Sundays or holidays?

No. We’re open Monday to Friday 7:30am to 6pm and Saturday 8am to 1pm, and closed on federal holidays. The booking calendar skips closed days for you.

Electric and hybrid cars

Do you work on electric cars?

Yes. Ade and Bethan are trained to work on high-voltage systems, and we have an insulated EV bay. We service, inspect and repair most electric cars and hybrids.

Do electric cars need maintenance?

Less often, but yes: brakes, suspension, cooling, cabin filters and tires all wear. Most makers recommend a yearly check, so see what your battery warranty asks for.

Paying

When and how do I pay?

When you pick up: card, tap to pay or check. No deposits for routine work.

Do you give a warranty?

Every repair has a 12-month parts and labor warranty, whatever the mileage.
