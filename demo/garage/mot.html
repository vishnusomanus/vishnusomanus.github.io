    How our dates work

    Three things worth knowing

    • 1 yr

      A year, to month end

      We work on a yearly cycle, through the end of the month: inspected on November 3, you’re good through November 30 next year.

    • 1 mth

      Come early, keep the date

      Come in any time during the month it’s due and your next one stays in that same month.

    • 10 days

      Just bought it?

      We suggest a first inspection within 10 days of registering a vehicle that’s new to you, then every year.

    New Hampshire’s inspection rules have been changing and can differ by vehicle type, so check with the NH Division of Motor Vehicles for what applies to yours. This is general information, not legal advice.
