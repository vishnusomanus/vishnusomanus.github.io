Let’s make a plan

Different teams, shared direction

There’s more than
one way to plan.

Start with the conversation your team needs. Keep the useful structure and leave the ceremony behind.

Before you start

Name the decision.

Are you deciding what to discover, build or release? Agree on that first, then choose the smallest useful planning view.

Bring the right people

Make the trade-off together.

Invite people who know the customer, system and constraints. A shared assumption is easier to challenge than a hidden one.

Keep it useful

Review, don’t recreate.

Keep a short cadence. Update the evidence, move the work and leave the discussion in the same place for next time.

Larkgrove Health

Three planning meetings became one.

The product team kept priorities in a spreadsheet that only two people edited. They moved the backlog in an afternoon, scored the top twenty initiatives together and now run a single 30-minute review each Wednesday.
