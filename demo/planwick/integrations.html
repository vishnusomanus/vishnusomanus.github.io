    How we take care of your data

    Read-only by default

    Most connections only read. The three that write (team chat, shared calendar and webhooks) only post where you choose.

    Scoped to what you pick

    Choose the projects, channels and folders to share. Everything else stays out of Planwick.

    Revoke in one step

    A workspace owner can pause or remove any connection. Imported evidence stays, the live link stops.

    Every sync logged

    The connection log shows what arrived, when and from where, kept for twelve months.
