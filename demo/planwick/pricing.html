Who needs a planning seat?

People who create, edit and prioritize work. People who only read a roadmap can use a shared view without a paid seat.

Can we change our team size?

We review seat changes with you before the next billing period. Annual agreements keep the agreed price for the year.

How does the annual saving work?

Annual seats cost 20% less than twelve monthly payments. Optional packs keep their monthly equivalent price.
