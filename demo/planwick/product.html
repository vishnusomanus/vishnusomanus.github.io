Useful boundaries

A roadmap is a conversation.
Not a promise machine.

Separate an idea from a commitment. Keep the reason beside the work. Review the plan when the evidence changes.

Open the roadmap workspace
Can a small team start without a process?

Start with Now, Next and Later. Put a handful of initiatives in each and agree on which problem matters most. Add scoring when it helps.

Does every idea need a date?

No. The roadmap shows direction. The capacity plan gives selected initiatives an estimated release week. Keep uncommitted ideas in Later.

Who owns the final priority?

Your team does. Planwick makes the inputs visible. A score is useful evidence, not an instruction.

Can we bring our existing backlog?

Yes. Import a spreadsheet or connect your issue tracker. We match identifiers, owners and stages before anything is created.

Who can see our roadmap?

Only the people you invite. Planning seats can edit; anyone else you share a view with can read it without a paid seat.

How long does setup take?

Most teams have a working roadmap in an afternoon. Team and Scale plans include a guided setup call with one of us.
