The 25-minute
roadmap review.

  1. 5 minutes / What changed?

    New evidence, capacity or commitments since the last review.

  2. 10 minutes / What matters now?

    Revisit the top priorities and the reason for each one.

  3. 10 minutes / What will we do?

    Agree on the next decision, owner and check-in. Leave the plan clearer than you found it.
