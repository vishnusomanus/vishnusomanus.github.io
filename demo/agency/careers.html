Why people stay.

  • Four-day weeks in August

    The whole studio slows down for a month, every year.

  • Sabbatical

    A paid month off after every five years.

  • Learning budget

    $2,000 a year for courses, books and conferences.

  • Hybrid by default

  • Health insurance

  • Profit share

  • Parental leave

    20 weeks fully paid for every new parent.

  • Studio lunches

    Cooked together every Wednesday in all three studios.
