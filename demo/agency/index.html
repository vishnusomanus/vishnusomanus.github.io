How we work

No black boxes. No big reveals.

Every project follows the same four phases, with a show-and-tell every Thursday so you see the work as it grows.

  1. 011–2 weeks

    Discover

    Workshops, interviews and audits to understand your business, your audience and the opportunity.

  2. 021–3 weeks

    Define

    Strategy, positioning and a clear brief everyone signs off on before a single pixel is pushed.

  3. 033–10 weeks

    Design

    Concepts, prototypes and testing with real people. We share work weekly, never in a big reveal.

  4. 042–8 weeks

    Deliver

    Build, launch and handoff, with guidelines, training and support for the first 90 days.
