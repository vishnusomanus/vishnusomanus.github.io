About the studio

Independent since day one.

We started Northbound because we wanted a studio where the people who sell the work also make it. Fourteen years later, that’s still how we run.

Our story

  1. 2012

    Founded in a Morris Avenue attic in Birmingham, Alabama, by Amara Okoye and Jonas Weller, with one client and two laptops.

  2. 2016

    Opened our Oakland studio. First product design project, for a then-tiny fintech called Halden.

  3. 2020

    Headed north with a New York studio, and went fully hybrid across all three cities.

  4. 2024

    Introduced a four-day week in August and a paid month of sabbatical every five years.

  5. 2026

    42 people, 120+ launches and still independent. Still answering our own phones.
