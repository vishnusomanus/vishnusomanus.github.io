FAQ

Billing and sales tax questions.

Something else? Ask the Grand Rapids team.

Do your prices include sales tax?

No. Prices are shown without sales tax. Whether tax applies depends on your state, and we show the exact amount before you pay. Use the switch above to see prices with a sample 6% sales tax included.

How does annual billing work?

You pay for ten months and get twelve. Solo is $120 a year instead of $144, Studio $300 instead of $360 and Practice $600 instead of $720. You can move from monthly to annual at any time and we’ll credit the unused part of your month.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged until you add a payment method. If you don’t, your account becomes read-only, so you can still open and export every invoice for as long as you need.

Does my accountant need a paid seat?

No. Every plan includes free accountant access, and accountants can manage all their clients from one login.

Will Tally file my sales tax or income tax return?

Tally prepares the figures and the reports; you or your accountant review and file them. Tally doesn’t give tax advice, so check anything you’re unsure about with a qualified tax professional.

Do you offer discounts?

Nonprofits and students in their final year get 50% off Solo and Studio. Get in touch with proof and we’ll set it up.
