Security and trust

Your numbers, kept carefully.

Invoices, bank transactions and client details are the most sensitive data a small business has. Here’s exactly how we look after them, in plain English.

How we protect it

Six things we never cut corners on.

Encrypted everywhere

TLS 1.3 between your browser and us, AES-256 for everything we store, and separate keys for backups.

Kept in the US

Your data lives in Ohio with an encrypted copy in Oregon. It isn’t moved outside the US.

Read-only bank feeds

Secure bank connections that can read transactions and nothing else. No banking passwords, ever.

Two-factor and passkeys

Authenticator apps and passkeys on every plan. Owners can require them for the whole team.

Hourly backups

Point-in-time backups every hour, kept for 35 days and restored as a test every month.

A full audit trail

Every invoice edit, payment match and export is logged with who did it and when.

Privacy

Your data is yours.

We process your clients’ details only to run Tally for you. We don’t sell data, show ads or train anything on your invoices.

  • Data processing agreement included in our terms
  • A named privacy lead in Grand Rapids
  • Export or delete everything from your settings

Responsible disclosure

Found a problem? Tell us first.

Email security@tally.example with the details. We reply within one business day, keep you updated while we fix it and credit you if you’d like.

Do you have an independent security test?

Yes. An outside firm tests the app and our infrastructure every year; the latest was in August 2026. Practice customers can ask for the summary report.

Who at Tally can see my data?

Only support staff you give permission to, for the length of a support conversation. Every access is logged and appears in your audit trail.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Your account stays read-only so you can export everything. When you ask us to delete it, it’s gone from live systems straight away and from backups within 35 days. Keep your own copy for as long as you need it for business and tax records.
