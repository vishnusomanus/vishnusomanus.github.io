Changelog

What’s new in Tally.

We ship small improvements every week and write them up here. Big ones get a note in the monthly email.

Updates

9 updates

  1. Oct 14, 2026

    New

    Deposits and part payments on quotes

    Ask for a percentage or a fixed deposit when a quote is accepted. The balance invoice is created for you with the deposit already deducted.

  2. Oct 2, 2026

    Improved

    A clearer sales tax summary

    The sales tax summary now shows sales and purchases side by side, explains every figure and links each number to the invoices behind it.

  3. Sep 24, 2026

    Fixed

    Reminders and federal holidays

    Reminders due on a federal holiday now go out the next business day instead of on the holiday itself.

  4. Sep 10, 2026

    New

    Passkeys

    Sign in with your phone’s fingerprint or face unlock. Owners can make passkeys or an authenticator app compulsory for the team.

  5. Aug 27, 2026

    Improved

    Faster bank matching

    Payments that cover several invoices are now matched in one go, and part payments are suggested when the amount doesn’t match exactly.

  6. Aug 6, 2026

    New

    Late-payment fees in reminders

    Optionally add interest and a flat late fee from your terms to a late reminder. Tally works out the figures; you decide whether to charge them.

  7. Jul 22, 2026

    Fixed

    Rounding on multi-line sales tax

    Invoices with many lines at different tax rates could be a cent off. Totals are now rounded once, per rate, as most accountants expect.

  8. Jul 8, 2026

    Improved

    Receipt reading in the mobile app

    Crumpled and faded receipts are read more reliably, and you can snap several in a row without leaving the camera.

  9. Jun 25, 2026

    New

    Canadian dollar and euro invoices

    Invoice in Canadian dollars on Studio, and in Canadian dollars or euros on Practice. Payments are converted at the rate on the day and the difference is recorded for you.
