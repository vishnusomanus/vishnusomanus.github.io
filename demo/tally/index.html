03 / Getting started

Set up before your coffee goes cold.

  1. Step 1 · 4 min

    Connect your business account

    Read-only bank feeds from US banks and credit unions. Tally can see transactions; it can never move money.

  2. Step 2 · 3 min

    Add your logo and payment details

    Your EIN, bank details and terms go on every invoice. Import clients from a spreadsheet if you have one.

  3. Step 3 · 1 min

    Send your first invoice

    Choose a reminder schedule, add a card payment link and press send. Tally takes it from there.
