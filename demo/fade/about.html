Our story

Brothers. Barbers.
Neighbors.

Fade Bros. started with two brothers, three chairs and a simple idea: a great barbershop is about the cut, the conversation and never feeling rushed.

Since 2012

Learned on 79th Street

Marcus and Danny Okafor grew up in their uncle Femi’s barbershop in Chatham, first sweeping floors, then cutting the regulars after closing. Femi had one rule: “Nobody leaves my chair unhappy, and nobody leaves without a hot towel.”

In 2012 they took that rule to a former shoe repair shop on Milwaukee Avenue. Today there are ten chairs across two shops, six barbers and thousands of regulars, and the rule is painted on the wall in both.

We’re still family-owned. We still play records. And Uncle Femi still gets his cut for free.

Timeline

Fourteen years, two shops

  1. 2002

    Sweeping floors on 79th Street

    Marcus, then 16, starts sweeping hair at his uncle Femi’s shop in Chatham. Danny follows two years later.

  2. 2012

    Three chairs on Milwaukee Ave

    The brothers sign a lease on a former shoe repair shop in Logan Square and open with three chairs and a borrowed record player.

  3. 2016

    Six chairs and Hal

    Logan Square doubles in size. Hal Brennan, a neighborhood legend since 1992, brings his clients and his flat tops.

  4. 2019

    Pilsen opens

    Danny opens our second shop on 18th Street under a mural by Mariela Ruiz. Rosa is the first barber through the door.

  5. 2023

    Our own products

    After years of mixing pomade in the back room, we launch a small line with a Wisconsin apothecary.

  6. 2026

    The apprentice program

    A paid 12-month program for new barbers, taught in both shops. Our first class of three starts this fall.

House rules

Our standards

Painted on the wall in both shops. If we fall short, tell the front desk or send us a note.

  1. 01

    Hot towel with every cut

    Every cut, including the $25 buzz, ends with a hot towel and a straight-razor neck shave.

  2. 02

    Clean tools, every client

    Combs, guards and clippers are disinfected between clients. Every shave gets a fresh blade, opened in front of you.

  3. 03

    On time or $10 off

    If you booked and we keep you waiting more than 10 minutes, the next $10 is on us.

  4. 04

    One price list

    Same prices at both shops and with every barber, from apprentice to master. No “consultation” upsells.

  5. 05

    Free fix for 7 days

    Not happy? Come back within a week and we’ll put it right at no charge.

  6. 06

    Everyone’s welcome

    All hair types, all ages, all genders. We price the service, not the person in the chair.
