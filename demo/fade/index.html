House rules

The Fade Bros. standard

Four promises painted on the wall of both shops. Hold us to them.

Read the full story

  1. 01

    Hot towel, every time

    Every cut finishes with a hot towel and a straight-razor neck shave. Even the $25 buzz.

  2. 02

    Clean tools, every client

    Combs and guards are disinfected between clients and every shave gets a fresh blade.

  3. 03

    On time or $10 off

    If we keep a booked client waiting more than 10 minutes, the next $10 is on us.

  4. 04

    Free fix for 7 days

    Not quite right? Come back within a week and we’ll tidy it up, no charge, no questions.
