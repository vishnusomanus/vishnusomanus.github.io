Missoula’s movie palace since 1931

Lights down, curtain up, every night.

The Garnet is a nonprofit, single-screen theater on North Higgins Avenue: 202 velvet seats, a 1929 theater organ and a projection booth that still runs 35mm. New films, classics, documentaries and family matinees, every day of the year.

        Series & events

        More than a movie

        Silent films with live organ, a members’ Cinema Club with pie, Saturday kids’ matinees and the Late Show Society after dark. Something happens at The Garnet most weeks that you can’t stream at home.

          Membership

          Keep the lights on Higgins Avenue

          More than 3,100 members keep The Garnet open, independent and showing films nobody else will. In return: $8 tickets, free popcorn, early booking and a vote on what we screen.

          Visiting The Garnet

          Getting here

          Downtown on North Higgins, a block from the Clark Fork. Free evening parking in the Front Street garage after 6 pm.

          Access for everyone

          Step-free entrance, four wheelchair spaces, a hearing loop, captioned and audio-described shows every week.
