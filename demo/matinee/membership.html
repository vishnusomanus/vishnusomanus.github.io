Membership

Your seat at The Garnet, every week of the year

The Garnet is a nonprofit. Memberships pay for the projectionist, the organ tuner and the films nobody else in Montana will show. You get cheaper tickets, free popcorn and a say in the program.

    Member questions

    Can I bring a guest at the member price?+

    Yes, one guest per visit at every level. Projectionist members also get two free tickets each month to use however they like.

    Is my membership tax-deductible?+

    Part of it. The Garnet Theater is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and we send a receipt each January showing the deductible portion. This is general information, not tax advice.

    Do member prices apply to special events?+

    For screenings with an intro or Q&A, yes. Organ Loft Silents and private events have their own prices, with a member discount listed on each.

    How do I cancel?+

    Call (406) 555-0125 or tell the box office. Monthly memberships stop at the end of the month; annual memberships simply don’t renew.
