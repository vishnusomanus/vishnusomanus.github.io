      Good to know

      Showtimes include trailers

      About ten minutes of trailers and one short reminder to silence your phone. Late seating is fine until the film starts.

      Matinee prices before 5 pm

      Every screening that starts before 5:00 pm is $10 for adults and $8 for kids. Members pay $7.

      Need something else?

      Ask for an audio-description or assistive-listening headset at any show. Call (406) 555-0120 with questions.
