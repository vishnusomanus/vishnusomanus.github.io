Common questions

Can’t find your question? Ask us directly.

Do you represent employees as well as employers?+

Our employment practice represents employers. If you are an employee, we are happy to refer you to lawyers who represent individuals in workplace matters.

How are employment matters billed?+

Advisory work is usually billed hourly or on a fixed fee for defined projects such as a handbook update. Litigation is billed hourly with a written budget agreed at the start.

Can you help with employees in other states?+

Yes. We advise on employees across the United States, working with trusted local counsel where a state’s law requires it.

How quickly can you start an investigation?+

For urgent complaints we can usually begin interviews within two business days of being engaged.
