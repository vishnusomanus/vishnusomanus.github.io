About

I’m Mira — I believe the best interfaces feel inevitable.

I grew up between Lagos and Newark, studied architecture, and fell for software when I realized buildings and apps share the same question: how should this feel to move through?

For eight years I’ve designed products for fintech, health and commerce companies — first in-house, leading a team at Northwind Pay, and now independently from a small studio in Lancaster, PA.

I work best embedded with a team for a few months: close to engineers, close to customers, and close to the code. I care about the boring details — empty states, error messages, loading screens — because that’s where trust is built.

Outside work I throw pots badly, paddle the Susquehanna whatever the weather, and run a small monthly meetup for designers new to Lancaster.
