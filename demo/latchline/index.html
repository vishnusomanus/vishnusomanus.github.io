The gap after “yes”

Most onboardings don't stall on the hard part.

They stall between people: a login nobody requested, a decision with no owner, a sponsor who stopped reading the updates. Latchline makes those handoffs visible, on both sides of the deal.

How it works

Plan it, share it, chase less, prove it.

Walk through the six stages

  1. 01 Plan

    Start from a playbook

    Fifteen tested playbooks, or your own. A board opens the moment the deal closes, with dates worked out from kickoff.

  2. 02 Share

    Give the customer a portal

    Customers see their tasks in plain language, upload proof and know who to ask, without a login to your tools.

  3. 03 Chase less

    Let blockers escalate themselves

    Polite reminders, coverage when someone is away and an escalation after two business days. Nobody has to be the nag.

  4. 04 Prove

    Measure time to first value

    The outcome you agreed on day one becomes the finish line, tracked per playbook, team and segment.
