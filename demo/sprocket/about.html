About us

Made on the hill.

Bonfield Bikes started in 2023 in a rented garage in West Duluth, with one welder, a borrowed lathe and a simple question: why does every cargo bike give up when it snows?

Tove Kettunen had spent nine years designing snowmobile chassis. Eino Wennberg had designed mining equipment on the Iron Range. Both had kids, both lived up the hill, and neither could find a bike that would carry a three-year-old up Mesaba Avenue in January. So they built one.

From garage to batches

  1. 2023

    First frame welded in a West Duluth garage. It weighed 91 lb and climbed everything.

  2. 2024

    Four prototypes, one winter on Mesaba Avenue, and a battery that keeps itself warm.

  3. 2025

    Reservations open. 300 deposits in the first month, mostly from people who had seen it on the hill.

  4. 2026

    Batch 1 ships in March. A seed round, a second frame jig and our own paint line.
