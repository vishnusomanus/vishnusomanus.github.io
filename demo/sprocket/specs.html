One frame size, one motor, two batteries. Everything here is on every Portage unless it says otherwise.

Motor & battery

MotorMid-drive, 250 W nominal, 750 W peak, 85 Nm
AssistClass 1, pedal assist to 20 mph. Four levels: Eco, Tour, Sport, Turbo
Standard battery625 Wh, inside the frame, removable and lockable
Dual battery625 Wh + 275 Wh range extender (900 Wh in all), +$520
Estimated range30–55 mi standard, 45–80 mi dual. Estimate yours
Cold weatherHeated battery sleeve below 20°F; charging held until the cells are above 32°F
Charger4 A, 0–80% in about 3½ h (standard battery)

Frame & fit

Frame6061 aluminum, TIG welded and powder coated in Duluth
Fits riders4′11″ to 6′5″, one size, with a telescoping stem and long seat post
Length74 in (188 cm); 32 in tall when stood on its tail rack
Weight68 lb with the standard battery; 72 lb with the dual battery
Capacity400 lb rider and cargo; 175 lb on the rear deck; 45 lb in the front basket

Drivetrain, brakes & wheels

DriveCarbon belt, stepless internal gear hub (380% range)
BrakesFour-piston hydraulic discs, 203 mm rotors front and rear
Wheels20 in, 36 spokes, double-wall rims
Tires20 × 3.0 in puncture-resistant; studded winter tires optional (+$170)

Everything else

Lights600 lm headlight, brake-sensing tail light, on the main battery
DisplayBacklit, readable with gloves; USB-C port to charge a phone
WeatherSealed connectors, IP65 battery and motor, zinc-rich primer under the paint
ColorsTaconite Red, Harbor Teal, Birch White, Iron Black, Lupine Violet
WarrantyLifetime frame; 2 years on motor, battery and electronics
