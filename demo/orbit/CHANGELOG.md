# Changelog

All notable changes to the Orbit template are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this template follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.6] - 2026-11-07

### Changed

- American wording throughout, including US terms for checkboxes, business days, schedules and units.

## [1.0.5] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Overland Park, Kansas, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.
- The schedule mock-ups, kiosk and availability use a 12-hour clock, and the Skycast weather rule uses Fahrenheit.

## [1.0.4] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-26

### Fixed

- Example email addresses (sign-up placeholders and invites) use the reserved `.example` domain, and the Manchester
  phone number is 0161 496 0142, from Ofcom's range reserved for drama.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-23

### Fixed

- The newsletter, updates, sales and sign-up confirmations no longer say the form is a demo and nothing was
  sent. Newsletter and updates subscribers are asked to look out for a confirmation email, the sales form says
  who has the request, when they reply and shows a reference like OR-7K2Q, and the sign-up summary says a link
  to confirm the email address is on its way. `DOCS.md` describes the new wording.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed

- The shift schedule's cells' accessible names now include the shift they show ("AM 7–3") (WCAG 2.5.3).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-14

### Added

- Initial release with 10 pages: Home, Features, Pricing, Integrations, Customers, Changelog, About & careers,
  Talk to sales, Start free trial, 404.
- Interactive hero schedule: click, drag or use the keyboard to place shifts, with live coverage, hours and an auto-fill button
- Product tour with tabs that swap HTML product mock-ups, plus arrow-key navigation
- Pricing with a monthly / annual switch, a per-location and per-employee price calculator and a sticky-header comparison table
- Searchable integrations directory with category filters, shareable URLs and an accessible detail drawer
- Customer stories filterable by industry, with before and after metrics and an SVG chart
- Changelog with topic filter and an email subscription form
- Sales form that routes visitors by team size, with work-email validation and conditional fields
- Three-step free trial sign-up with progress stepper, password strength meter and per-step validation
- Bright pastel design with Gabarito display type, illustrated SVG avatars and fictional customer wordmarks
- SEO and Open Graph tags, SVG favicon, 1200×630 social image, skip link and reduced-motion support
- Full documentation in `DOCS.md`.
