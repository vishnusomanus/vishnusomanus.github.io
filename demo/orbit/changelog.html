What’s new in Orbit

We ship something every two weeks. Most of it starts as a message from a manager in the middle of service.

  1. October 8, 2026NewImproved

    Auto-fill now balances hours fairly

    Auto-fill used to pick the first available person. Now it looks at everyone’s hours for the week and spreads shifts evenly, while keeping minimum hours and never pushing anyone into overtime unless you allow it.

    You’ll see a small “fair” score under each person. Hover it to see why Orbit picked them.

    Tablebook reservations on the schedule

    Connect Tablebook and booked covers appear above each day. Parties of 12 or more get a flag and a suggestion to add a server.

  3. September 16, 2026MobileNew

    Home-screen widgets for your next shift

    The team app now has widgets for iOS and Android. Your next shift, who you’re working with and a clock-in button, right on the home screen.

  4. September 2, 2026Improved

    Break rules by location

    Different sites, different rules. Set paid and unpaid breaks per location and Orbit warns you before you publish a shift that breaks them.

    • Rules can depend on shift length and age
    • Warnings show in the builder and on timesheets

  5. August 21, 2026Fixed

    Overnight shifts on timesheets

    Shifts that cross midnight now count toward the day they started, which fixes double-counted hours for late-night bars. Thanks to everyone who reported it.

  6. August 7, 2026Integrations

    Weather from Skycast, with staffing rules

    Add the forecast to every day and set rules like “over 72°F, add one person to the patio”. Orbit suggests the change; you decide.

    Read receipts on announcements

    See who has read an announcement and nudge the rest with one tap. Handy for menu changes, fire drills and anything nobody can miss.

  8. July 10, 2026ImprovedMobile

    Faster swaps on slow connections

    Swap requests now work offline and sync when you’re back on signal. Basement stockrooms, we see you.
