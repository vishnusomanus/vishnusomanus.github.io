Schedule builder

Build a week in the time it takes to drink a flat white.

Start from a template or last week, drag shifts where you want them and let auto-fill suggest the rest. Orbit checks coverage, target hours and skills while you work.

  • Templates for regular weeks, holidays and quiet months
  • Auto-fill that balances hours fairly across the team
  • Roles and skills, so only trained people land on the bar
  • Multi-location view for district managers

Availability & time off

Never schedule someone who already said no.

Your team sets when they can work and requests time off from their phone. Approved time off is blocked on the schedule, and PTO balances update automatically.

  • Recurring availability with exceptions
  • Time-off requests with notes and approval
  • PTO balances and accrual by employee
  • Blackout dates for your busiest weekends

Swaps & open shifts

Cover gaps before you even notice them.

Post an open shift and Orbit offers it to people who are available, trained and under their hours. Swaps between teammates can go straight through or wait for your approval.

  • Open shifts offered to the right people first
  • One-tap swaps with optional manager approval
  • Automatic checks for conflicts, overtime and rest breaks
  • A record of every change for fairness

Labor costs

See the cost of the week before you publish it.

Connect your POS and Orbit forecasts sales by hour. Labor cost and labor % sit next to the schedule, so you can trim a quiet Tuesday or add a server for a big Saturday.

  • Sales forecasts from your POS history
  • Live labor cost and labor % by day and location
  • Targets with gentle warnings, not hard blocks
  • Weekly email with last week’s actual vs plan

Time clock

Clock in on any phone or a shared tablet.

People clock in from their own phone on site or from a kiosk tablet by the door. Timesheets fill themselves, ready to approve and send to payroll.

  • Phone clock-in with location check
  • Kiosk mode with PIN or photo
  • Breaks, late and early arrivals highlighted
  • One-click export to payroll

Team messages

Announcements that people actually read.

  • Direct messages between teammates
  • Quiet hours so nobody gets pinged on a day off
