All plants/Your next plant

We pack every plant snugly in recyclable cardboard, with a care card. We ship Monday through Thursday, so no plant waits in a warehouse over a weekend: order by 12 noon Pacific on a shipping day and it leaves the same afternoon. Tracked delivery then takes 1–2 business days. Local delivery is $8.95, or free when your plant subtotal after any discount reaches $75.

Pickup at 18 East 5th Avenue is free, ready the next day we’re open. Our courier covers ZIP codes around Eugene only: Eugene, Springfield, Coburg, Pleasant Hill, Creswell, Cottage Grove, Junction City, Veneta and Harrisburg. We can’t send plants to PO boxes.

If your plant arrives damaged, email a photo to hello@frond.example within 48 hours. We’ll take a look and help make it right.
