The care cupboard

A little looking.
A lot of learning.

No rigid watering days. No magic tricks. Start with the light, feel the soil and get to know what your plant is telling you.

The simplest growing kit

Check the soil.
Watch the leaves.
Let the light lead.

A curious eye and a finger in the soil will tell you more than a watering calendar.

The right care, for your plant

Know what’s in your pot.

Leaf a clue

Something looking off?

One leaf rarely tells the whole story. Choose the closest symptom for a sensible first check.

Small habits, healthier plants

Make a little time.

Each week

Look before you pour.

Check the soil with a finger, then lift the pot. Light pot, dry soil? Water thoroughly, let it drain and empty the cover pot. A calendar can remind you to check, but should not decide the watering.

Each month

A clean leaf works harder.

Wipe smooth leaves with a damp, soft cloth. Skip leaf-shine sprays. Turn the pot a quarter turn and check the undersides for pests. Keep new plants apart from your collection for two weeks.

Through the seasons

Let the light lead.

Spring and summer bring growth and a little feeding. In fall and winter, growth slows: check less often, feed less and keep leaves away from cold glass. A bright window is still useful in the darker months.

When it’s time for more room

Roots at the door?

If roots circle the pot tightly or water runs straight through, spring is a good time to repot. Move up just an inch or two in diameter and always choose drainage.

Loosen the outer roots gently, use a suitable fresh potting mix, and settle the plant at the same depth. Don’t bury the stem.

Not sure about the roots? Bring a clear photograph of the plant and its pot to the shop. We’ll help you work out the next step.
