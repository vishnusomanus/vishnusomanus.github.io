How we match

Less guesswork.
More growing.

Light matters most, so we start there, then weigh up your routine and the spot you have in mind. Every suggestion comes with the reason we picked it, best matches first.

  • Bright

    Within a few feet of a south, east or west window.

  • Softer

    Further into the room, or by a north window.

  • Lower

    A hallway or corner where you can still read by day.

Pet-friendly means commonly listed as non-toxic to cats and dogs. Even non-toxic plants can upset a stomach if eaten, so keep plants out of chewing reach and ask a vet about any ingestion.

Just want a safe bet?

Three plants that forgive almost anything.

  • ZZ plant Copes with a dim corner and a missed month.
  • Golden pothos Grows almost anywhere and droops when thirsty.
  • Baby rubber plant Pet-friendly, compact and very forgiving.
