# Changelog

All notable changes to Frond are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-07

### Fixed

- The plant finder's page description now says four questions, matching the page.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Eugene, Oregon, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.
- Delivery checks now use 5-digit ZIP codes (optional +4) around Eugene, shipping and opening hours follow US federal
  holidays by rule in Pacific time, and the cart and checkout note that Oregon has no sales tax, so none is added.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Nine complete pages and a 404 for an independent houseplant shop in Knaresborough.
- Fifteen illustrated houseplants under their common and botanical names, each with its own care card, two sizes and
  an optional cover pot.
- A saved basket with delivery, collection, postcode checks and a welcome code.
- A three-step checkout with gift message and gift wrap, card validation and an order confirmation with a dispatch
  date worked out from the cut-off, dispatch days and UK bank holidays.
- A four-question plant finder with graded, ranked matches and a reason for each plant.
- Care tools, a visit page with an illustrated map and live opening hours, and a contact form.
- Bree Serif and Cabin from Google Fonts, Tailwind styling and full documentation.
