On foot, by rail, by wheel.

We’re a three-minute walk from the Amtrak station, where the Cascades and Coast Starlight stop, and half a block from Willamette Street. East 5th Avenue is level and easy by bike; the river path and Skinner Butte are an easy wander after.

A note on access

There is one shallow step at the entrance, and the shop is compact. Call us before visiting if you’d like us to bring plants to the doorway or talk through what will make your visit easier. Assistance dogs are welcome.

Click, pick up, say hello

Choose free pickup at checkout and your plants are ready the next day we’re open, checked, watered and wrapped. Bring your order number.
