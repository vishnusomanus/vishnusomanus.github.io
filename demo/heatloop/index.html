Thermal storage for homes · Bangor, Maine

Buy your heat at night. Use it all day.

Heatloop is a thermal battery the size of a refrigerator. It charges from cheap off-peak electricity, holds the energy as heat in a phase-change store, and warms your radiators and hot water whenever you need it.

01How it works

Three steps, every day, without you thinking about it.

Electricity is cheapest when the country is asleep and the wind is still blowing. Heatloop buys it then, and your home doesn’t notice the difference at suppertime.

  1. Step 01 · 11 PM–7 AM

    Charge on the cheap rate

    The controller reads your rate plan and charges when power is cheapest, usually overnight and sometimes for an hour after lunch.

  2. Step 02 · up to 28 kWh

    Hold it as heat

    Sealed cells of a salt-based material melt at 136°F and soak up energy as they do. Vacuum panels keep it in for the day.

  3. Step 03 · whenever you like

    Release it to radiators and faucets

    Water runs through the copper exchanger and comes out at up to 144°F, to the radiators or baseboard you already have and to your hot faucets.
