Company

We think heat should be bought like groceries: when it’s cheap, and kept until you need it.

Heatloop started in 2022 in a garage in Brewer, when two engineers from Maine’s paper mills asked what their city would heat its homes with next. Today we are 34 people with a workshop in Hermon and fifty homes’ worth of data.

01What we believe

What we believe

  1. i.

    Measure it, then say it

    Every claim on this site comes from a test rig or a pilot home. Where we’re estimating, we say so and show the sums.

  2. ii.

    Comfort before cleverness

    A warm house on a January evening matters more than an elegant charging schedule. The controls are built in that order.

  3. iii.

    Made where it’s used

    We design, build and install in eastern Maine, with local suppliers wherever we can, so we can fix what we make.

04Timeline

The story so far

  1. 2022

    A garage in Brewer

    Margot and Rasmus melt their first salt hydrates in a borrowed garage and a lot of saucepans, and Sea Smoke Angels write the first check.

  2. 2023

    A cell that lasts

    The sealed cell design passes 1,000 melt-freeze cycles with no measurable loss. Ledgewood Capital joins for a $2M pre-seed round.

  3. 2024

    Ten pilot homes

    Hand-built units go into homes in Brewer, Orono and Holden, each logging every half hour.

  4. 2025

    Fifty homes, one cold snap

    The pilot grows to fifty homes and comes through a week of 15 below zero without a single cold house.

  5. 2026

    Seed round and the Hermon workshop

    An $8.5M seed round led by Tidehead Ventures. Production moves to Coldbrook Road, Hermon, with room for 2,000 units a year.

  6. Next 2027

    500 homes

    Installs across Bangor and Holden, then Ellsworth, Bucksport and Newport.
