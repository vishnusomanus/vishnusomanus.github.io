05How we worked it out

The formula, with your numbers in it.

No hidden factors. Every price and efficiency below is a sample assumption you can argue with, so swap in the numbers from your own bills.

  1. 1 · Heat

    Typical yearly heat for your home size, adjusted for your region’s climate.

    22,000 kWh × 1.00 = 22,000 kWh

  2. 2 · Now

    Fuel needed at your system’s efficiency, times its unit price and its carbon factor.

    –

  3. 3 · Blend

    The price a Heatloop pays on average: the share bought at the cheap rate, and the rest at the day rate.

    –

  4. 4 · Heatloop

    Electricity needed at 95% overall efficiency (store losses included), times the blended price. Carbon uses illustrative New England grid figures of 0.55 lb/kWh at night and 0.60 lb/kWh by day.
