From permit to license

Four intersections between you and your license.

  1. 1

    Permit tests

    Practice with our road-sense quiz, then pass the Wisconsin DMV knowledge, signs and vision tests.

  2. 2

    Instruction permit

    Your “temps.” Available from 18 with the tests, or from 15 if you’re also enrolled in driver education.

  3. 3

    First lesson

    Two hours, $55 an hour for new students. Your instructor works out a plan and a rough number of hours.

  4. 4

    Road test

    Schedule it with the DMV when your instructor says you’re ready, in the car you learned in, with a warm-up lesson first.
