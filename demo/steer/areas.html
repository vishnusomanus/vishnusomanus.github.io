Test routes

The kinds of road your test can use, practiced ahead of time.

The DMV examiner chooses the roads, so we don’t teach routes by heart. We teach the kinds of road you could meet, plus the Y-turn, roadside stop and start, parking and backing, in this order.

  1. Neighborhoods and quiet streets

    Putnam Heights and Randall Park. Pulling out, stopping, four-way stops and your first maneuvers where there’s room to think.

    • Clutch control
    • Four-way stops
    • Parking

  2. The hill streets

    The climb up from downtown to the hill neighborhoods. Hill starts, narrow streets with parked cars on both sides, and judging when to yield.

    • Hill starts
    • Yielding
    • Crosswalks

  3. Clairemont Avenue and roundabouts

    Multi-lane traffic on Clairemont Avenue and Hastings Way, roundabouts out toward Altoona, lane choice from the signs, and keeping up without rushing.

    • Lane choice
    • Left-turn signals
    • Following GPS

  4. County highways and US-53

    County highways out toward Elk Mound, then merging on and off US-53 at highway speed.

    • Curves
    • On-ramps
    • Passing bicyclists

Outside our area?

Students from Menomonie, Osseo and Bloomer can take an intensive course with pick-up by arrangement, or meet their instructor at our office on Water Street, a short walk from an Eau Claire Transit bus stop.
