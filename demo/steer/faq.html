Getting started

How old do I have to be to start lessons?

In Wisconsin you can get an instruction permit from age 15, as long as you’re enrolled in a certified driver education program if you’re under 18. Adults 18 and over start with the tests. You need the permit before your first lesson with us. Check current rules with the Wisconsin DMV.

What do I need before my first lesson?

Your instruction permit, comfortable flat shoes, and glasses or contact lenses if you need them to drive. Under 18, your driver education program signs your MV3001 application and handles its part of the paperwork; ask them how lessons with us fit in.

Should I learn in a manual or an automatic?

Your Wisconsin license covers both, whichever one you test in. Most cars here are automatic, so most students start there and often need fewer hours. Choose manual if you’ll be driving a stick shift or want the skill. If you’re unsure, ask your instructor.

I’ve had lessons elsewhere. Can I carry on with you?

Yes. Your first lesson is an assessment drive, and your instructor will pick up where you left off rather than starting again.

Can I choose a male or female instructor?

Of course. Choose anyone on the instructors page, or tell the office what you’d prefer and we’ll match you.

Lessons

How many lessons will I need?

It varies a lot. Our students pass after 38 hours on average, and many add practice with family. Your instructor gives you an honest estimate after your first lesson and updates it as you go.

Where do lessons start and finish?

Anywhere in our six ZIP codes around Eau Claire: home, school, college or work, and it can be different each time. Check your ZIP code on the areas page.

Are 2-hour lessons better than 1-hour?

For most learners, yes: you spend less of the lesson warming up and get further from home. One-hour lessons work well for a quick refresher or practicing one maneuver.

Can I have lessons in the evening or on Saturdays?

Lessons run from 7am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday. Harpreet teaches evenings and four of our instructors work Saturdays. We don’t teach on Sundays or federal holidays.

Can I practice with my parents as well?

Yes, and it helps. Your supervising driver needs a regular license and at least two years of licensed driving, and sits beside you. Under 18, you must log at least 50 hours, 10 of them at night, before a probationary license. Ask your insurer about their car, and your instructor what to practice between lessons.

Tests

When should I take the knowledge test?

Before your permit, since you need it to get one. It’s 50 questions and you need 40 right, plus a 15-question signs test and a vision screening at a DMV service center. Try our practice quiz to see where you are.

Who schedules the road test?

You do, on the Wisconsin DMV’s website, or the office can do it with you so the date fits your instructor’s schedule. Appointments fill up, so plan ahead. Under 18, you must hold your permit at least six months with no violations; 18 and over, at least seven days. You take the test in a car you provide, and the examiner checks it first.

Can I use the school car for my road test?

Yes. It’s $145 for the car, a one-hour warm-up lesson just before and your instructor waiting at the DMV. On intensive courses it’s included.

What happens if I don’t pass?

It happens to lots of good drivers. Your instructor goes through the examiner’s results with you, you’ll work on what needs it, and you can schedule again. The DMV sets the wait before another test, from 1 to 14 days depending on your results, and charges a fee for each test.

Can my instructor ride along on the test?

Plan on it being just you and the examiner. Your instructor waits at the DMV and goes through the results with you afterward, so you know exactly what happened if you need a second try.

Paying and cancelling

How do I pay?

Pay as you go at the end of each lesson by card or check. Blocks and intensive courses are paid up front or in two halves.

What if I need to cancel a lesson?

Cancel or move any lesson for free up to 48 hours before. With less notice we charge the full lesson, unless your instructor can fill the time. Illness with a doctor’s note is always free.

Do block booking hours expire?

No. Use them over as many weeks as you need. If you stop learning, unused hours are refunded at the block rate.

Is there a discount for students?

Full-time students get 5% off blocks of 10 hours or more and intensive courses. Show your student ID at your first lesson. See the price calculator.

Can I buy lessons as a gift?

Yes. Gift certificates for 2, 5 or 10 hours never expire. Call the office or send us a message.
