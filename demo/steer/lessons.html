Intensive courses

Learn in weeks, not months.

Daily lessons with one instructor, the car for your road test and a warm-up lesson before it. Courses start on a Monday. Once you have your instruction permit we help you schedule your road test and aim to line it up with the end of your course, subject to availability.

Next course starts: Monday, two weeks from now

    Road test dates depend on Wisconsin DMV availability, and drivers under 18 must hold a permit for at least six months first. If the test date moves, your remaining hours move with it at no extra cost.

    After your test

    Winter & Highway: the roads your test didn’t cover.

    Six hours over two or three sessions, any time after you pass, though it helps most in your first year on the road, and most of all before your first Wisconsin winter. No test at the end: your instructor assesses you as you go. Some insurers may offer a discount for extra training, so ask yours.
