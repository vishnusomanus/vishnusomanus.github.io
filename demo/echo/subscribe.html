Questions

New to podcasts? Start here.

You don’t need an app to listen: every episode plays right here on the site, and your place is saved in this browser. Apps add downloads and automatic new episodes.

Play an episode now
Is Overtone free?

Yes, every episode is free in every app. We are paid for by a few sponsors per season, and by listeners who choose to support the show.

How long are episodes?

Between 45 minutes and an hour and twenty. Every episode has chapters, so you can jump to the part you care about.

Where are the transcripts?

On each episode page, below the show notes. You can search them, and select any line to play from that moment.

Do I have to listen in order?

No. Each episode is a standalone conversation. If you want a starting point, the four on our home page are a good introduction.

Can I share a moment from an episode?

Yes. On an episode page, pause where you want, check “Start at” in the share box and copy the link. It opens at that exact second.
