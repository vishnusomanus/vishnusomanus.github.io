The Quietest Room on Earth

      Your hosts

      A designer and a journalist walk into a piano shop.

      Nadia spent twelve years designing kitchen appliances. Eli spent ten producing science radio. In 2022 they rented the back room of a piano repair shop in Philadelphia and started asking people how they make things.
