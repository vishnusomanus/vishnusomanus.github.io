01 · Search

Ask once. Prism searches everywhere.

Prism plans a search across your drives, wiki, help desk, CRM and the web, reads the passages that matter and ranks them before it writes a word.

  • Hybrid keyword and semantic search, tuned per source
  • Filters for date, author, source and file type in plain language
  • Flags when two sources disagree instead of picking one

02 · Write

Drafts with the receipts attached.

Turn research into briefs, emails, reports and specs. Citations stay attached as you edit, and Prism re-checks them when you change a sentence.

  • Follows your team’s style guide and templates
  • Unsupported sentences are underlined, never hidden
  • Export to docs, slides or Markdown with footnotes

Security and privacy

Your data trains nothing. Ever.

Customer content is never used to train models, on any plan. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the region you choose and deleted on your schedule.

  • SOC 2 Type IIReport on request
  • ISO 27001Aligned controls
  • CCPAService provider terms
  • SSO and SCIMSAML 2.0, OIDC
  • AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Audit log of every query and export
  • US East, Central or West data residency
  • Annual third-party penetration tests
