Dining

The Terrazza, from breakfast to the last glass

Coastal New England cooking by chef Lucia Ferraro, served on the terrace from May to October and by the fire in winter.

Breakfast

7:30 – 10:30 am

Served on the terrace or in your room: warm johnnycakes, fruit from the market, eggs and pancakes made to order.

Terrazza

Lunch 12:30 – 3 pm · Dinner 7:30 – 10:30 pm

Fish from the morning boats, pasta made by hand each afternoon and vegetables from the garden terraces.

Bar Marea

5 pm – late

Aperitivo hour as the sun sets: Rhode Island white wine, a basil negroni and focaccia still warm from the oven.
