Location & contact

Above the harbor, at the end of Lighthouse Lane

Getting here

12 Lighthouse Lane, Newport, RI 02840

  • By train

    Newport has no train station. Amtrak trains from Boston and New York stop at Kingston (35 min by car) and Providence (50 min by car).

  • By air

    T.F. Green Airport in Providence is 40 minutes away; Boston Logan about 90 minutes. A driver costs $120 each way: add it when you book.

  • By car

    Take Route 138 over the Newport Pell Bridge and follow the signs for Ocean Drive for 2 miles. Garage parking is $35 per night.

Good to know

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out by 11 am. If your room is ready early we'll let you know; late check-out until 3 pm can be added to your booking.

Is there parking?

Yes, eight spaces in our garage behind the house at $35 per night. Please reserve one when you book, especially in July and August.

Are children welcome?

Very. The Family Suite sleeps four, cribs are free, and the pool has a shallow end. Children under 4 stay free.

Can I bring my dog?

Small, well-behaved dogs are welcome in the Garden Rooms for $40 per night, including a bed and bowls.

Is the hotel accessible?

The pool level, restaurant and one Garden Room are step-free, with an elevator between floors. Tell us what you need and we'll plan around it.
