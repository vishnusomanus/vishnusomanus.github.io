A quiet house above the sea.

Twenty-two rooms in a 1903 villa above the harbor in Newport, with an infinity pool, a terrace restaurant and the whole of Narragansett Bay below.

Welcome

Twenty-two rooms, one long terrace and the sound of the sea.

Casa Marea was built in 1903 as a summer house for a family of New York shipowners. A century later we restored it slowly, keeping the arched windows, the terrazzo floors and the garden of hydrangeas and beach roses.

The wharves of Newport Harbor are a four-minute walk down the old steps. The Ocean Drive passes our gate.

Getting here

Forty minutes from Providence, a world away

  • By train · Amtrak stops at Kingston, about 35 minutes by car, and Providence, about 50 minutes.
  • By air · T.F. Green Airport in Providence is 40 minutes by car; we can arrange a driver.
  • By car · Take Route 138 over the Newport Pell Bridge. Our garage has eight spaces.
