About us

Marlow Bay’s independent agent since 1999

We started with one desk above a bakery on Harbor Street. Today we’re a team of twenty-four across two offices, still independent, still local, and still answering the phone ourselves.

What we believe

Honest pricing

We’d rather lose a listing than win it with a number we can’t achieve. Our valuations are backed by real sales on your street.

One agent, start to finish

No hand-offs to call centers. The person who values your home is the person who sells it.

Rooted in the bay

We live here, sponsor the harbor regatta and know which streets flood and which get the evening sun.

Our story

Twenty-seven years on the harbor

  1. 1999

    One desk on Harbor Street

    Eleanor Vance opens Harbor & Hill above Malone’s Bakery with a borrowed fax machine.

  2. 2006

    Rentals arrive

    We launch a rentals and management service. Priya Nair joins two years later and builds it to 400 homes.

  3. 2014

    Westshore office

    A second office opens beside the Westshore marina to serve the growing coastal market.

  4. 2021

    Agency of the Year

    Named Independent Agency of the Year at the Coastal Property Awards, the first of three wins.

  5. 2026

    Still independent

    Twenty-four people, two offices and the same promise: local agents who answer their own phones.
