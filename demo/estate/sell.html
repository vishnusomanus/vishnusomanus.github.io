Selling questions

How accurate is an online valuation?+

Online estimates are a starting point. An in-person visit lets us account for condition, light, views and the details that make your home different from its neighbors.

Is there a minimum contract length?+

No. Our agreement is rolling: you can give two weeks’ notice at any time. We’d rather keep you because we’re good.

Do I need to be home for viewings?+

No. We host every viewing and send you feedback the same day, so you can get on with life.

How long does a sale take?+

Our homes go under offer in 24 days on average. From offer to completion typically takes another 8–12 weeks.
