The people behind the pieces

A small yard.
A careful eye.

We are three people with a habit of looking twice. At a good joint, a worn step, the weight of a handle. Things that still have something to give.

Our way of working

Keep it useful.
Tell it plainly.

Most pieces arrive from house renovations and careful clearances. We record the account we are given, but do not invent a grand provenance where there is none.

  1. 01. Look closely

    Check joints, glass, edges and hardware. Measure in more than one place and record what is missing.

  2. 02. Do what is needed

    Clean, stabilize and make sound repairs. Keep older work visible where it is part of the piece.

  3. 03. Pass it on well

    Talk through the intended use, the installation questions and the journey home before agreeing a sale.

Have something
worth saving?

We consider small groups of architectural pieces, with clear ownership and safe access for removal. Send a few photographs, approximate sizes and the location. We cannot take every piece, but we will give you a straight answer.
