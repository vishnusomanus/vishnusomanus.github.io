Make a real visit of it.

The gravel yard has uneven surfaces and a level entrance to the covered store. Tell us about any access needs before you set off; we can bring smaller pieces to the front table.

Coming by car

The customer parking lot is just inside the yard gate. Keep the loading area clear unless we have arranged a pickup.

Picking up a piece

Bring blankets, straps and a truck or van with a flat load space. We will agree on a safe loading plan before pickup.

The next opening days

Closed on federal holidays and from Christmas Eve to January 2. Visits outside these hours need prior agreement.

The journey home

Big things need
a little planning.

Our guide price covers one street-level stop with clear access. Stone, steps, narrow entrances and long carries need a separate plan. We deliver within the contiguous US by arrangement.

  • Pickup in Troy is free.
  • Capital Region ZIP codes start at $65; the Hudson Valley, the Adirondacks, the Berkshires and southern Vermont at $145.
  • The rest of the contiguous US starts at $395. Weight adds to the guide price.
