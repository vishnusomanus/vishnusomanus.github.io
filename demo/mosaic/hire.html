    Good to know

    • Wi-Fi, tables, chairs and a flip chart in every room
    • Step-free entrance, elevator and accessible restroom
    • Hearing loop in the Yard Room
    • A building manager on site for every booking
    When can we rent a room?
    Between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM, any day we’re open, with a two-hour minimum. Evening hours (after 6:00 PM) cost 15% more because they need a building manager.
    Who gets the community rate?
    Registered nonprofits, community groups, neighborhood associations and anyone running something free or pay-what-you-can for local people. Check the box in the estimate and tell us a little about your group.
    Can we bring food and drink?
    Yes. You can use the kitchen for tea, coffee and cold food, or bring a caterer. A bar is possible through a licensed caterer; ask us early.
    Can we see the rooms first?
    Of course. Book a ten-minute tour with the welcome desk, Tuesday to Saturday.
    What about cancellations?
    Cancel up to 14 days before for a full refund. After that we refund half, or move your booking to another date for free.
