# Changelog

All notable changes to the Mosaic HTML template are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.6] - 2026-11-07

### Changed

- American wording throughout, including US terms for checkboxes, business days, schedules and units.

## [1.0.5] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Holyoke, Massachusetts, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.
- Times show on a 12-hour clock in Eastern time and dates use the US format. The building now also closes on Thanksgiving Day and the Fourth of July, worked out by rule, and the program and room-rental form respect those days.

## [1.0.4] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-04

### Fixed

- A change you make is kept for the visit when the browser can't save it, for example when its storage is full, instead of going back to the old value.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-23

### Fixed

- Form success messages no longer say the form is a demo or that nothing was sent. Booking, room-hire,
  volunteer, Friends and contact forms now confirm the request, say what happens next (using the reply times
  the pages already promise) and show a reference like MA-7K2Q; the newsletter points to a confirmation email.
  The footer line now just says the center is fictional. `DOCS.md` describes the reference.
- The Friends confirmation heading was white text on a light panel; it is now ink, like the newsletter's.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed

- The logo link's accessible name now includes its visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).
- The header's Saved link and the Save buttons' accessible names now include their visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Nine pages for Mosaic, a community arts center in Ulverston: home, what's on, event, workshops, hire a space, get involved, our story, visit and 404.
- A rule-based program of 20 listings (weekly and monthly sessions, rotating exhibitions with preview evenings, a yearly winter break), worked out in UK time so it never goes out of date.
- What's on filters by category, age, price and date range, a seven-day strip, saved events and .ics downloads.
- Room-hire estimate with live pricing and capacity checks, plus booking, volunteer, Friends and contact forms with inline validation.
- Cut-paper SVG artwork for every category, an illustrated town map, Tailwind CSS v4 styling and the Bagel Fat One and Epilogue typefaces.
