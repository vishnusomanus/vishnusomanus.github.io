How it works

Show up, try it, come back if you like it

You don’t need to be “arty”, bring gear or know anyone. Book the classes that have limited spots, drop in to the rest, and ask at the welcome desk if anything is unclear.

  1. 1

    Pick a session

    Filter the program by what you like, who it’s for and what it costs.

  2. 2

    Book or drop in

    Classes have small groups, so book ahead. Socials and the gallery are drop-in.

  3. 3

    Pay what’s fair

    Three regular sessions are pay what you can, and we keep supported spots in every class.
