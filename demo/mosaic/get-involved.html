Mosaic runs on people like you

Eighty-six volunteers, six hundred and forty Friends and a lot of teachers who started as students. Give an hour, share a skill or put your work on our walls.

Volunteering

Six ways to lend a hand

Every volunteer gets an induction, a named contact, travel expenses and free tea. Give as much or as little time as works for you.

  • Welcome desk

    The first face people see: say hello, point the way, sell the odd ticket and keep the coffee on.

    A morning or afternoon a week

    I’m interested in welcome desk

  • Workshop helper

    Set out the tools, help teachers with bigger groups and make sure first-timers never feel stuck.

    Evenings or Saturday mornings

    I’m interested in workshop helper

  • Gallery host

    Look after the Foyer Gallery, chat to visitors about the work and help hang new shows every few weeks.

    Three hours, any day we’re open

    I’m interested in gallery host

  • Repair café fixer

    Good with a screwdriver, a sewing machine or a bike pump? Fix things alongside their owners.

    Second Saturday of the month

    I’m interested in repair café fixer

  • Set-up crew

    Chairs, staging, lights and the big clear-up after gigs, film nights and open studios.

    A few evenings a month

    I’m interested in set-up crew

  • Something else

    Photography, social media, bookkeeping, baking for open days: tell us what you’d like to give.

    Whatever works for you

    I’m interested in something else

Start with a cup of coffee

Tell us a little about yourself and Ned Askew, our volunteer coordinator, will invite you in for a chat and a look around. There’s no application form and no commitment until you’ve tried it.

Under 16? Our Young Ambassadors meet on the first Saturday of the month. Ask a grown-up to get in touch.

Prefer to talk? (413) 555-0173 or volunteer@mosaic.example

Friends of Mosaic

Keep the doors open, and the prices low

Ticket sales and room rental cover about two thirds of what it costs to run Mosaic. Friends cover most of the rest, and fund the supported spots in every class.
