Café corner

Tea, coffee and cake from local bakers, pay what you can. Coffee is free for anyone at a class.

Getting here

  • By train

    Amtrak stops at Holyoke station on Main Street, with trains south to Springfield and north to Northampton and Greenfield. Walk up Main Street to Dwight Street, then along Race Street: about eight minutes, mostly flat.

  • By bus

    PVTA buses from Springfield, Chicopee and South Hadley stop at the Holyoke Transportation Center on Maple Street, about six minutes from our door.

  • By car

    There’s no parking in the yard. Use the lot on Cabot Street or the lot on Maple Street. Two accessible parking spaces are kept by the yard gate; call ahead and we’ll hold one.

  • By bike

    Six covered bike racks in the yard, and a pump at the welcome desk.

Access

Made to be easy to get around

Cass Birkett looks after access. If you’d like to talk anything through before you come, or want a photo guide to the building, get in touch.

Ask about access
  • Step-freeLevel entrance from the yard, an elevator to the second floor and wide doors throughout.
  • RestroomsAn accessible restroom with a changing table on each floor.
  • Hearing loopIn the Yard Room and at the welcome desk. One captioned film screening each season.
  • Quiet roomThe Snug is kept calm during busy sessions and open studios.
  • SeatingChairs with arms in every room, and a seated potter’s wheel.
  • CompanionsCaregivers and personal care attendants come free. Service animals are welcome everywhere.

Ask us anything

About access, a class, a booking or where to find us. We reply within two business days, or call the welcome desk during opening hours.
