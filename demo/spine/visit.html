    Our story

    A bookstore the size of a living room, mostly.

    Odessa Merriweather opened Larchmere in 1998 in what used to be a hardware store, which is why the shelves are on rollers and the floor still creaks by the poetry. The café corner arrived in 2011, when we knocked through into the old paint room.

    We’re a general bookstore with a strong lean toward fiction, nature writing and kids’ books, and we order anything in print. Every book on the front tables has been read by someone who works here.

    The booksellers

    Who you’ll find behind the counter

    • Odessa Merriweather

      Owner and buyer

      Opened Larchmere in 1998 with four thousand books and a borrowed van. Still reads every advance copy that comes in, and still buys too many hardcovers.

      Can’t stop recommending: The Long Field Almanac

    • Tobiah Renwick

      Café and events

      Runs The Margin and the events calendar. Bakes the buns at seven and introduces the authors at seven. Has strong opinions about rye.

      Can’t stop recommending: Field Guide to Leaving

    • Mireille Duchaine

      Children’s and teen buyer

      Built the kids’ corner, the rug and the Saturday story time. Can find the right picture book for any three-year-old in under a minute.

      Can’t stop recommending: Pippa Dunmore and the Badger Door

    • Augie Sorensen

      Bookseller

      Mysteries, sci-fi and every book about a small island. Hosts Mystery Night and Starfarers, and will absolutely not spoil the ending.

      Can’t stop recommending: A Map of Slow Stars

    Special orders & questions

    Looking for something?

    If a book is in print, we can almost always get it, usually in three to five business days, with no extra charge. Send us the title, or any question at all, and a bookseller will get back to you.

    Prefer to talk? Call (802) 555-0154 during opening hours.
