Venue

The Foundry Hall

A 1920s power station turned event space on Bellevue’s Meydenbauer Bay, with four stages under one roof and a rooftop terrace with a view of Lake Washington.

Address

4 Powerhouse Way, Bellevue, WA 98004

Doors

8:30 AM every day

Wi-Fi

Fast, free, everywhere

Food

Lunch & coffee included

Getting here

By air

Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA) is about an hour away by light rail: take the 1 Line downtown and change to the 2 Line for Bellevue Downtown station.

By train

From King Street Station in Seattle, walk to International District/Chinatown station and take the 2 Line to Bellevue Downtown. Amtrak trains arrive from Portland, Vancouver and Chicago.

By bike

Ride in from Seattle on the I-90 Trail across Lake Washington, or rent a bike downtown; there is covered parking for 400 bikes at the venue.

Accessibility

Everyone belongs here

We want Summit to work for every attendee. If something on this list doesn’t cover what you need, email access@summit.example and we’ll work it out together.

  • Wheelchair access to every stage, the terrace and all restrooms
  • Reserved seating near the front on request, plus companion tickets at no cost
  • Live captions on the Main Stage and Hall B screens
  • Hearing loop in the Main Stage
  • A quiet room open throughout all three days
  • Gender-neutral restrooms on every floor
  • Service animals welcome, with water bowls at the entrance
