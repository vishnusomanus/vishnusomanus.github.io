Stowe, Vermont

Pine lodge

$1,150,000

4 bedrooms · 3 baths · 2,400 sq ft

A log house set back from a quiet road, with the pines doing most of the privacy.

The lodge faces the trees rather than the road. Mornings come in through the gable windows. The ground floor is one long room, a kitchen at the end, and a porch that is usable from May. Upstairs, four bedrooms share three baths. Heat is oil, with a wood stove in the main room that the owners actually use. The land is just under three acres, surveyed last spring. Taxes last year were $9,400. The well was tested in September and the report is in the packet.
