The desk

One person, a short list.

Harbor & Co is Jonah Ellis. The office is a room on Exchange Street in Portland. The houses are not all in Maine. They are wherever a house is worth a trip and the owner will let the awkward facts onto the page.

Jonah does not take a listing he has not walked. The packet for each house — survey, taxes, insurance, the well or the septic or the plow agreement — is ready before the page goes up.

If you are selling, write first. The list stays short on purpose. If you are buying, use the form on the house. You will get the packet, not a newsletter.
