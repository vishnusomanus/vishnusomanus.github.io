What does the price include?

Labor prices include inspection of the part being worked on, installation or adjustment and a short test ride where appropriate. Prices shown don't include sales tax.

Parts allowances are estimates for everyday components. We confirm compatibility and the exact parts price before work starts. Specialty, electronic and proprietary systems may need a referral.
