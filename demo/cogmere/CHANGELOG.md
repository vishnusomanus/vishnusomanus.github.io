# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Chico, California, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.
- The drop-off calendar now closes on US federal holidays, worked out by rule for any year, and uses Pacific time.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-04

### Fixed

- A change the browser couldn't save, for example when its storage is full, now stays in place for the visit
  instead of going back to the old value.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Ten complete HTML pages for the Cogmere bicycle workshop, including the repair board, estimates, drop-off booking and pre-ride checklist.
- Sixteen original repair drawings, a bicycle illustration, a hero scene for every inner page, eight ride-note illustrations and three mechanic portraits, with Chivo and Spline Sans from Google Fonts.
- Drop-off booking and workshop question forms with inline validation and a success state with a reference.
- Validated saved repair plans and checklist progress with an in-memory fallback.
- Full customization and deployment documentation.
