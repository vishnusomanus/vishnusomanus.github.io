OUR WORKSHOP STANDARD

Better work.
Fewer loose ends.

Repair before replace.

We clean, adjust and inspect before recommending new parts. When replacement makes sense, we show you why and explain your options.

A second pair of eyes.

Safety-critical work gets a second check. Every completed bike leaves with clear notes about the work and anything to keep an eye on.

What we take on.

Road, gravel, hybrid, touring and children's bikes, with common mechanical drivetrains. We also repair tires, brakes and drivetrains on many e-bikes and cargo bikes.

We do not open e-bike motors or battery packs, service suspension internals, repair carbon frames or promise compatibility with every proprietary system. Tell us what you have before bringing it in.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your old parts tell a story.

Ask us to keep replaced parts for pickup. We can show you a worn chain, a glazed pad or a rough bearing and explain how it affects the ride.
