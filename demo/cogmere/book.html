BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Bike. Key. A little context.

  • Bring the bike reasonably clean, with lights and bags removed.
  • Bring an e-bike battery key if access is needed. We work on mechanical parts only.
  • Tell us about any recent crash, loose part or change in handling.
  • If riding feels unsafe, walk the bike or arrange transport.
