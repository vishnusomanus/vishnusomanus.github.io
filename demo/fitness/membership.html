Membership FAQ

Something else? Get in touch and we'll reply within a day.

Is there a joining fee?+

No. You pay your first month (or year) and that's it.

Can I freeze my membership?+

Yes. Freeze for up to three months a year for injury, travel or life in general, free of charge.

How do I cancel?+

Monthly plans can be canceled any time from the Keel app with 30 days' notice. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata in the first 30 days.

Can I switch plans later?+

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades start at your next billing date.

Do you offer student or corporate rates?+

Students and healthcare workers get 15% off any plan. We also have corporate packages for teams of five or more. Ask at the front desk.
