About the class

Strength Lab is where Keel members get genuinely strong. Each six-week block follows a written program built around the squat, hinge, press and pull, with a coach adjusting the load for every athlete on the platform. You'll log every set and watch the numbers climb.

What to expect

  1. 01A 10-minute movement-prep warm-up tailored to the day's lift
  2. 02Main lift: work up to the target sets with a coach checking every rep
  3. 03Accessory supersets for the muscles that support the main lift
  4. 04Log your numbers on the board and leave a few minutes to stretch

Who it's for

All levels. New lifters start on technique blocks with a lighter bar.

Equipment

  • Olympic barbell & bumper plates
  • Squat rack & platform
  • Dumbbells
  • Lifting belt (optional)
