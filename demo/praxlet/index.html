02 / A BETTER FIRST BUILD

A plan before
the first wire.

Our kit bench checks the things that are easy to miss: voltage, interfaces, power headroom and the small mechanical parts a bigger idea needs.

Open the kit bench

  1. 01 / CHOOSE

    A question small enough to test.

    Follow a line, compare two readings or make a clear state visible. Start with one thing you can explain.

  2. 02 / CHECK

    The connections between the parts.

    Make sure the modules agree on voltage and interfaces, with enough power and mounting room for the whole build.

  3. 03 / LEARN

    A result you can discuss.

    Keep a build record, repeat the test and change one thing. A useful question is more valuable than a fast finish.
