- A compatibility, power and cost kit configurator with named builds and module comparisons.
- Six practical projects, twelve field notes, three team profiles and validated inquiry success states.

- An ages 11+ and small-parts note wherever kits and modules are described.
- Original SVG artwork, Google Fonts typography and full customization documentation.
