Questions and answers

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

Usually not. Montana generally lets you see a physical therapist directly, without a doctor’s referral. This is often called direct access. Some health plans and employers still ask for a referral before they’ll pay, so check your plan if you’re using insurance. The Montana Board of Physical Therapy Examiners has the current rules.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable that lets us see and move the area: shorts for hips, knees and ankles, and a tank top or sports bra for shoulders and backs. We have shorts to borrow if you forget.

How long is each appointment?

Initial evaluations are 45 minutes and follow-ups 30 minutes. Pelvic health and running gait analysis appointments are an hour. You’ll find every length on the treatments page.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies. Some problems need one visit and a good home plan; others take six or more sessions over a few months. We’ll give you an honest idea at your first appointment and review it as you go.

Can I bring someone with me?

Of course. You’re welcome to bring a friend or family member, or ask for a chaperone. Patients under 18 must come with a parent or guardian.

Do you see children?

We see young people aged 12 and over for sports injuries and growth-related pain. For younger children we’ll suggest a pediatric physical therapist.

What if I need a scan or an injection?

If we think a scan or a specialist opinion would change your treatment, we’ll contact your doctor or help you find a specialist, and explain the options and costs.

How do I cancel or change an appointment?

Call (406) 555-0126 or reply to your confirmation. Please give us 24 hours’ notice; late cancellations are charged at half the session price.

When not to wait

Physical therapy is for problems that can wait a day or two. For numbness around your buttocks or genitals, new bladder or bowel problems, chest pain or a serious injury, call 911 or go to the ER. For anything else urgent, call your doctor or go to an urgent care clinic.
