§ Good to know

Pain, explained simply

A few things that are true for most of the people we see, whatever the area.

Hurt doesn’t always mean harm

Pain is a protective alarm, and it can stay switched on after tissues have healed. Moving within comfort is usually safe and helpful.

Scans are often not needed

Many findings on scans are normal signs of age. We’ll talk to your doctor about imaging when it would change what we do.

Little and often wins

Ten minutes of exercise most days beats an hour once a week. We’ll help you fit it into your routine.

Sleep and stress matter

Poor sleep and a stressful patch can turn the volume up on pain. They are part of the conversation too.

When not to wait

Physical therapy is for problems that can wait a day or two. For numbness around your buttocks or genitals, new bladder or bowel problems, chest pain or a serious injury, call 911 or go to the ER. For anything else urgent, call your doctor or go to an urgent care clinic.
