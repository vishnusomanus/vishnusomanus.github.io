                          When not to wait

                          Physical therapy is for problems that can wait a day or two. For numbness around your buttocks or genitals, new bladder or bowel problems, chest pain or a serious injury, call 911 or go to the ER. For anything else urgent, call your doctor or go to an urgent care clinic.
