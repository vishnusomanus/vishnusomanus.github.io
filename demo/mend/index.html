Notes for your

Lower back

Low back pain is very common and usually improves within six weeks. Gentle movement helps more than rest.

What it often is

  • Non-specific low back painAche or spasm across the lower back, worse with bending or sitting.
  • SciaticaPain, tingling or numbness running from the buttock down the leg.
  • Disc-related painPain after lifting or bending, sometimes with leg symptoms.

§ 01

When not to wait

Examples, not a full list. If you are worried, get checked.

Some symptoms need a doctor first, not a PT

Physical therapy is for problems that can wait a day or two. If you have any of these, please don’t book online.

  • Numbness around your buttocks or genitals
  • New problems controlling your bladder or bowels
  • Weakness in an arm or leg that is getting worse
  • Pain after a serious fall, crash or blow
  • A hot, swollen calf, especially after surgery
  • Pain with a fever or unexplained weight loss
Call 911 or go to the ER
For a medical emergency, including numbness in the saddle area or loss of bladder or bowel control.
Call your doctor or go to urgent care
For a problem that isn’t an emergency but shouldn’t wait for an appointment.

§ 02

Your first visit

Initial evaluation
45 minutes · $140
Usually no referral needed

What happens in the first 45 minutes

Most of an initial evaluation is listening and looking. By the end you’ll know what we think is going on, what to do about it and whether you need to come back.

Wear something you can move in: shorts for knees and hips, a tank top for shoulders. If you’re using insurance, bring your insurance card and any authorization number.

Book an initial evaluationAbout evaluations
  1. 0 min

    Arrive and settle in

    Seren checks you in. The rooms are on the main floor, with a no-step entrance from the street.

  2. 5 min

    Your story

    How it started, what makes it better or worse, your work, your sport and what you want to get back to.

  3. 15 min

    How you move

    We watch you move, test strength and joints, and check nerves where that matters.

  4. 30 min

    A plain explanation

    What we think is going on, in plain English, and how long it usually takes to settle.

  5. 35 min

    Treatment and exercises

    Hands-on treatment if it helps on the day, and two or three exercises to start at home.

  6. 45 min

    A plan you agree to

    Written down: what to do, when to check back in, and the signs that mean you should call us sooner.
