§ Good to know

Questions people ask before their first appointment.

Before your appointment

Wear clothes you can move in and that let us see the area: shorts for knees and hips, a tank top for shoulders. Bring a list of any medicines and, if you’re using insurance, your insurance card and any authorization number.

How many sessions?
It depends on the problem. Many people need three to six; some just one. We’ll tell you honestly at your first visit and never sign you up to more than you need.
Will it hurt?
Some treatments can feel uncomfortable, and you may ache for a day after. We check with you throughout and adjust anything you’re not happy with.
Chaperones
You’re welcome to bring someone with you to any appointment, or ask for a chaperone from our team.
Results
Everyone recovers differently, so we can’t promise a result. We set goals together, measure progress and change the plan if it isn’t working.

When not to wait

Physical therapy is for problems that can wait a day or two. For numbness around your buttocks or genitals, new bladder or bowel problems, chest pain or a serious injury, call 911 or go to the ER. For anything else urgent, call your doctor or go to an urgent care clinic.
