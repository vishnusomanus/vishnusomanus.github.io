What a Charleston wedding really costs in 2027

By Josephine Hale, founder

The question we hear most, usually in a whisper on the first call, is “what does it actually cost?” Here is our honest answer for a Saturday wedding in Charleston, with 120 guests, in spring or fall.

Most of the couples we planned for in 2026 spent between $52,000 and $95,000 on the whole celebration, including our fee. The biggest single line is almost always food and drink, at around a third of the total. The venue and rentals come next, then flowers and decor, photography and film, and music.

Where the money goes

  • Catering and bar: about 36% of what you spend with vendors. Charleston caterers price per guest, and the bar is often the easiest place to save.
  • Venue and rentals: about 28%. A beautiful empty room can cost more in the end than a dull one that comes with tables and chairs.
  • Florals and decor: about 12%. Seasonal, local flowers go further.
  • Photo, film and music: about 17% together, and the last place we’d cut.

Three ways to spend less

Move to a Friday, Sunday or Thursday (see below). Choose a winter date: December to February rates are often 10 to 20 percent lower. And trim the guest list: every guest you don’t invite saves around $285 at a typical Charleston wedding.

Want your own numbers? Our budget estimator uses the same figures we use on discovery calls. It’s an estimate only, but it’s a good place to start the conversation.

Hurricane season and the art of the rain plan

Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, and some of Charleston’s loveliest wedding weeks fall right inside it. That’s no reason to avoid them; it’s a reason to plan for weather the way you plan for dinner.

Every one of our events has a written rain plan, agreed with the venue in advance, with a decision time: usually 48 hours before the ceremony, when we look at the forecast together and choose. Tents are reserved, not hoped for. And for September and October dates, we suggest event insurance that covers named storms. It’s inexpensive, and it’s the best sleep you’ll buy all year.

Planning · 3 min read

The case for a Thursday wedding

Saturdays in April, May and October are booked at Charleston’s favorite venues more than a year out. Thursdays are not. Many venues discount weekday dates by 20 to 40 percent, your first-choice photographer is far more likely to be free, and guests who travel for a long weekend get one.

The trade-off is real: some guests won’t be able to take the Friday off. Send save-the-dates early (ten months is good), keep the ceremony after 5pm, and plan a lazy Friday for everyone who stays. We’ve never had a couple regret it.

Etiquette · 3 min read

Tipping your vendors: a cheat sheet

Tips are never required, always appreciated, and the question we answer every single week. These are the ranges we see in Charleston; we put the envelopes together for you and hand them out at the end of the night.

VendorTypical tip
Catering and bar staff15–20% of the food and drink bill, if not already included
Photographer and videographer$100–$200 for each person on the team
Band or DJ$50–$100 per musician, $100–$200 for a DJ
Hair and makeup15–20% of the service
Drivers and shuttles15–20% of the transport bill
Your plannerNever expected. A kind review means just as much.

General guidance, not a rule: check your contracts, as some vendors include service charges.
