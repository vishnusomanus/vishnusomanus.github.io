Dogs welcome · Good to know

Bring the dog. Bring the muddy boots.

Most of our guests come to hike, and plenty bring a four-legged companion. Here’s how dogs fit in at the Hart, and everything else that’s useful to know before you come.

Dogs at the Hart

  • Four dog-friendly rooms

    The Snug, the Hayloft, Shepherd’s Room and the Old Dairy take up to two dogs. Brook View and the Ledges Suite stay dog-free for guests with allergies. Service animals are welcome everywhere, at no charge.

  • $30 per dog per stay

    That covers a deep clean after you leave. It’s added to your price when you check a stay.

  • Welcome downstairs too

    Dogs are welcome in the snug, our fireside lounge, and at the porch tables in season. The tavern and dining room are dog-free.

  • Never alone in the room

    Please don’t leave dogs alone in the rooms. We know a dog-sitter in the village who takes them walking for the afternoon.

Waiting for your dog

  • A bed and a blanket

    And old towels for wet days, so yours stay dry.

  • Bowls and a biscuit

    Water bowls in the room and the snug; biscuits in the jar.

  • A paw wash

    A warm spigot and a brush by the porch for muddy legs.

  • Trails for every dog

    Easy riverside strolls to big ridge days.

Easy trails for dogs

On the trails: farms and hill pastures run right up to the paths, so keep dogs on a leash near livestock and in the national historical park.

Good to know

Questions we’re asked at the bar

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Check-in is from 3pm to 9pm and check-out is by 11am. If you’re arriving after 9pm, tell us and the night manager will have your key. You’re welcome to leave bags and boots with us and hike after you check out.

Is there parking?+

Yes, a free parking lot behind the inn with twelve spaces, plus one Level 2 EV charger you can reserve at the bar. The driveway is narrow; take the second entrance.

Is there Wi-Fi and cell service?+

Wi-Fi reaches every room and the garden. Cell service in the valley is patchy: you’ll usually get a bar or two by the front window and on the green.

Are the rooms accessible?+

The Old Dairy is step-free with a roll-in shower and a wide door. Shepherd’s Room is on the ground floor with one small step. The other rooms are up a steep, characterful staircase. Call us and we’ll talk it through.

Are children welcome?+

Very. The Ledges Suite and the Old Dairy sleep four, we have a travel crib and high chairs, and children’s portions of most dishes. The bar is family-friendly until 9pm.

What if we need to cancel?+

Cancel free of charge up to 7 days before you arrive. After that we charge the first night, unless we can rebook the room, in which case we refund it.

Do you cater for allergies?+

Yes. Tell us when you book and when you order. The menus mark vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and the kitchen has the full allergen list for every dish.

Can we rent the inn for a celebration?+

The barn seats forty for a meal and you can rent the whole inn for a weekend. Send us a note through the contact page.

House rules

  1. Boots off at the porch; slippers are by the stairs.
  2. Quiet in the upstairs hall after 10:30pm.
  3. Breakfast is served until 9:30am (10am on weekends).
  4. The fire is for everyone. Don’t hog the bench.
  5. No smoking or vaping indoors, including the rooms.

Anything else? Ask us.
