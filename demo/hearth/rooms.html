Rooms

Six rooms, low beams and wide pine floors

Good to know

Check-in
3pm to 9pm. Arriving later? The night manager has your key.
Check-out
11am. Leave your bags and hike; we’ll watch them.
Saturdays
Stays that include a Saturday night are two nights minimum.
Dogs
$30 per dog per stay, up to two, in the Snug, Hayloft, Shepherd’s Room and Old Dairy.
