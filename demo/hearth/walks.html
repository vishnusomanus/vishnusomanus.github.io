Trails from the door

Eight trails, no car keys needed

Every one of these starts at our porch, where there’s a boot scraper, a spigot for the dogs and a stack of route cards by the bar. Save the ones you like; we’ll remember them on this device.

Choose a trail to see its route

Ledges and roots are slick when wet, and spring brings mud season. Tell us where you’re going, carry a map and check the forecast at the bar.

  • Village green and river loop

    2 miles · 1 hour · 60 ft up

    Easy

    A flat dog walk around the green, over the Middle Covered Bridge and along the Ottauquechee, back past the white church. Good before breakfast.

    • Dog-friendly
    • Stroller in dry weather

  • Mount Tom by the Faulkner Trail

    3 miles · 2 hours · 550 ft up

    Moderate

    Up the Faulkner Trail’s long switchbacks to the South Peak, with a handrail on the last rocky pitch and the whole village at your feet. Down by the carriage road for a cup of coffee.

    • Steep final stretch
    • Famous view

  • River Road along the Ottauquechee

    4 miles · 2 hours · 130 ft up

    Easy

    East along River Road beside the Ottauquechee, past old dairy barns and hayfields. Back the same way, or turn around when the dog says so.

    • Riverside
    • Dog-friendly

  • The Pogue by the carriage roads

    4.5 miles · 2½ hours · 600 ft up

    Moderate

    Through the national historical park on the old carriage roads, past Upper Meadow and under the hemlocks to the Pogue, a fourteen-acre pond in the hills. The side trail up the ledges is optional.

    • Mountain pond
    • Old carriage roads

  • Mount Peg and Cox District Road

    6 miles · 3 hours · 850 ft up

    Moderate

    Up through the maples to the meadow on top of Mount Peg for a view over the whole valley, then out along Cox District Road and down through the hayfields.

    • Big views
    • Quiet

  • Mount Tom ridge and the Pogue

    7.5 miles · 4 hours · 1,000 ft up

    Moderate

    Over the top of Mount Tom and along the ridge to the Pogue, then home down the carriage roads through the sugar maples.

    • Pond
    • Ridge walk

  • The Woodstock big three

    9.5 miles · 5 hours · 1,400 ft up

    Strenuous

    Mount Tom, the Pogue and Mount Peg in one long, glorious day. Reserve a late table: you’ll have earned the pie.

    • Full day
    • All the sights

  • Cloudland and the Appalachian Trail

    12 miles · 6½ hours · 1,800 ft up

    Strenuous

    A long day north by back roads and hill pastures to meet the Appalachian Trail near Cloudland, with the Green Mountains laid out to the west.

    • Hill country
    • Take a map
