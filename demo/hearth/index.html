Country inn & tavern · Woodstock, Vermont · since 1794

A good fire, a cold pint and a bed in Vermont.

The Fallow Hart is an old stagecoach inn a short walk from the green in Woodstock, Vermont: six rooms, a slate-floored tavern, New England cooking and Mount Tom an easy hike from the door. Dogs and muddy boots welcome.

Today at the Hart

Open the door, watch the step, find a seat by the fire.

More than two hundred years of drovers, farmers, hikers and their dogs have worn the floors smooth. We pour Vermont ales, cook what the farms around us raise, and light the fire every day from October to April.

Food & drink

Pies, popovers and a Sunday roast worth the drive

Lamb from Windsor County farms, beef from Barnard, eggs from the hens behind the barn. The menu follows the seasons and the specials board follows whatever turned up that morning. Four New England ales on tap, all brewed within twenty miles.

Trails from the door

Lace your boots on the porch and go

All eight trails

  1. 1

    Mount Tom by the Faulkner Trail

    3 miles · Moderate

    Up the long switchbacks to the South Peak above the village, back by the carriage road.

  2. 2

    The Pogue by the carriage roads

    4½ miles · Moderate

    Through the hemlocks on the old carriage roads to the pond. Back for a late lunch.

  3. 3

    Village green and river loop

    2 miles · Easy

    A flat dog walk over the covered bridge, along the river and back past the green.

Dogs welcome

The best spot by the fire is usually taken by a dog

Four of our rooms take up to two dogs, there’s a paw wash by the porch and a jar of biscuits behind the bar. Dogs are welcome in the snug and at the porch tables; the tavern and dining room are dog-free.
