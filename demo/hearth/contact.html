Getting here

By car, train, bus or boot

By car
From I-89, take exit 1 and follow US 4 west for about ten miles into Woodstock. At the green, turn south on Route 106; Old Stage Road is a short way along on the left. Free parking lot with twelve spaces behind the inn and one EV charger.
By train
Amtrak’s Vermonter stops once a day each way at White River Junction, about 14 miles away. From there it’s 25 minutes by taxi; we can arrange one for you.
By bus
Long-distance buses stop in White River Junction. Local bus service to the village is limited, so check current schedules before you travel.
On foot
The Appalachian Trail passes a few miles north of the village. Follow Route 12 south from the trail crossing and call us if you’d like a ride.
