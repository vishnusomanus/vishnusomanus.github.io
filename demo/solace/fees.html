Sliding scale

Check your sliding-scale fee

We don’t ask for pay stubs. We trust you to choose honestly, and we review the fee together every six months.

Insurance

Is your plan in network?

We’re in network with three health plans and one employee assistance program. With any other plan, we can give you a monthly superbill to claim out-of-network benefits yourself.

Network status can change, and coverage depends on your specific plan. We always confirm your benefits before your first session.

Questions to ask your plan

  • Do I have outpatient mental health benefits?
  • What’s my deductible, and how much is left this year?
  • What do I pay per session for codes 90791 and 90837?
  • Do I have out-of-network benefits, and at what percentage?
  • Do I need pre-authorization or a referral?

Your rights

Your right to a Good Faith Estimate

Under the federal No Surprises Act, if you don’t have insurance or aren’t using it, health care providers have to give you a Good Faith Estimate of the expected cost of your care.

You can ask us for one at any time. We’ll send it before your first session and give you an updated one if anything changes, such as a new fee or a change in how often we meet.

If your bill ends up at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you can dispute the bill. Keep a copy of your estimate. For questions or more information about your right to a Good Faith Estimate, visit cms.gov/nosurprises.

This section is general information about a federal rule, not legal advice.
