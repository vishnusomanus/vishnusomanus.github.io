In crisis right now? Call or text 988, any time. In an emergency, call 911.

You don’t have to wait for an appointment.

The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is free and confidential, 24 hours a day. You can call 988, or text 988 from your phone’s messages app. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call 911. Budd Bay Counseling can’t respond to emergencies by form, email or voicemail: messages are read during office hours only.
