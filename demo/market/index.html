From our wheel to your table

Forty-one prototypes for one good pour.

Every piece starts as a lump of local clay on one of our six wheels. It is thrown, trimmed, dried for a week, bisque-fired, dipped in glaze by hand and fired again at 2300°F. Then we test it — in our own kitchens — before it ever reaches yours.

Why Kiln & Co.

  • Thrown by hand

    No molds, no factories. Small differences in every piece are the point.

  • Food-safe glazes

    Lead-free and cadmium-free, tested by an independent lab every season.

  • Plastic-free shipping

    Packed in recycled paper and cornstarch peanuts. Breakages replaced, no questions.

  • Kintsugi repairs

    Chipped a favorite? Send it back and we will mend it with gold, for life.
