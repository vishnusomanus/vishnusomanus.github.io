Orders & shipping

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave the studio within 2–3 business days. Standard delivery takes 4–6 business days in the US; express takes 1–2.

How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $75 and a flat $8 below that. Express shipping is $18.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to Canada, the UK, the EU, Australia and Japan. International rates and duties are shown at checkout.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Write to us within 12 hours of ordering and we will change or cancel it before it is packed.

Returns & breakage

What is your return policy?

Return any unused piece within 30 days for a full refund. Return shipping is free for exchanges.

Something arrived broken. What now?

We are sorry! Send a photo of the piece and the box to our team and we will ship a replacement the same week. You do not need to send it back.

Do you repair pieces?

Yes. Our kintsugi service mends chipped or broken Kiln & Co. pieces with gold-dusted lacquer, free for life. Just pay for shipping.

Care & use

Is everything dishwasher safe?

All mugs, bowls and plates are dishwasher safe. Hand-washing keeps glazes glossy for longer. Vases and planters should be washed by hand.

Can I use your pieces in the microwave or oven?

Mugs, bowls and plates are microwave safe. The Wide Serving Bowl is oven safe to 425°F. Avoid moving pieces straight from the fridge into a hot oven.

Are your glazes food safe?

Yes. All of our glazes are lead-free and cadmium-free, and they are tested by an independent lab every season.

Why does my piece look different from the photo?

Every piece is thrown and glazed by hand, so the color, speckling and shape vary a little. We think that is the best part.

Wholesale & custom

Do you sell to cafés and restaurants?

We do. We make custom runs from 50 pieces with your choice of glaze. Use the form below and choose “Wholesale”.

Can I order a wedding registry?

Yes — we offer registries with a 10% thank-you discount for the couple after the big day.
