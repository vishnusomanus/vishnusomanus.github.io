Our studio

A small studio with a very big kiln.

Kiln & Co. began in 2014 with one secondhand wheel in a garage on Tacoma’s East Side. Today twelve potters make everything you see here, by hand, in a converted bakery with a lot of windows.

We make the things we wanted to buy and couldn’t find.

Our founder, Ruth Calloway, was a line cook who got tired of plates that chipped in a month and mugs too thin to hold heat. She took an evening class, bought a wheel from a retiring teacher and started selling at the Saturday market.

The Daily Mug sold out every week. Then a café ordered two hundred. Then a waitlist of four thousand people taught us that “handmade” and “everyday” were not opposites — people wanted both.

We still work the same way: local clay, glazes mixed in-house, pieces fired slowly and checked by the person who made them. We just have more wheels now, and a solar roof that runs the kiln.

Twelve years at the wheel

  1. 2014

    One wheel, one garage, one very patient neighbor.

  2. 2017

    First studio and first hire. The Daily Mug is born.

  3. 2020

    The waitlist hits 4,000 and the online shop opens.

  4. 2023

    We move into the old bakery and go solar.

  5. 2026

    Twelve potters, six wheels and the kintsugi repair service.
