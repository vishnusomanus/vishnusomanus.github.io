How the planner works it out

The bed is divided into equal squares, one per plant, with the spacing as the side of each square. Each plant sits in the middle of its square, so the outer plants are half a spacing from the edge, where they won’t crowd a path.

Spacings are the ones we use at the plot for a well-fed bed. Seed packets sometimes give a row spacing and a plant spacing; if yours are different, follow the packet. The count is an estimate, not a crop guarantee: light, soil and slugs all have a say.
