Getting here

By car

Take SE 3rd Street south from downtown, then follow SE Alder Bend Road east past the last houses; our gate is the green one in the brick wall on the left. Free parking for 14 cars inside, with two wide spaces by the barn.

By train or bus

The nearest Amtrak station is in Albany, about 12 miles away. Corvallis Transit buses from the Downtown Transit Center stop a fifteen-minute walk from the gate. Tell us when you enroll and we can usually arrange a ride from the station.

By bike or on foot

There is a covered bike rack beside the barn. On foot, it is a ten-minute walk from Willamette Park along quiet sidewalks.

Accessibility statement

A garden for more people.

The plot is on level ground. From the parking lot to the barn and the teaching beds is about 260 feet of firm, rolled gravel; on request we open the lower gate so you can park right beside the barn.

The barn has step-free access, a wheelchair-accessible restroom with grab bars, heating, and seats with and without arms. Six of the teaching beds are raised to 30 inches with knee space beneath, and there is always a stool at the potting bench.

The orchard and the hedge line are on rough grass, which can be soft and muddy from November to March. The pruning and hedge-laying courses spend most of the day there.

Service animals are welcome. Handouts are available in large print, and we can send them in advance. If anything would help, tell us when you enroll or call the office; we would much rather plan it with you beforehand.

Booking terms

Booking and cancellation.

  • a.Your place is confirmed once we email you and your payment arrives. We hold a requested place for five days.
  • b.Cancel 14 days or more before the course and we refund you in full or move you to another date, whichever you prefer.
  • c.From 13 to 7 days before, we move you to another date at no charge, or refund half the fee.
  • d.Within 7 days we can’t refund, but you are welcome to send someone in your place. Just tell us their name.
  • e.If we have to cancel for weather or illness, you choose a full refund or a new date. We decide by 4pm the day before and call everyone.

Student safety

Who our courses are for.

Our courses are for adults aged 18 and over. Young people aged 14 to 17 are welcome on beginner courses when a parent or guardian is enrolled alongside them.

All three instructors have passed background checks and hold current outdoor first-aid certificates. Halima Fennimore is our student safety lead; you can reach her in confidence through the office.

Questions people ask

Do I need my own tools?

No. We supply hand tools, pruners, gloves in several sizes, compost and seeds. If you have a favorite trowel, bring it along.

What happens if it rains?

We carry on in light rain and move into the barn or hoop house when it pours. For storms, high winds or snow we postpone and call you by 4pm the day before.

Is lunch included?

Tea, coffee and cake are. Please bring a bag lunch on full-day courses; there is a fridge and a kettle in the barn.

Can I give a course as a gift?

Yes. Gift certificates for any amount never expire and can be used on any course. Call the office or send us a message.

Can I visit before I book?

Of course. Call the office to arrange a weekday visit and someone will show you around the barn, the beds and the paths.
