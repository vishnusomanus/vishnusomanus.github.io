About the school

Less perfect gardens. More happy growers.

Loamstead is a small, independent gardening school on a walled plot on the south side of Corvallis. We believe useful skills grow best when nobody is afraid to ask a question.

A note from Eudora

I started with one pot, and rather too much water.

Before teaching, I spent ten years growing vegetables in a narrow rented yard. I tried seed trays on every windowsill, made compost in a borrowed bin and learned that a failed crop is a useful note, not a verdict.

Loamstead began in 2014 as a few Saturday afternoons around a neighbor’s raised bed. When the old market garden behind the brick wall on SE Alder Bend Road came up for rent, we took it on, cleared the brambles with our first students and built the beds you will work in today.

We still teach small groups, explain the why as well as the how, and send everyone home with something growing and a practical next step.

Eudora Wickmere

How we work

Three things we stand by.

01

Use what you have

Good growing doesn’t need a shopping list of gadgets. We start with your space, your time and your budget, and we never sell you anything on a course.

02

Notice before doing

Watch the light, touch the soil, look at the leaves. A little observation is worth a lot of rushing, so every course starts with a slow walk around the plot.

03

Leave room for nature

We feed the soil, leave the nettles in one corner and lay our own hedge. A garden is a shared space, and the garter snakes deal with our slugs.

Come as you are.

Complete beginners, serial plant rescuers and quietly curious people are all welcome. You never need to know the right words.
