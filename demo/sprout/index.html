    How it works

    Three steps to muddy boots.

    1. 01

      Pick a course and a date

      Every course page shows its next four dates and how many places are left. Save a few to your shortlist while you decide.

    2. 02

      Request your places

      Tell us who is coming and anything that would make the day easier. We confirm by email within one business day, with arrival notes and payment details.

    3. 03

      Show up in boots

      We provide the tools, gloves, seeds, compost and tea. You bring a bag lunch and whatever questions you have been saving up.

    Your space has potential

    A windowsill counts. So does a bucket.

    Our bed planner works out how many plants will comfortably fit in your bed, draws them to scale and points you to the course that teaches that crop, before you buy a single seed.
