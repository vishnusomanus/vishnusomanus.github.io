    How dates move

    A federal deadline on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday moves to the next business day. Legal holidays include those in Washington, DC, so DC Emancipation Day (April 16) can push the April 15 deadline back. Tennessee deadlines move for weekends and state holidays, including Good Friday.
