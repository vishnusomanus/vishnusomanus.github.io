What the letters mean

Credentials matter when something goes wrong. Here’s what ours let us do for you.

CPA
Certified Public Accountant, licensed by the state. Can prepare returns, represent you before the IRS and issue financial statements.
EA
Enrolled Agent, licensed by the IRS itself, with unlimited rights to represent taxpayers in audits, collections and appeals.
CPP
Certified Payroll Professional: tested on payroll law, withholding and year-end reporting.
