    03 — Tennessee

    No income tax on wages. Still plenty to file.

    Tennessee doesn’t tax your paycheck, but it does tax businesses, sales and, for commuters, the state next door might tax you.

    What you still file in Tennessee

    Households

    A federal return, plus a Georgia or Alabama nonresident return if you work across the line.

    LLCs and corporations

    Franchise & excise tax every year, with quarterly estimates once the tax reaches $5,000.

    Anyone who sells

    Sales tax (9.25% combined in Chattanooga) and the state, county and city business tax.
