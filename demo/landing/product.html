Product

A board, a digest, and a door for clients.

Nimbus does not try to be the place you write, draw, or bill. It is the list. Everything else can stay where it is.

Leave the dashboard in the other tab.

Studios already have a place for traffic, revenue, and the numbers. Nimbus does not redraw them. The board is for the work those numbers depend on: who is doing it, and whether it will be done on Thursday.

Cards have a name, a due time, and a lane. Files can be attached. Comments are a thread, not a second meeting.

What a week looks like

  1. Monday. The digest lists what is due and what moved over the weekend.
  2. Midweek. Review lives on the card. The client lane updates when you move a card into it, not before.
  3. Friday. Finished cards archive themselves after seven days, so the board does not become a scrapbook.
