Getting to Hollowridge

Hollowridge House sits at the edge of Rhinebeck, a small village in the Hudson Valley, an hour and forty minutes from New York City by train. Here's everything you need to get there and somewhere lovely to sleep.

Getting there

By train

Direct Amtrak trains from New York Penn Station to Rhinecliff run several times a day (about 1 h 40 min). The station is a 10-minute taxi ride from the house.

By car

Take Route 9 to the village of Rhinebeck, then Mill Road past the church. Free parking in the meadow; you can leave cars overnight.

The shuttle

A shuttle bus runs from the station to the house at 1:45pm on Saturday, then home again in the evening.

While you're in the valley

  • Walk to the oak

    The path from the Swan Inn up Lookout Hill takes 40 minutes. The old oak is at the top.

  • Sunday market

    Rhinebeck's farmers market runs on Sunday mornings in the village. Get there early for the cinnamon buns.

  • Swim at Mill Bend

    A calm bend in the Landsman Kill with a pebbly beach. Bring a towel and some courage.

  • Mills Mansion

    Gilded Age mansion and park on the Hudson, a 15-minute drive away. Lovely on Sunday afternoon.
