The weekend

Schedule

Friday to Sunday in Rhinebeck. You're welcome at every part of it; the ceremony is the one not to miss.

Friday June 18Saturday June 19Sunday June 20

All times are Eastern time (EDT). Calendar files work with Google, Apple and Outlook calendars.

FridayJune 18

Arrive & unwind

  1. 7pm – 10:30pm

    Welcome drinks

    The Swan Inn, Rhinebeck Relaxed

    Arrive whenever works for you. Pizzas from the wood oven, local hard cider and a first hello before the big day. Everyone is welcome, including little ones.

    Map & directions

SaturdayJune 19

The wedding day

  1. 3pm – 3:45pm

    The ceremony

    The Walled Garden, Hollowridge House Garden formal

    Please take your seats by 2:40pm. The ceremony is outdoors on the lawn; in rain we move into the Orangery next door.

    Map & directions

  2. 3:45pm – 5:30pm

    Drinks & canapés

    The Orchard, Hollowridge House Garden formal

    Elderflower spritz, lawn games and a string trio under the apple trees while we steal away for photos.

    Map & directions

  3. 5:30pm – 8pm

    Wedding dinner

    The Long Barn, Hollowridge House Garden formal

    Three courses from the kitchen garden, speeches (short ones, we promise) and the cutting of a very tall lemon cake.

    Map & directions

  4. 8pm – 12:30am

    Dancing

    The Long Barn, Hollowridge House Bring flats

    First dance at 8pm, then a live band until 11 and a DJ playing your song requests until the last song at 12:30am.

    Map & directions

SundayJune 20

Slow morning

  1. 11am – 2pm

    Farewell brunch

    The Orangery, Hollowridge House Whatever you slept in

    Bacon, egg and cheese rolls, pastries, strong coffee and tired, happy faces before everyone heads home.

    Map & directions
