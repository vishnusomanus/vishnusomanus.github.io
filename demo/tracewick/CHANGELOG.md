## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Cary, North Carolina, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Nine complete HTML pages for an observability platform, including the service console, pricing, engineering guides, reliability and contact.
- Fourteen service illustrations, trace and service-map artwork, team portraits and social artwork.
- Saved service watchlists, per-service alert budgets and usage estimates with storage fallbacks.
- A trace waterfall dialog for every workbench trace, with spans across services, the slow or failed span marked
  and attributes for the selected span.
- Current component status, 90-day uptime bars and a dated incident history on the reliability page.
- Rolling maintenance windows, accessible interaction patterns and full documentation.
