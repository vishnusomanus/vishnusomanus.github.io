Questions about fees

Is sales tax included?OpenClose

No. Prices here are before sales tax. If any applies to your project, it’s shown as its own line on the invoice.

How do I pay?OpenClose

40% on signing to book the dates, the balance on delivery, by ACH or check within 14 days. Projects over $20,000 can split the balance at styleframes and delivery.

Who owns the finished film?OpenClose

You do, for any use, worldwide, once the balance is paid. Rillmark keeps the right to show it in the reel and on this site. Fonts and stock are licensed in your name.

What about music?OpenClose

For music videos the track is yours. For everything else, Calum Torrance can write an original score, quoted separately, or we license library music at cost.

Can you start sooner?OpenClose

Sometimes. Short jobs of a week or two can slot in between projects. Ask on the inquiry form and say what date is fixed.
