Questions

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 9 to 14 months ahead. Portrait sessions usually have availability within three weeks.

Do you travel?

Yes, anywhere. Within California travel is included; elsewhere there's a flat travel fee and flights are booked together.

When will we see our photographs?

A preview of twenty frames within a week, and the full gallery within six weeks.

How does the deposit work?

A 30% deposit secures your date. The balance is due a month before the wedding.
