Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of any medicines you take, and your completed forms if you filled them in at home.

How early should I arrive?

About 10 minutes before your appointment if you’re new, so we can say hello and check your details without rushing.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely, and you’re far from alone. Tell us when you book. We can schedule a longer first visit, explain each step before it happens and agree a stop signal. Sedation options are available for some treatments.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice by phone or email so we can offer the slot to someone else. We understand things come up.

Do you see children?

Yes, from their first tooth. Dr. Raman leads our children’s care, and kids under 3 are seen free.

Is the building accessible?

Yes. We have step-free access from the parking lot, a wide-door accessible restroom, a hearing loop at reception and one treatment room with extra space for wheelchairs.

Your privacy

We keep your health records secure and only share them with your permission or where the law requires. You can ask for a copy of your records at any time. For our full privacy notice, ask at reception or call us at (503) 555-0142.
