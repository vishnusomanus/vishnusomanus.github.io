Services & costs

Treatments explained, with honest prices

Every treatment starts with a conversation and a written estimate. These are typical self-pay ranges; your exact cost depends on your needs and insurance.

Preventive care Restorative Cosmetic Urgent care

Preventive care

Keep small problems small with regular check-ups, cleanings and advice that fits your life.

Check-up & cleaning$95 – $180

A full exam, gentle scale and polish, and digital X-rays when needed. Most visits take about an hour.

Typical cost: $95 – $180Usually covered 100% by insurance

Children’s dentistry$65 – $140

Relaxed first visits, check-ups, fluoride and sealants for kids from their first tooth to their teens.

Typical cost: $65 – $140Kids under 3 see us free

Gum (periodontal) care$220 – $480

Deep cleaning and maintenance for bleeding or receding gums, with a plan to keep them healthy.

Typical cost: $220 – $480Per quadrant, where needed

Restorative

Repair damaged or missing teeth so they look natural and work properly again.

Tooth-colored fillings$150 – $300

Composite fillings matched to your tooth shade, usually done in a single visit.

Typical cost: $150 – $300Per tooth, depending on size

Crowns & bridges$950 – $1,400

Strong ceramic crowns designed from a 3D scan, so no gooey impressions.

Typical cost: $950 – $1,400Per crown

Saves an infected tooth with modern rotary tools and plenty of numbing. Most people say it felt like a filling.

Typical cost: $750 – $1,200Front to back teeth

A permanent, natural-looking replacement for a missing tooth, planned with 3D imaging.
