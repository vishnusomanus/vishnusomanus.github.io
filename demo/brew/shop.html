    Good to know

    Bring photo ID
    Oregon law means we check ID on every beer pickup, and we can’t hand beer to anyone under 21 or anyone who’s had too much.
    Crowlers are filled to order
    We fill and seam your 32 oz can at pickup, from whatever you chose on the board. Drink it within a week.
    10¢ deposit
    Oregon’s bottle bill adds 10¢ per can or bottle. Bring empties back to any redemption center or drop them in our blue bin.
