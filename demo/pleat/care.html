Before a small problem grows

A five-minute wardrobe check.

Turn a favorite garment inside out. Check the underarm seams, pocket corners and the bottom of the zipper. A loose stitch now can become a split seam later.

If something needs a second pair of eyes, add a repair to your plan. There is no need to know the right technical word.
