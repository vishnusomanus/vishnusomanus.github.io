What the price includes

Pinning, the agreed alteration, matching thread, pressing and a pickup fitting. Replacement zipper and lining materials are included in their listed prices. Extra work is discussed first.

When is a fitting needed?

Anything changing the shape of a garment needs a fitting. For a straight repair or matching pants length, bring a well-fitting reference pair. Bridal and major redesign work begins with an individual consultation.
