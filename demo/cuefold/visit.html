Before the lights go down

Give the evening some breathing room.

On foot & by bicycle

We’re downtown, a short walk from Beverley Street. East Johnson Street has a gentle slope. Bicycle parking is available in the courtyard; bring your own lock.

By bus & car

Downtown trolley and bus stops are around Beverley Street. Public parking garages and lots are signed from the approach roads. There is no audience parking lot on site; allow time for the walk through downtown.

Drop-off & step-free arrival

Ask the box office about the nearest suitable drop-off point before you travel. The main entrance, lobby and both performance spaces are step-free.

Your evening, timed well

A little less rushing.

Choose a curtain time and your travel time. We’ll work backward, with a little room for finding the door and settling in.

A drink, a snack, a conversation

The lobby serves tea, coffee, soft drinks and a small selection of bottled drinks. There are sweet and savory snacks, but no full kitchen. You’re welcome to bring a refillable water bottle.

Running late or need to change plans?

Call the box office as soon as you can. Late entry depends on the production and may mean waiting for a suitable pause. Ticket changes are subject to availability; please ask us before traveling.
