04 / Access at Cuefold

A welcome that
works for you.

You know what helps you enjoy an evening. Here are the details, without the guesswork. If something isn’t covered, we’re happy to talk it through.

Your route through the theater

A guide to the first-floor spaces. The route from the entrance to the main room has no steps.

From sidewalk to seat

The East Johnson Street entrance has a level threshold and a 37-inch clear opening. The lobby, main room, studio, quiet room and accessible restroom are on the first floor.

A place that suits you

There are four removable-seat wheelchair spaces in the main room and two in the studio. Companion seating is alongside. Ask for an aisle seat or an end-of-row place when you contact us.

Room to pause

The quiet room is open from 45 minutes before curtain. You can leave and re-enter any performance with help from an usher; we’ll show you the easiest route.

Find your performance

Different ways into the story.

Captioned performances

A caption screen carries dialogue and important sound information. The listing marks which performances include captions; tell us if you need a clear view of the screen.

Audio description

A live description fills in visual detail through a headset. A short touch tour is offered 45 minutes before selected shows, subject to the production’s set.

Relaxed performances

House lights stay gently raised, sound levels are softened and there is freedom to move. A visual story is available from the box office before your visit.

What would be useful?

Before you arrive

Can a service dog come with me?

Yes. Service dogs are welcome in all public spaces and in the auditorium. Tell us when you request tickets so we can allow comfortable space, or arrange for an usher to look after your dog during the show.

Is hearing support available at every show?

Assistive listening devices are available in both rooms. Please ask an usher before taking your seat. Captioning and audio description are offered at the specifically marked performances.

What should I know about light and sound?

Each show page lists the effects and themes we currently know about. Productions can develop during rehearsal, so call the box office if you need a specific answer before deciding.
