About

Hello, I’m Pipa.

I grew up in Louisville drawing on the back of my mother’s church bulletins, studied printmaking, and spent six years as a staff illustrator at a magazine before going freelance in 2019. That year I bought a secondhand two-drum riso, rented a room above a bike shop on Frankfort Ave, and called it Pomelo Press, after the fruit I was eating when the lease came through.

These days I split the week between commissions and the press. Editors and art directors hire me for pictures with a point of view; publishers for picture books that children can read in the dark; food and drink makers for packaging that looks handmade because it was.

Dez Arbogast runs the press two days a week and packs every shop order. If you’ve bought a print, Dez folded the tissue.

Process

Every picture is three drawings stacked up

  1. 1. Pencil

    Thumbnails, then one tight drawing with every overlap planned.

  2. 2. Plates

    Each ink redrawn as its own black-and-white layer.

  3. 3. Press

    Proofed on the riso, one pass per ink, to check the overprints.

  4. 4. Post

    Print-ready files to your printer, or an edition from the studio.

Tools

  • A two-drum riso, 2009 vintage, named Doris
