Good to know

Questions about an order? Email shop@pomelopress.example or call (502) 555-0186. Dez, who packs every order, will answer.

Why do no two prints look the same?+

A riso prints one ink per pass through the drum, and the paper never lands in exactly the same place twice. Each copy is a little off register and a little speckled. That’s the charm, and why every print is signed and numbered.

Do the colors fade?+

Fluorescent pink fades faster than the other inks in direct sun. Frame prints behind UV glass, or hang them on a wall that doesn’t catch the afternoon light.

Can I return something?+

Yes, within 30 days if it arrives in the condition it left in. If a print is damaged in the mail, send a photo and we’ll send another from the same edition.

Do you ship outside the US?+

Not through the shop yet. Email us and we’ll quote international postage by hand.
