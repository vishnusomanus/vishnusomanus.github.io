Work / Picture books

Otto and the Upside-Down Moon

A 32-page picture book about a boy who climbs onto his roof to turn the moon the right way up.

Client
Dovetail Books
Kind
Picture books
Year
2026
Inks
  • Federal blue, yellow, fluorescent pink

From sketch to print

The pencil stage: every plate drawn as line on one sheet, so the composition and the overlaps are agreed before any ink goes down. Clients see two or three of these.

Each ink is its own drawing, printed in its own pass through the machine. On their own they look strange; on top of each other they make every other color.

The finished print, with the plates a hair off register the way a riso leaves them. Switch an ink off to see what it adds.

All 3 inks printed.

The brief

Wendeline Saltmarsh’s text is only 410 words long, so the pictures carry the night.

Delivered

  • 32 pages and cover
