What I do

Four kinds of job, one way of drawing

Every picture is drawn as separate ink plates, the way a riso prints. It keeps the color bold, the files simple for printers, and the work looking like it was made by a person.

  • Editorial

    Covers, spots and openers for magazines and weeklies, usually turned around in two weeks.

    3 projects in editorial

  • Picture books

    Thirty-two pages of night skies, buses and mice, from thumbnails to color separations.

    3 projects in picture books

  • Packaging

    Tins, labels and wrappers that sit together on a shelf and stand out across a room.

    3 projects in packaging

  • Posters

    Gig, film and fair posters, printed here in editions of a few hundred or sent to your printer.

    3 projects in posters

How a commission runs

From a brief to print-ready plates

You tell me about the job, I send a fee and a timeline, and we start on a Monday. New projects can start from Monday, November 16.

Get an estimate

  1. 1

    Brief and quote

    A short call, then a fee, a license and dates in writing.

  2. 2

    Sketches

    Two or three pencil ideas. You pick one, or a mix.

  3. 3

    Color and plates

    Finals drawn as separate ink plates, with one round of changes.

  4. 4

    Files or prints

    Print-ready files for your printer, or an edition from the studio riso.
