co/ Company

We build the plumbing so you don’t have to.

Forge started in 2021 in an Erie co-working space, after our founders spent three years rebuilding the same webhook system at three different companies: a queue, a retry loop, a signing scheme, a log table nobody could search.

Today 34 people in Erie, Bellingham and remote across nine time zones run it for more than 2,400 engineering teams. We’re profitable, independent and plan to stay boring.

01/ Principles

Four rules we argue with, then follow.

  1. 01

    Never lose an event.

    If we answered 202, the event exists in three places. Every design review starts with “how could this drop a message?”

  2. 02

    Boring beats clever.

    Postgres, a queue, well-understood failure modes. We spend our novelty budget on developer experience, not on the database.

  3. 03

    Publish the numbers.

    Uptime, latency, incidents and postmortems are public, including the embarrassing ones. You’re putting your name on our delivery.

  4. 04

    Docs are the product.

    An API nobody can figure out is an outage with better uptime. Engineers write the docs for the features they ship.
