Delivering webhooks for 2,400+ engineering teams

01/ Platform

Everything between your app and their endpoint.

Webhooks look like one HTTP request. In production they’re queues, signatures, retries, rate limits, logs and a support inbox. Forge is all of that behind one endpoint.

  • POST /forge HTTP/1.1
    forge-id: msg_2Lq8vX4m
    forge-timestamp: 1760521267
    forge-signature: v1,K5oZfzN95Z9UVu1Ex
    ✓ HMAC-SHA256 verified

    Signed by default

    Every request carries a timestamped HMAC signature. Our SDKs verify it in one line and reject replays older than five minutes.

  • Retries that back off

    Failed deliveries retry on an exponential schedule for up to 72 hours. Endpoints that keep failing are paused and their owners emailed, not hammered.

  • $ forge replay --status 5xx --since 2h
    matched 1,284 events · 2 endpoints
    ████████████████░░░░ 81%
    ✓ requeued 1,284 events
    oldest: evt_2Lp1… (1h 58m ago)

    Replay anything

    Every attempt is logged for 30 days with headers and body. Replay one event, or everything that failed while a customer’s server was down.

  • Fan-out with filters

    Endpoints subscribe to event types with wildcards and payload filters. You send each event once; Forge works out who gets it.

  • Per-endpoint rate limits

    Set a ceiling per endpoint and Forge smooths bursts into a steady stream, so a billing run never takes down a customer’s small server.

    A portal for your customers

    Drop in the embeddable portal and your customers add endpoints, pick events, rotate secrets and read their own delivery logs. Fewer tickets for you.

02/ How it works

One request in. Every endpoint served.

  1. 01 · Persist

    Your event is written to three availability zones before we answer 202. If we said yes, it will be delivered.

  2. 02 · Route

    Subscriptions and payload filters decide which endpoints get it. Idempotency keys stop duplicates at the door.

  3. 03 · Sign

    Each endpoint has its own secret. Rotate it with a 24-hour overlap and no dropped requests.

  4. 04 · Deliver

    Delivered with retries, throttling and a full log of every attempt, from the region your data lives in.
