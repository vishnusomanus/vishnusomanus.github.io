Budgets and pockets

It's this week's share of your budget, minus bills due before Sunday and money already spent, plus anything rolled over.

Each card purchase is rounded up to the nearest $1, $2 or $5 and the difference moves into a pocket you choose.

On Plus and Family you can lock any pocket until a date. You can still unlock it early after a 24-hour cool-off.
