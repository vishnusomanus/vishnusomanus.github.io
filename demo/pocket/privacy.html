Privacy policy

Last updated September 1, 2026 · Applies to the Pocket apps and pocket.example

On this page

  1. The short version
  2. What we collect
  3. How we use it
  4. Who we share it with
  5. How long we keep it
  6. Your rights
  7. Contact us

1. The short version

  • We collect what we need to run Pocket and nothing more.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and app settings.
  • Financial data from accounts you connect: balances, transactions and merchant names, through read-only connections.
  • Device information needed for security, such as app version and sign-in history.

3. How we use it

  • To categorize transactions, calculate budgets and power insights.
  • To send reminders you turn on, like bill alerts and weekly check-ins.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent fraud.

4. Who we share it with

  • Bank connection partners, only to fetch your read-only data.
  • Infrastructure providers that host Pocket under strict contracts.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires and after careful review.

5. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, and for 30 days after you delete it, so you can change your mind.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics may be kept longer; they can't identify you.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, export or delete your data from Settings → Privacy.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features at any time.
  • Complain to the Federal Trade Commission or your state attorney general.

7. Contact us

  • Email privacy@pocket.example, or write to Pocket Money, Inc., 12 River Street, Tulsa, OK 74103.

This policy is a template. Have it reviewed by a lawyer for your product and state before publishing.
