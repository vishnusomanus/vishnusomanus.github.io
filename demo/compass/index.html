The Bearing Method

Four points, one direction.

Every engagement follows the same four points of the compass. Not a script: a way of making sure we never skip from “what’s wrong” straight to “try harder”.

How the method works
  1. N

    1. Notice

    Where are you really? A 360° listening tour, a week of calendar data and an honest look at the habits that got you here.

  2. E

    2. Envision

    Where are you heading? We define the leader you want to be in a year, in specific behaviors your team would notice.

  3. S

    3. Strengthen

    Build the two or three skills that close the gap: delegation, feedback, saying no, running a meeting that ends with a decision.

  4. W

    4. Walk it

    Practice in the real job between sessions, with small experiments, a weekly check-in and someone noticing when you slip back.
