Eight weekly sessions with seven other new managers from different companies. Short teaching, lots of practice, and a group you’ll keep calling long after week eight.

    Delegation and trust

    The delegation ladder, and how to hand off without hovering.

  3. Weeks 5–6

    Feedback both ways

    Clear, kind feedback, practiced live with peers until it feels normal.

  4. Weeks 7–8

    Managing up and out

    Your manager, your peers, and a 90-day plan you present to the group.
