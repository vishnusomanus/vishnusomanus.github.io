My story

The manager I needed at 29.

My first management job came with a bigger title, nine direct reports and no training at all. For a year I did my old job and my new one, badly, mostly after 9pm. I thought that was what leading looked like.

A coach my company hired for the leadership team changed that in about four sessions. She didn’t tell me what to do. She asked what I was still holding on to, and why. By the end of that year I was home for dinner and my team was doing the best work of their careers.

I went on to run operations at a logistics software company through its growth from 60 to 400 people, then trained as a coach and opened Bearing in 2017. The studio is in the Strip District, five minutes from the river.

Credentials

Trained, accredited and supervised.

  • Professional-level coaching credential

    Accredited by an international coaching federation, with 500+ logged client hours and a recorded, assessed session.

  • MSc, Organizational Psychology

How I work

Four promises to every client

  • 01

    Candor, with care

    I’ll tell you what I see, kindly and clearly. You’re paying for honesty, not applause.

  • 02

    Practical over profound

    Every session ends with something you’ll do differently this week.

  • 03

    Your answers, not mine

    I bring tools and questions. You decide what kind of leader you want to be.

  • 04

    Confidential, always

    What you say stays between us, even when your employer is paying.
