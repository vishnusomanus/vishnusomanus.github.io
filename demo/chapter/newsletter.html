The Marsh Letter

Four letters a year, written by Clemency herself and sent from her desk in Salem. Never more, occasionally late.

  • i.News first. Titles, jackets and publication days before they are announced anywhere else.
  • ii.The real places. Photographs and notes from the churches, tide gates and beaches behind each book.
  • iii.A short story. Every letter ends with a piece of fiction you won’t find in the books.
  • iv.Events near you. Tell us where you are and you’ll hear about readings in your region.

The night the creeks froze

Short story: The Ferry Bell
