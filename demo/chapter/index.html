Chapter One

A Lamp in the Reeds

The marsh had frozen overnight, and in the first light it looked like a floor of broken windows.

Detective Sergeant Esme Lark left the car on the causeway and walked the last half-mile, because the track to Fennick Landing had not been plowed in living memory and she did not trust it.

The series

The Esme Lark mysteries

Detective Sergeant Esme Lark was born on the Great Marsh, left it at eighteen and came back to police it. Each book is a complete case, but her story runs through all four. Start with The Drowned Church.

    The author

    Clemency Thorne

    Clemency grew up above a tavern on the Great Marsh, spent eleven years covering the courts for a newspaper in Newburyport and now writes in a narrow house in Salem with a view of a parking lot and, if she leans, the harbor.

    Her first novel, The Drowned Church, won the Sandpiper Award for best debut. Her books have been translated into fourteen languages.
