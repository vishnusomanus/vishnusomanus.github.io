Biographies

Short biography (50 words)

Clemency Thorne is the author of the Esme Lark mysteries, set on the Great Marsh, and two standalone novels. A former court reporter, she won the Sandpiper Award for her debut, The Drowned Church. Her books are translated into fourteen languages. She lives in Salem, Massachusetts.

Long biography (150 words)

Clemency Thorne grew up above a tavern on the Great Marsh in Massachusetts and spent eleven years covering the courts for a newspaper in Newburyport before turning to fiction. Her first novel, The Drowned Church, introduced Detective Sergeant Esme Lark and won the Sandpiper Award for best debut.

Three more Esme Lark mysteries have followed: Salt and Shingle, Low Tide at Plum Island and The Glass Marsh. She has also written two standalone novels: The Cartographer’s Widow, a finalist for the Harrowell Prize, and the bestselling Every Bell in Salem.

Her books are published by Finch & Holloway and translated into fourteen languages, and the screen rights to the Esme Lark series have been optioned. She lives in Salem and swims at Salem Willows all year round.
