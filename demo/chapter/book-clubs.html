    6 questions

      Some questions touch on the ending, so save them for when everyone has finished.

      Hosting a good evening

      i.

      Start with the place

      Ask everyone to pick one passage where the landscape did something to them. It gets quiet members talking, and nobody can be wrong.

      ii.

      Vote on the culprit

      Before you discuss the ending, go around the room: who did each person suspect, and at which page? Clemency loves hearing the answers.

      iii.

      Bring something salty

      Oyster crackers, potato chips, a bowl of steamers if you’re brave. Turner Street Books offers 10 percent off sets of six or more copies for book clubs.

      Online visits

      Invite Clemency to your book club

      A few times a year, Clemency joins reading groups by video call for half an hour of questions. Groups anywhere in the world are welcome; tell us which book you’re reading and roughly when you meet.
