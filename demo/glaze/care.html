Looking after stoneware

  • Everyday washing

    Four of our five glazes are happy in the dishwasher. Burnt earth is a dry, satin glaze: wash it by hand to keep the sheen.

  • Hot and cold

    Microwave safe, but not oven or stovetop safe. Avoid going straight from the fridge into boiling water: stoneware dislikes sudden changes.

  • The unglazed foot

    We sand every foot smooth, but it is raw clay. Put a pad under vases on a polished table, and dry the foot before stacking.

  • Little marks

    Gray silverware marks on chalk and oat glazes come off with a dab of baking soda paste. They are on the surface, not in the glaze.

Glaze by glaze

All of our glazes are lead-free and food safe, and tested at the studio on every batch.

    Returns and breakages

    Something arrived broken+

    It happens, rarely. Send a photo to hello@vennickclay.example within seven days and we’ll send a replacement or refund you, whichever you’d prefer. No need to ship the pieces back.

    Changing your mind+

    You can return unused pieces within 30 days for a full refund. Wrap them as well as we did, and send them back to 228 N Mesquite Street, Las Cruces, NM 88001. Made-to-order pieces can be returned too.

    Seconds+

    Seconds are sold in the shop on kiln-opening Saturdays, a third off. They are perfectly usable, just not quite what we meant. We can’t take seconds back, so take a good look before you buy.

    Workshop spots+

    Tell us at least seven days before and we’ll move you to another date or refund you. After that, you’re welcome to send a friend in your place.
