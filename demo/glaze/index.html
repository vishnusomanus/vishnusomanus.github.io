      A little less perfect

      There’s a person in every piece.

      The curve of a thumb. A pool of glaze in a throwing line. The slight lean of a rim. We keep the small marks that tell you something was made rather than manufactured, and we mix our five glazes ourselves, from scratch, in the back room.
