        Good to know

        Do I need any experience?+

        None at all for the wheel intro or the hand-building evening. The six-week course starts from the beginning too, but it suits people who want to keep going.

        What should I wear?+

        Something you don’t mind getting clay on, short sleeves or sleeves you can push up, and short nails if you can. We have aprons and towels.

        When can I take my pots home?+

        About four weeks later, once they’ve dried, been fired, glazed and fired again. We email when they’re ready to pick up, or ship them for $12.

        How old do you have to be?+

        16 and over, or 12 and over with an adult booked alongside. For a birthday or a group of friends, ask about a private session.

        Is the studio accessible?+

        The studio is at street level with a step-free entrance from the yard behind the shop. One of our wheels sits at table height for anyone who would rather not sit low. Tell us what would help when you book.
