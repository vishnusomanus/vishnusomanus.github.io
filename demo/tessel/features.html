Features

Everything a form needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Four big blocks, built to work together: logic decides what to ask, payments collect the money, integrations move the answers and analytics show you what to fix.

01 · Logic & branching

Rules you can read out loud.

Every rule is a sentence: if this answer is that, show this question. Change an answer in the form on the right and watch the rules switch on and off.

  • Show and hide questions based on any earlier answer, or on a hidden field from the link.
  • Jump to a page, an ending or a different thank-you message.
  • Calculate scores, prices and quiz results as people answer.
  • Group conditions with AND and OR, and get a warning when a rule can never fire.

02 · Payments

The checkout is the form.

Add a payment field and Tessel works out the total from the answers: ticket tiers, quantities, add-ons and tips. No second page, no lost carts.

  • Cards and digital wallets
  • Monthly giving and memberships
  • Coupons and early-bird prices
  • Receipts with your logo

No Tessel fee on paid plans. On Free, a 2.9% platform fee applies on top of your processor's.

03 · Integrations

Answers go where the work is.

Connect once, then forget about it. Every new response lands as a row, a contact, a card or a ping, with field mapping you can see.
