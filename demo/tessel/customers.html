How Bramblecourt Schools got 92% of permission slips back on time

Bramblecourt runs nine schools around Council Bluffs, Iowa. Every field trip used to mean a stack of paper slips, a lunch-money envelope and a teacher chasing signatures the morning of the trip.

Now parents get a text with a link. The form asks only what applies to their child, takes the bus fee with a card and lands in a sheet the front office shares with drivers.
