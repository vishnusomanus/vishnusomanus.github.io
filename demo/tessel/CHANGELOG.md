## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Initial release with 10 pages: Home, Features, Templates, Pricing, Integrations, Changelog, Customers, Talk to
  sales, Sign up & sign in, 404.
- Live hero form builder: add, remove, reorder and rename fields, toggle required, with a preview that validates as
  you type and is saved in the browser.
- Conditional-logic demo where rules switch on and off and the ticket total updates as answers change.
- Drop-off analytics card with a 7 and 30 day switch that flags the question losing the most people.
- Template gallery with category chips, search, shareable URLs, an empty state, a preview dialog and a saved
  shortlist.
- Pricing with a monthly / annual switch, a response-volume slider that reprices every plan and picks the best fit,
  a comparison table and FAQ.
- Searchable integrations directory with category filters and what-syncs details.
- Changelog with type filters and dates that stay current, plus a roadmap and newsletter form.
- Sales form with routing and a reference number, live sales hours in Central Time and the next weekly walkthrough.
- Sign-up and sign-in tabs with password strength, plan and template hand-off, and a password reset.
- Neo-brutalist design with Bowlby One and Ubuntu Sans, hand-drawn SVG art and a dark mode that keeps the thick
  borders.
- Full documentation in `DOCS.md`.
